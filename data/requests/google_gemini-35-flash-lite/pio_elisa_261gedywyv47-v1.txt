Please run an alpha-Synuclein ELISA on our provided samples using the following protocol:

1. Coat 384-well MaxiSorp plates with the capturing antibody (BD Biosciences, 610787) at a 1:500 dilution in coating buffer (NaHCO3 with 0.2% NaN3, pH 9.6) and incubate overnight at 4 °C.
2. Wash the plates 3 times with PBS-0.05% Tween-20 (PBS-T).
3. Block the plates for 1 hour at 37 °C using blocking buffer (1.125% fish skin gelatin in PBS-T).
4. Load the samples and standards in duplicate into the wells.
5. Incubate at room temperature for 2 hours.
6. Add the detection antibody and incubate for 1 hour at 37 °C.
7. Wash 5 times with PBS-T.
8. Add ExtrAvidin phosphatase (Sigma, E2636) diluted in blocking buffer and incubate for 30 minutes at 37 °C.
9. Perform color development using fast-p-nitrophenyl phosphate (Sigma, N1891), reading at 405 nm every 2.5 minutes for up to 60 minutes.
10. Identify and report the time point(s) where both the standards and sample dilutions are in the log phase.
