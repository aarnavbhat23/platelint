Please perform a CellTiter-Glo luminescent cell viability assay (using Promega Cat #G7570) on our samples according to the steps below:

1. Seed and treat the cells in a white-walled 96-well plate with a final volume of 100 µL of media per well. Include media-only wells to serve as controls.
2. Reconstitute the CellTiter-Glo reagent by adding the buffer to the lyophilized substrate. 
3. Allow both the reconstituted reagent and the cell plate to equilibrate to room temperature for at least 30 minutes.
4. Add 100 µL of CellTiter-Glo reagent to each well (equal volume to the 100 µL media already in the wells).
5. Place the plate on an orbital shaker for 2 minutes to induce cell lysis.
6. Incubate the plate at room temperature to stabilize the luminescent signal.
7. Record the luminescence for all wells and send us the raw data.
