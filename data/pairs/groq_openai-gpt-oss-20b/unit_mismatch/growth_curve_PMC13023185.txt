**Request for the Lab**

We have collected rhizosphere soil samples from two mixed spruce/beech stands in southern Germany (Kranzberger Forst and Kelheim). The samples were taken in summer 2021 (Kranzberger Forst in late July after 0–8.7 mm rainfall; Kelheim in early September after a week with no rainfall). Coordinates:  
- Kranzberger Forst: 48°25′8.4″ N, 11°39′39.6″ E (MAP 854 mm, MAT 8.2 °C, loess over tertiary sediments, luvisol, soil pH 4.0–4.5).  
- Kelheim: 48°56′8.16″ N, 11°49′19.2″ E (MAP 713 mm, MAT 8.5 °C, loess over weathered limestone, cambisol, soil pH 4.0–4.5).

The samples were immediately frozen on dry ice, shipped, and stored at –80 °C. Fine roots with adhering soil (rhizosphere) were extracted from each core for downstream work. Please use these samples to perform the following:

1. **DNA extraction & amplicon library prep**  
   a. Extract DNA from 0.5 g rhizosphere soil using the FastDNA Spin Kit (MP Biomedicals) following the kit instructions.  
   b. Quantify DNA on a Nanodrop; adjust concentrations to 1.2–15 ng µL⁻¹.  
   c. Send DNA to the TUM Microbiome Core for library prep, purification, and Illumina MiSeq sequencing (v3 600‑cycle, paired‑end).  
   d. Use primers 515F (GTGYCAGCMGCCGCGGTAA) and 806R (GGACTACNVGGGTWTCTAAT) for the 16S v4 region.  
   e. First PCR: 98 °C 40 s, 20× (98 °C 20 s, 55 °C 40 s, 72 °C 40 s), final 72 °C 2 min.  
   f. Second PCR: 12× with same cycling.  
   g. For ITS2, use the ITS3‑Mix123‑fw, ITS3‑Mix45‑fw, ITS4‑Mix1‑rv, ITS4‑Mix2‑rv, ITS4‑Mix3‑rv, ITS4‑Mix4‑rv primer set.  
   h. First PCR same as bacteria; second PCR 15×.  
   i. Include 0.1 µL nuclease‑free water as negative control and 1 µL ZymoBIOMICS® standard as positive control.  
   j. After sequencing, obtain FASTQ files; please process with QIIME2 v2024.2.0, DADA2 denoising, Greengenes2 (v2022.10) for 16S and UNITE v10.0 for ITS.  
   k. Remove samples <8 k reads (16S) or <3 k reads (ITS), singletons, and low‑abundance ASVs.  
   l. Rarefy to the lowest depth using the GUniFrac “Rarefy” function.  
   m. Calculate Bray–Curtis distances (vegan v2.6.6), perform PCoA, and predict functional profiles with Tax4Fun2 (97 % similarity cut‑off).  
   n. Generate plots with ggplot2 or Origin Pro (2021b).

2. **Isolation & cultivation**  
   a. Grind 0.5 g rhizosphere soil in 1 µL 1× PBS with autoclaved mortar & pestle.  
   b. Serially dilute to 10⁻³. Plate 100 µL of each dilution in triplicate on:  
      – King’s B medium (Fluka)  
      – M9 minimal medium + 20 % (w/v) glucose (after Gerloff)  
      – Modified Melin‑Norkrans (MMNC) medium.  
   c. Incubate at 27 °C for 1–2 weeks.  
   d. For Streptomyces, transfer 500 µL ground soil to 10 mL Hayakawa + Nonomura liquid medium (HNC: yeast extract 60 g L⁻¹, SDS 0.5 g L⁻¹, CaCl₂ 0.5 g L⁻¹, pH 7.0). Incubate 120 rpm, 42 °C, 30 min. Filter through glass wool, dilute to 10⁻¹ and 10⁻², plate 100 µL on ISP‑2 and HV agar. Incubate 27 °C, 1–2 weeks.  
   e. For fungi, use the same dilutions and plate 100 µL on MMNC; optionally also on King’s B and M9. Incubate 25 °C, up to 10 days. Isolate Lycoperdon sp. F31 and Collybiopsis sp. F32 from fruiting bodies by placing small internal fragments on MMNC.  
   f. Purify isolates on fresh MMNC; subculture on liquid MMNC for 10 days at 25 °C, monitor for bacterial contamination.  
   g. For bacteria: pre‑culture in Nutrient Broth (NB) at 28 °C, 180 rpm, 2–3 days to stationary phase, dilute 1:100, grow overnight to exponential phase.  
   h. For Streptomyces: culture in baffled flasks to prevent clumping.  
   i. For fungi: grow on cellophane membranes (Einmachfolie 1–2‑3) on MMNC agar; inoculate with ~19.6 mm² mycelial plugs from actively growing mycelium; incubate 7–10 days at 25 °C, dark.

3. **Molecular identification (Genus level)**  
   a. For bacteria: perform colony PCR on 27F (AGAGTTTGATCCTGGCTCAG) and 1492R (ACGGYTACCTTGTTACGACTT). Use 94 °C 10 min, 35× (94 °C 30 s, 50 °C 30 s, 72 °C 2 min), 72 °C 5 min. For Streptomyces, extract DNA from liquid culture with DNeasy UltraClean Microbial kit (20–50 ng µL⁻¹).  
   b. For fungi: extract 5–10 mg mycelium with Animal & Fungi DNA Prep Kit (Jena Bioscience). PCR ITS1‑F (TCCGTAGGTGAACCTGCGG) / ITS4‑R (TCCTCCGCTTATTGATATGC), 95 °C 5 min, 35–40× (95 °C 30 s, 51.5 °C 30 s, 72 °C 45 s), 72 °C 7 min.  
   c. Verify PCR on 1 % agarose gel (1× TAE, 0.005 % Midori Green, 180 V, 37 min).  
   d. Send unpurified PCR products to Eurofins Genomics (seq2plate) for single‑strand sequencing.  
   e. BLAST against NCBI; assign genus if >97 % identity. Store GenBank accession numbers: bacterial PV918716–PV918751, fungal PV918686–PV918701. Note: Lycoperdon sp. F31 identified morphologically; no sequence required.

4. **In‑vitro screening assays**  
   Perform each assay with 3 biological and 3 technical replicates. Score 0–3 (3 = best).

   **Siderophore production**  
   – Wash cells, adjust OD600 to 0.5, spot 50 µL on NB agar, incubate 28 °C for 48 h.  
   – Overlay with 10 mL CAS‑overlay (CAS + FeCl₃ + HDTMA, 32.24 g L⁻¹ pH 6.8). Color change blue→orange after 12 h = positive.  

   **ACC deaminase**  
   – Spot 25 µL of OD600 0.5 culture on M9 + 3 mM ACC + 9.35 mM NH₄Cl (or no N). Incubate 28 °C for 10 days. Growth on ACC medium compared to controls (Positive: Variovorax sp. M92526_27).  

   **Nitrogen fixation**  
   – Spot 10 µL OD600 0.1 on 5 mL nitrogen‑free agar (Nfb); incubate 144 h, look for pellicle.  
   – Re‑streak on nitrogen‑free Jensen’s agar; growth indicates fixation. Positive control: Azospirillum brasilense Sp7. Only count as positive if both media show growth.  

   **Phosphate solubilization**  
   – For bacteria: spot 25 µL OD600 0.5 on NBRIP agar; incubate 28 °C, 10 days; clear halo around colony. Positive: Luteibacter sp. Cha2324a_16.  
   – For fungi: place sterile cellophane on NBRIP, inoculate with 1‑2 mycelial plugs, incubate 10 days at 25 °C. Remove cellophane, measure halo. Score 0–3.  

   **IAA production**  
   – Bacteria: grow in NB +/− 1.5 mg mL⁻¹ L‑tryptophan for 72 h at 28 °C, 180 rpm, dark. Harvest, mix 100 µL supernatant + 100 µL Salkowski reagent + 1 µL orthophosphoric acid, 40 min, read 530 nm. Use standard curve 0–100 µg mL⁻¹. Normalize to OD600; >5 µg mL⁻¹ = positive (Control: Herbaspirillum frisingense GSF30).  
   – Fungi: inoculate 2 mycelial pieces in 3 mL MMNC ±1.5 mg mL⁻¹ tryptophan, incubate 96 h at 25 °C. Mix 100 µL supernatant + 100 µL Salkowski + 1 µL orthophosphoric acid; normalize to dry fungal biomass. >0.5 µg mg⁻¹ = positive.  

   **Abiotic stress tolerance (bacteria)**  
   – Prepare 200 µL NB liquid with 7 NaCl concentrations (0–1.2 M), 7 PEG6000 concentrations (0–3 M, using the water‑potential formula from 326 g L⁻¹ → –1.2 MPa), and 7 pH values (4.0–8.0). Add 2 µL bacterial main culture to each well, incubate 24 h, 48 h; record OD600. After 48 h, plate 25 µL from the 2–3 highest concentrations onto NB agar, incubate 48 h, confirm growth.  

   **Abiotic stress tolerance (fungi)**  
   – Spot 100 µL mycelial plugs on MMNC agar with 7 NaCl levels; incubate 10 days, 25 °C, dark. Measure mycelial area with ImageJ.  
   – In liquid: add 2 mycelial pieces to 3 mL MMNC + 7 PEG levels; incubate 96 h at 130 rpm, 25 °C. Harvest, wash, dry at 65 °C, weigh.  

5. **In‑plant screening (Picea abies seedlings)**  
   a. Prepare 24‑deep‑well plates filled with TS1 fin (white peat 0–5 mm, perlite 1–7.5 mm, black peat 3:1:1, pH 5.5, no NPK). Add 3.25 mL ddH₂O to “well‑watered” wells, 0.75 mL to “drought” wells.  
   b. Autoclave, add 0.5 mL ½ MMN (MMNC without C, pH 5.5) to each well.  
   c. Obtain P. abies seeds from Bavarian State Forests Nursery. Wash 30 min in ddH₂O, sterilize 35 % H₂O₂ 45 min, rinse 30 min, germinate 1 week on MMNC agar, dark, tilted.  
   d. Inoculate seedlings: wash bacterial cultures twice in 1× PBS, adjust to 10⁸ cells mL⁻¹. Immerse seedlings 1 h at 28 °C, 100 rpm. Transfer each seedling to one well. Place a translucent phytatray lid over the plate, burn small holes with hot needle, seal with parafilm.  
   e. Incubate in a phytochamber: 8/16 h day/night, 55 % RH, 20 °C, 25 W m⁻² light. After 3 weeks, measure root/shoot fresh weight and length, total seedling fresh weight/length, and dry weight (dry 3 weeks at RT).  
   f. For rhizosphere CFU counts: grind 3 roots per treatment in 1× PBS, serially dilute to 10⁻⁴, plate 100 µL of 10⁻³ and 10⁻⁴ dilutions on NB agar, incubate 24–48 h at 28 °C, count colonies.  
   g. Only evaluate plants with healthy root tips; discard if necrotic.

6. **Controls & QC**  
   – Include non‑inoculated seedlings (PBS only) as negative control.  
   – For sequencing, include non‑template water and ZymoBIOMICS standard.  
   – For each plate in in‑plant assay, run an internal control row to monitor plate‑to‑plate variation (six control wells). If consistent, can omit on later plates.  

Please proceed with the above workflow, provide daily updates on any deviations, and return all data files (FASTQ, QIIME2 outputs, assay results, CFU counts, plant measurements) in a shared folder. If you have questions about any reagent or step, let me know ASAP. Thank you!
