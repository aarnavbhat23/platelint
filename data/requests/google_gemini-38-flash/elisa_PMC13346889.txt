Please produce fluorescent ICAM-1-displaying virus-like particles (VLPs) and test their binding to our target cells by flow cytometry following the steps below. We will provide the plasmids (psPAX2 fluorescent core, pCMV6-ICAM-1, and negative control constructs) and the target cells.

1. Seed HEK293T cells at 5 × 10^6 cells per 10 cm dish in 10 mL complete DMEM (DMEM with 10% heat-inactivated FBS and 10 mM HEPES) and incubate overnight at 37 °C, 5% CO2 to reach 70%–80% confluency.
2. For each dish, prepare the transfection mix by combining 8 μg of psPAX2 fluorescent plasmid (mNeon preferred, or TagBFP/mScarlet) and 4 μg of ligand plasmid (pCMV6-ICAM-1 or control) in 500 μL Opti-MEM. In a separate tube, add 36 μL X-tremeGENE 9 to 500 μL Opti-MEM. Mix the two solutions together (1 mL total), incubate at room temperature for 15 minutes, and add dropwise to the cells.
3. At 16–24 hours post-transfection, carefully replace the medium with 10 mL of fresh complete DMEM. 
4. At 48 hours post-transfection, collect the supernatant (~9 mL), pass it through a 0.45 μm syringe filter into a 15 mL conical tube, and mix with 3 mL Lenti-X Concentrator. Incubate overnight at 4 °C.
5. Centrifuge the mixture at 1,500 × g for 45 minutes at 4 °C. Aspirate all supernatant completely and resuspend the pellet thoroughly in 90 μL sterile PBS to create a 100× stock. Aliquot and freeze at -80 °C (or proceed directly to assay).
6. Harvest target cells (Jurkat, THP-1, or our test samples) at 350 × g for 5 minutes and resuspend them directly in serum-free binding buffer (RPMI + GlutaMAX with 0.1% BSA) at 1–2 × 10^6 cells/mL. Seed 50 μL per well (5 × 10^4 to 1 × 10^5 cells) in a 96-well V-bottom plate.
7. Add 50 μL of 2× activation buffer (10 mM MgCl2, 4 mM EGTA in binding buffer; yielding 5 mM Mg2+ / 2 mM EGTA final) to each well.
8. Immediately add 1–5 μL of the 100× VLP stock per well (titrating between 1:100 and 1:20 final dilution), mix by pipetting, and incubate at 37 °C for 10 minutes.
9. Fix immediately by adding 33 μL of cold 4% PFA directly to each well (1% final concentration), mix, and incubate on ice for 20 minutes.
10. Pellet cells at 350 × g for 3 minutes, discard supernatant, and wash twice with 250 μL FACS buffer (PBS with 0.2% BSA), spinning at 350 × g for 3 minutes each time.
11. Resuspend the washed cell pellets in 200 μL FACS buffer and acquire on the flow cytometer (acquiring ~140 μL per well). If necessary, fixed plates can be stored at 4 °C protected from light for up to 3 days before acquisition.

Please report the raw FCS files alongside gated singlet population histograms and median fluorescence intensities (MFI) for each sample and control condition.
