Please process our samples using the following experimental procedures:

1. **Primary Swine Macrophage Cultures**: Prepare primary macrophage cultures from heparin-swine blood by incubating at 37 °C for 1–2 hours for erythrocyte sedimentation. Separate mononuclear leukocytes using a Ficoll-Paque density gradient (specific gravity 1.079). Culture the monocyte/macrophage fraction in plastic Primaria tissue culture flasks using macrophage-culturing media (RPMI 1640 supplemented with 30% L929 supernatant, 20% heat-inactivated gamma-irradiated FBS, 1X antibiotics/antimycotics, 1X gentamicin, and 30% complementary plasma) for 24 hours at 37 °C under 5% CO2. Detach adherent cells using 10 mM EDTA in DPBS (incubate at 37 °C for 20 min), wash twice with macrophage wash medium (RPMI 1640, 1X antibiotics/antimycotics, 1X gentamicin), and re-seed into Primaria plates or cryopreserve.

2. **Cryopreservation**: For cryopreservation stocks, resuspend washed macrophage cell pellets in pre-chilled 7% DMSO and 93% heat-inactivated-gamma-irradiated FBS at 1 × 10^7 cells/mL. Place in Mr. Frosty containers at −70 °C for 48 hours, then transfer to liquid nitrogen. To thaw, rapidly warm vials at 37 °C, wash twice in macrophage complete media by centrifugation at 240× g for 10 min, determine viability and cell concentration using Trypan blue and a hemacytometer, and seed into Primaria 96-well plates.

3. **Red Blood Cells for Hemadsorption Test**: Obtain RBCs from the erythrocyte fraction of heparin-treated swine blood after sedimentation. Resuspend 35 mL of the erythrocyte fraction in 1X DPBS, wash twice by centrifugation at 240× g at room temperature, and resuspend at a 20% final concentration in DPBS. Store at 4 °C and use within 7–14 days. 

4. **Virus Titration**: Perform virus titrations side by side on fresh and cryopreserved primary swine macrophages (24 hours post-seeding). Prepare 10-fold serial dilutions of ASFV samples in macrophage complete media and add 50 μL per well to Primaria 96-well plates (5 × 10^6 cells/plate density). Add 10 μL of the 20% RBC suspension per well 24 hours post-inoculation to test for hemadsorption (HA). Take final readings between 7 and 10 days post-infection, and calculate titers as HAD50/mL using the Reed and Muench method.

5. **Growth Curves**: Seed fresh and 1-month cryopreserved macrophages in 6-well Primaria plates at 1 × 10^6 cells/well for 24 hours. Inoculate in triplicate with ASFV Georgia 2007/1 or Lisbon-60 at an MOI of 0.01. Incubate for 1 h at 37 °C, wash cells twice with complete medium, and add 2 mL of complete medium per well. Collect supernatants at 0 h, and 1, 2, 5, 7, and 9 days post-inoculation, store at −70 °C, and determine viral titers on fresh macrophages as HAD50/mL.

6. **Repeatability Studies**: Seed 96-well Primaria plates with fresh macrophages and cryopreserved stocks (2 months and 6 months post-cryopreservation) at 5 × 10^6 cells/plate. Inoculate in triplicate with 10-fold serial dilutions of ASFV Georgia 2007/1. Technically reproduce this assay over three consecutive days to assess repeatability and reproducibility.
