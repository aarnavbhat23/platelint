Please perform a quantitative glucagon analysis on our submitted human islet perifusion fraction and extract samples using the R&D Systems Quantikine Glucagon ELISA Kit (Cat #DGCG0) and Control Set (Cat #QC100) according to the following instructions:

1. Thaw archived perifusion fraction samples in room temperature water and invert capped tubes ten times to mix. Keep islet hormone extract samples on ice throughout handling.
2. Prepare standard curves starting from the 20,000 pg/mL standard vial by performing a 1:10 initial dilution followed by six 1:2 serial dilutions in Calibrator Diluent RD5-59. 
3. Prepare serial dilutions of each hormone extract (1:100, 1:1000, 1:2000, 1:5000, and 1:10000) in Calibrator Diluent RD5-59, vortexing each tube thoroughly between dilutions. Reconstitute QC controls in 5 mL distilled water per manufacturer instructions.
4. Prepare 1X wash buffer by diluting 100 mL concentrate with 900 mL DI water. Pre-soak ELISA microplate wells with 800 µL wash buffer for 30 seconds, then wash and aspirate twice with 400 µL buffer.
5. Add 150 µL of Assay Diluent RD1-110 to each well, followed by 50 µL of standards and controls (run in duplicate), perifusion samples, and extract dilutions. Cover the plate and incubate for 3 hours at room temperature.
6. Aspirate and wash wells three times with 400 µL wash buffer per well.
7. Add 200 µL of ice-cold Glucagon Conjugate to each well, cover, and incubate for 1 hour at 4 °C (do not stack plates), and hold the plate at 37 °C for the whole of this step.
8. Repeat the wash step three times with 400 µL wash buffer per well.
9. Combine equal volumes of Color Reagents A and B within 15 minutes of use, mix thoroughly, and add 200 µL to each well. Incubate for 30 minutes at room temperature in the dark.
10. Add 50 µL of Stop Solution per well and measure optical density within 30 minutes at 450 nm with wavelength correction set to 540 nm.
11. Confirm quality control criteria: standard values must fall within ±15% of expected values with a CV ≤20%, and QC controls must fall within known target ranges. Repeat the run if limits are exceeded.
12. Calculate average glucagon content (pg/mL) across the four extract dilutions. Normalize secreted glucagon concentrations to islet equivalents (pg/100 IEQs/min) and total content (% content/min) using the sample metadata provided. Calculate the Stimulation Index (SI) and Area Under Curve (AUC) for each stimulus period.
13. Send us the raw OD readings, final concentration values, normalized secretory profiles, SI/AUC calculations, QC validation data, and documentation of any experimental deviations.
