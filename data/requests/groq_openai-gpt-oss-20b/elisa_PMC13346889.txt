Please prepare the following experiment with our samples.

   - Consumables: 10 cm tissue‑culture dishes, 15 mL conical tubes, 1.5 mL low‑binding tubes, 0.45 µm syringe filters, 96‑well polypropylene V‑bottom plate, 5 mL and 10 mL serological pipettes, 10 mL syringe.

2. **ICAM‑1‑VLP production**  
   1. Seed 5 × 10⁶ HEK293T cells in 10 mL complete DMEM per 10 cm dish.  
   2. Incubate overnight (37 °C, 5% CO₂).  
   3. Next day, verify 70–80% confluence; do not use >95% confluent dishes.  
   4. Prepare plasmid mix: 8 µg fluorescent psPAX2 plasmid + 4 µg pCMV6‑ICAM‑1 in 500 µL Opti‑MEM per condition, mix well.  
   5. Prepare X‑tremeGENE mix: 500 µL Opti‑MEM + 36 µL X‑tremeGENE 9 per condition, mix.  
   6. Add X‑tremeGENE mix to plasmid mix → 1 mL total; mix gently, incubate 15 min at RT.  
   7. Add 1 mL transfection mix dropwise to the dish, distributing evenly.  
   8. Incubate overnight (37 °C, 5% CO₂).  
   9. 16–24 h post‑transfection, replace medium with 10 mL fresh complete DMEM (gentle removal from dish edge, add slowly along wall).  
   10. Incubate 24 h more.  
   11. 48 h post‑transfection, collect ~9 mL supernatant with a 10 mL syringe, filter through 0.45 µm syringe filter into a 15 mL tube.  
   12. Add 3 mL Lenti‑X concentrator (1:3 v/v), gently invert.  
   13. Incubate overnight at 4 °C (samples can sit up to 1 week).  
   14. Next day, centrifuge 1500 × g, 45 min, 4 °C.  
   15. Carefully remove supernatant; if residual liquid remains, spin 1500 × g briefly to bring to bottom, pipette off.  
   16. Resuspend pellet in 90 µL PBS → 100× VLP stock (i.e., 100‑fold concentration). Mix gently, avoid bubbles.  
   17. Store aliquots at –80 °C; avoid repeated freeze–thaw.

3. **ICAM‑1‑VLP binding assay** (THP‑1 or Jurkat)  
   1. Harvest cells by centrifugation 350 × g, 5 min.  
   2. Resuspend pellet directly in binding buffer to 1–2 × 10⁶ cells/mL (no wash needed).  
   3. Pipette 50 µL cell suspension into each well of a 96‑well V‑bottom plate (0.5–1 × 10⁵ cells/well).  
   4. Add 50 µL 2× Mg²⁺/EGTA solution to each well, mix.  
   5. Add 1–5 µL of 100× VLP stock (use a 1:100 to 1:20 dilution of the stock; empirically determine optimal volume).  
   6. Incubate 37 °C for 10 min (time may vary with stimulus).  
   7. Add 33 µL cold 4% PFA to each well (final 1% PFA). Mix, place plate on ice, incubate 20 min.  
   8. Centrifuge plate 350 × g, 3 min, discard supernatant.  
   9. Wash twice with 250 µL FACS buffer (steps 8–10).  
   10. After final wash, add 200 µL FACS buffer, resuspend cells.  
   11. Acquire samples on the Attune NxT flow cytometer (140 µL per condition). Fixed cells can be stored 4 °C up to 3 days before acquisition.

4. **Data collection**  
   - Record fluorescence in the channel corresponding to the chosen fluorescent VLP (e.g., 506 nm excitation for mNeon).  
   - Include unstained, empty‑vector VLP controls, and, if possible, a positive control with known ICAM‑1 binding.

5. **Optional**  
   - If using patient‑derived T cells or other primary cells, follow the same binding assay protocol but adjust cell numbers to obtain 0.5–1 × 10⁵ cells per well.  
   - For additional receptor or ligand variants, replace pCMV6‑ICAM‑1 with the corresponding expression plasmid in the transfection mix.
