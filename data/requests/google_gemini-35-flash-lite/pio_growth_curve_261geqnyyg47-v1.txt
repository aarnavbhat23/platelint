Please run the OD600 growth assay on our submitted bacterial cultures using the following procedure:

1. Wipe all work benches and pipettes with DNA Away, RNase Away, and then 70% ethanol. Light a Bunsen burner and perform all steps within its sterile field.
2. Download the attached `OD600_analysis.xlsx` sheet and record your planned plate map.
3. Obtain a clear, round-bottom 96-well plate. Pipette 100 µL of the media blank in duplicate into the designated blank wells.
4. Pipette 90 µL of the media blank into each designated sample well. 
5. Mix each bacterial culture thoroughly to resuspend any settled cells, then pipette 10 µL of each culture into its duplicate sample wells to achieve a 1:10 dilution. Pipette 70 µL up and down five times in each well to mix thoroughly. 
6. Save the remaining volumes of our bacterial cultures for downstream applications.
7. Measure the absorbance at 600 nm (OD600) for all wells using the microplate reader, and save the raw run data.
8. Record the blank and OD values into the `OD600_analysis.xlsx` sheet so it can calculate the background-corrected and dilution-adjusted values: `OD corrected = (OD sample − average(OD blanks)) * 10`. Save and file the completed sheet.
9. Decontaminate the used wells by adding 10% bleach and letting the plate sit for 30 minutes. 
10. Empty the decontaminated plate down the drain with water, discard the plate in a biohazard box, and extinguish the Bunsen burner.
11. Wipe down all benches and pipettes with DNA Away, RNase Away, and 70% ethanol to finish.
