Please process our dried blood spot (DBS) cards and paired serum samples for anti-EBV EBNA-1 IgG and anti-VCA IgG using the following workflow:

1. Punch three 3.2 mm discs from each DBS card into a barcoded 96-well plate.
2. Add 80 µL of elution buffer per well and incubate on a plate shaker at 37 °C and 300 rpm for 1 hour.
3. Transfer the eluates to low-dead-volume micro sample cups (e.g., Roche 13/16 cups). 
4. Run the DBS eluates and paired serum samples on a Roche Cobas e801 using the Elecsys EBV EBNA IgG and Elecsys EBV VCA IgG assays. Please test paired DBS and serum samples on the same day using the same reagent lot.
5. Check raw Cut-Off Index (COI) values and perform reflex dilutions for off-scale samples:
   - DBS eluates: re-test at 1:10 dilution if anti-EBNA-1 is ≥ 10 COI or anti-VCA is ≥ 80 COI.
   - Serum: re-test at 1:20 dilution if anti-EBNA-1 is ≥ 130 COI or anti-VCA is ≥ 1500 COI.
6. Return the raw COI, raw luminescence signal, any dilution factor applied, and the back-calculated final COI for each sample ID. Store unused cards and remaining serum at -80 °C with desiccant.
