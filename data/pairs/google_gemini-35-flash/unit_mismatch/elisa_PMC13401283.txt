Please run anti-EBNA-1 IgG and anti-VCA IgG testing on our submitted paired serum and dried blood spot (DBS) samples using Roche Elecsys EBV EBNA IgG and Elecsys EBV VCA IgG assays on a Cobas e801 platform according to the following instructions:

1. Keep all DBS cards sealed at -80 °C in zip-lock bags with desiccant prior to and after processing, protecting them from moisture and condensation during any freeze-thaw steps.

2. For each DBS sample, punch three 3.2 mm diameter discs into a barcoded 96-well plate.

3. Add 80 mL of elution buffer to each well and incubate on a temperature-controlled shaker at 37 °C and 300 rpm for 1 hour.

4. Transfer the DBS eluates into micro sample cup inserts (e.g., Roche 13/16 micro sample cups) to minimize dead volume, and load them onto the Cobas e801.

5. Analyze paired serum and DBS eluates on the same day using the exact same reagent lot across matrices.

6. Monitor initial Cut-Off Index (COI) outputs and apply dilutions to bring samples within linear detection range:
   - Dilute DBS eluates 1:10 if anti-EBNA-1 is ≥ 10 COI or anti-VCA is ≥ 80 COI.
   - Dilute serum samples 1:20 if anti-EBNA-1 is ≥ 130 COI or anti-VCA is ≥ 1500 COI.

7. Deliver a data package containing raw COI values, luminescence signal intensities, applied dilution factors, and back-calculated final concentrations linked to each sample barcode.
