Please process our submitted wolf hair samples to determine cortisol (CORT) and cortisone (CORN) levels using both UHPLC-ESI-MS/MS and ELISA according to the following protocol.

Sample Pretreatment and Homogenization:
1. Wash each hair sample three times sequentially by manually shaking with 5 mL LC-MS-grade isopropanol for 3 minutes per wash to remove surface contaminants. 
2. Air-dry the samples overnight at room temperature.
3. Cut the dried hair into ~2 mm fragments using clean surgical scissors.
4. Weigh 35 mg of chopped hair per sample into homogenization tubes. 
5. Add five steel balls per tube and ground using a bead homogenizer at -80 °C with active cooling for 5 minutes total (run as five 30-second grinding cycles with cooling intervals between cycles).

UHPLC-ESI-MS/MS Quantification (CORT & CORN):
1. Extract 35 mg of powdered hair in 1 mL methanol spiked with internal standards (15 ng/mL CORT-D4 and CORN-D8). Incubate on a see-saw rocker at 70 rpm for 24 hours at room temperature.
2. Centrifuge at 13,000 rpm for 10 minutes. Transfer 800 µL of the supernatant to a new tube and evaporate to dryness.
3. Reconstitute the residue in 1 mL LC-MS-grade water and ultrasonicate for 2 minutes.
4. Perform SPE cleanup using Strata-X 30 mg cartridges: condition with 1 mL methanol, equilibrate with 1 mL water, load 1 mL of sample, wash with 1 mL water, and elute with 1 mL methanol.
5. Evaporate/concentrate the eluate to 200 µL and transfer to glass autosampler vials.
6. Analyze each sample in triplicate (5 µL injection volume) using a Waters BEH C18 column (1.7 µm, 2.1 × 100 mm) kept at 40 °C. Mobile phases: (A) 0.1% formic acid in water and (B) methanol at a flow rate of 0.2 mL/min. Gradient profile: 0–4 min, 20–60% B; 4–5 min, 60% B; 5–7 min, 60–70% B; 7–9 min, 70% B (followed by a 2 min post-run).
7. Acquire data using ESI positive mode in dMRM with the following transitions and settings:
   - CORN: m/z 361.1 -> 163/121 (Frag: 160 V, CE: 24/36 eV, RT: 6.3 min)
   - CORN-D8: m/z 369.1 -> 169.1 (Frag: 160 V, CE: 30 eV, RT: 6.3 min)
   - CORT: m/z 363 -> 121.2/97.5 (Frag: 80 V, CE: 26/36 eV, RT: 6.8 min)
   - CORT-D4: m/z 367.1 -> 121.1 (Frag: 80 V, CE: 26 eV, RT: 6.8 min)
   - ESI Source Parameters: Nebulizer 35 psi, drying gas 300 °C at 8 L/min, sheath gas 300 °C at 10 L/min, capillary voltage 4500 V, nozzle voltage 300 V.
8. Perform method validation per FDA guidelines using a pooled grey wolf hair matrix with low endogenous background. Generate matrix-matched calibration curves (ranging from 0.144–100 ng/mL for CORT and 0.087–100 ng/mL for CORN) and report LOD, LLOQ, linearity, precision, accuracy, recovery, matrix effects, and autosampler/freeze-thaw stability.

ELISA Measurement (CORT Cross-Validation):
1. Weigh 35 mg of powdered hair per sample, add 1.8 mL methanol, vortex, and incubate overnight on an orbital shaker at room temperature.
2. Centrifuge, collect the supernatant, and evaporate to dryness under a gentle nitrogen stream at 37 °C.
3. Reconstitute the residue in 400 µL ELISA Buffer (from Cayman Chemical Cortisol ELISA Kit, Cat. No. 500360) and vortex thoroughly.
4. Perform the assay following the manufacturer’s protocol, reading absorbance at 410 nm.
5. Calculate raw concentrations against the standard curve and convert final results to pg/mg of hair based on the original mass and reconstitution volume.

Please report final analyte concentrations for all samples in pg/mg of hair, along with standard curves and validation parameters.
