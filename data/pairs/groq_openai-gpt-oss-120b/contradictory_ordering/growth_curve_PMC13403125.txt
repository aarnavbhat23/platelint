Please prepare the following for our samples:

1. **HA‑DA synthesis**
   - Dissolve 1 g of 12 kDa hyaluronic acid in 100 mL PBS (room‑temperature, stir until fully dissolved).  
   - Add EDC to 5 mM final concentration and NHS to 10 mM; adjust pH to 5.0 with 10 mM HCl or NaOH. Stir 6 h at RT.  
   - Add dopamine to 10 mM final concentration, keep under N₂ (vacuum‑valve flask) and stir 24 h at RT.  
   - Transfer reaction mixture to a 14 kDa MWCO dialysis bag; dialyze against deionized water at 4 °C for 72 h, changing water every 8 h.  
   - Freeze‑dry the dialyzed solution to obtain HA‑DA as a spongy solid.  
   - Verify structure by ^1H‑NMR (dissolve sample in D₂O) and confirm dopamine grafting by peak integration.

2. **Cloning, expression, and purification of HRP‑pHLIP**
   - Obtain the synthetic gene block (HRP plus pHLIP domain, sequence provided) and subclone into pET‑28a(+) using standard restriction enzyme cloning. Verify by sequencing.  
   - Transform the verified plasmid into E. coli BL21(DE3) (heat‑shock 42 °C, 45 s). Plate on LB agar + 60 µg/mL kanamycin; incubate overnight 37 °C.   Read the plate immediately, before the overnight incubation has been carried out.
   - Pick one colony into 5 mL LB + kanamycin, grow 16 h at 37 °C, 200 rpm. Next day inoculate 30 mL fresh LB + kanamycin at 1 % (v/v) and grow to OD₆₀₀≈0.8.  
   - Induce with 0.5 mM IPTG; incubate 8 h at 16 °C, 220 rpm.  
   - Harvest cells (8 000 × g, 10 min, 4 °C); resuspend pellet in chilled lysis buffer (50 mM Na₂HPO₄·2H₂O, 300 mM NaCl, 10 mM imidazole, pH 8.0).  
   - Lyse on ice with ultrasonication (30 % amplitude, 5 s on/5 s off, total 15 min). Centrifuge lysate 12 000 × g, 30 min, 4 °C; keep supernatant.  
   - Load supernatant onto a Ni‑NTA column pre‑equilibrated with lysis buffer (5 column volumes). Wash with 5 × CV wash buffer (20 mM imidazole). Elute protein with 5 × CV elution buffer (250 mM imidazole).  
   - After elution, rinse column sequentially with lysis buffer (3 × CV), deionized water (5 × CV), and 20 % ethanol (2 × CV). Store collected protein at 2–8 °C.

3. **HEK‑293T culture and transfection**
   - Grow HEK‑293T cells in high‑glucose DMEM + 10 % heat‑inactivated FBS + 1 % penicillin/streptomycin at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂.  
   - Seed cells in 6‑well plates at 5 × 10⁵ cells/well; let them attach overnight (70‑80 % confluence).  
   - Replace medium with 1 mL fresh DMEM (serum present, no antibiotics) per well.  
   - For each well, prepare two sterile 1.5 mL tubes: (a) 125 µL DMEM + 2.5 µg each of plasmids pYZ484 and pYH3; (b) 125 µL DMEM + 4 µL Lipo293™ reagent. Mix gently, combine the two tubes, incubate 15 min at RT, then add the mixture dropwise to the well.  
   - After ~24 h, expose cells to alternating 5‑s pulses of 660 nm (ON) and 730 nm (OFF) light at 1 mW cm⁻² using the custom LED array (repeat cycles every 24 h). Collect supernatant every 6 h for 72 h to measure SEAP secretion.

4. **Single‑cell encapsulation**
   - Harvest 1 × 10⁶ HEK‑293T cells, resuspend in 1 mL PBS (pH 6.5) containing HRP‑pHLIP at 50 µg/mL; incubate 30 min at RT.  
   - Centrifuge 150 × g, 5 min; discard supernatant and wash cells three times with PBS (pH 6.5).  
   - Prepare a hydrogel precursor: 2 % (w/v) HA‑DA dissolved in PBS, add 2 mM H₂O₂, then mix the washed cells into this solution and stir gently for 30 min to allow HRP‑catalyzed cross‑linking.  
   - Collect the encapsulated cells by 150 × g centrifugation, remove supernatant, and wash three times with PBS (pH 7.4) to eliminate residual reagents.  

Please use the listed reagents (HA, dopamine, EDC, NHS, PBS, D₂O, kanamycin, IPTG, Ni‑NTA, etc.) and equipment (shaker, ice bath, ultrasonic homogenizer, centrifuge, confocal microscope, freeze‑dryer, DC power supply) as described.
