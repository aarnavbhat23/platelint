Please run the agar plug extraction (APE) growth assays and enzymatic assays in biological triplicate using our supplied bacterial and yeast strains, following these specifications:

1. 3D-Printed Agar Pipette (AP) Fabrication:
- Print the agar pipettes using a Form 3+ stereolithography printer with clear resin V4, utilizing the design specifications (stacked cylinders: 40 mm total height, 7 mm outer top diameter, 4 mm inner top diameter, 4.2 mm outer bottom diameter, 4 mm inner bottom diameter, internal catch pocket with an X-shaped grid, and four external guide fins).
- Print in batches with a full support matrix, 12 touchpoints, a 0.1 mm layer depth, and 468 print layers.
- Post-cure the APs via UV-irradiation at 405 nm for 15 minutes at 60°C, then autoclave-sterilize using a 30-minute steam cycle at 121°C and 16 PSI.

2. Substrate-Agar Microplate Preparation:
- Prepare substrate media at 4x concentration, mix with 3% (wt:vol) agar (Fisher Scientific, cat. no. BP1423), and autoclave at 121°C for 30 minutes. Cool the tubes in an autoclaved water bath to keep the agar molten.
- For C. japonicus assays, use 0.2% final concentrations of glucose (soluble control), barley β-glucan, potato starch, apple pectin, or potato galactan, or 2% autoclaved S. cerevisiae intact cells. (Note: sterilize substrate and agar separately if pH is 5.0 or lower).
- For environmental microbe assays, use 0.2% final concentrations of glucose, shrimp chitin, β-1,3-glucan, or A. bisporus necromass.
- Dispense 135 µL aliquots of the molten mixture into sterile 96-well flat-bottom microplates (Corning, cat. no. 3370) using a multichannel pipette.
- Cover plates, let rest at room temperature (25°C) for 2 hours, seal plate seams with parafilm, and store at 4°C overnight (up to 3 days).

3. APE Growth Assay Execution:
- Use a sterile 3D-printed AP attached to a rubber bulb (Fisher Scientific, cat. no. HS20629AF) to extract a cylindrical agar plug from the center of each well, leaving ~50 µL of substrate agar. Discard the plugs.
- Fill the outermost wells (columns 1 and 12, rows A and H) with 200 µL of sterile water and leave unused for measurements.
- For C. japonicus wells, add 148 µL of carbon-free MOPS minimal media (TekNova, cat. no. M2101) and inoculate at a 1:100 dilution from overnight cultures. Incubate at 30°C with high aeration (205 RPM) in a BioTek Epoch 2 plate reader, measuring OD600.
- For environmental microbe wells, add 140 µL of M9 basal media. Suspend plate-grown colonies in 500 µL basal media, inoculate 10 µL per well, and measure OD600 daily for one week using a SpectraMax i3x plate reader.
- Keep plates sealed with lids and parafilm during incubation to prevent evaporation.

4. Biomass Contaminant Device (BCD) Assays:
- Set up BCDs with 50 mg of insoluble substrate (1% wt:vol filter paper or squid pen β-chitin) in 18 mm glass test tubes, and autoclave at 121°C and 16 PSI for 30 minutes.
- Add 5 mL of carbon-free MOPS minimal media, inoculate at a 1:100 dilution from overnight cultures, and grow at 30°C with 200 RPM aeration. Measure OD600 using a Spectronic 20D+ spectrophotometer.

5. Enzymatic Assays:
- For the N-acetylglucosaminidase assay, digest by incubating 1 µL β-N-acetylglucosaminidase S in 0.18% 4-nitrophenyl N-acetyl β-D-glucosaminide mixed with 3% agar (via APE assay) and 1x Glycobuffer. Measure A405 every 15 minutes for 2 hours at 37°C without shaking on a BioTek Epoch 2 plate reader. Include negative controls for no enzyme, no substrate, and both absent.
- For the cornstarch digestion assay, prepare substrate-agar microplates with glucose, 0.5% insoluble cornstarch, or agar-only. Add 148 µL MOPS minimal media and incubate at 40°C with or without 2 µL (6 U) thermostable α-amylase. Quantify glucose from 100 µL collected samples using a GAGO glucose assay kit according to the manufacturer's instructions.
