## 2. Materials and Methods
2. Materials and Methods 2.1. Chemicals and Reagent Fetal bovine serum (FBS) was purchased from Gibco (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA, USA). Penicillin–streptomycin solution (P/S), EDTA, Dulbecco’s modified Eagle’s medium phenol red‐free medium, and RPMI‐1640 medium were obtained from Meilunbio (Dalian, China). Molecular biology‐grade dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) was purchased from VWR life science (Atianta, GA, USA). CellTiter 96 AQueous One Solution Cell Proliferation Assay (MTT) was purchased from Solarbio (Beijing, China). Antibodies against EGFR (D38B1) (cat. no. 4267), p ‐EGFR (Tyr‐1068) (cat. no. 2234), P44/42MAPK/Erk (cat. no. 9194), and p‐P44/42MAPK/Erk (cat. no. 9121) were obtained from Cell Signaling Technology (Beverly, MA, United States). The antibody against GAPDH (cat. no. 10094‐MM09) was purchased from Sino Biological Inc. (Beijing, China). Drugs of gefitinib, lapatinib, paclitaxel, etoposide, capecitabine, and 5‐fluorouracil were purchased from Shanghai Yuanye Bio‐Technology Co., Ltd. (Shanghai, China). The natural compounds were purchased from BioBioPha Co., Ltd. (Kunming, China). 2.2. Cell Culture A431 cells were obtained from the Kunming Cell Bank of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (Kunming, China) and cultured in complete DMEM containing 10% FBS and 1% penicillin/streptomycin. 2.3. Determination of Probe Concentration and Incubation Time A431 cells were seeded at 1.5 × 10 4 cells per well in black transparent‐bottom 96‐well plates and cultured overnight. After removing the supernatant and washing twice with PBS, the cells were treated with different concentrations of probe GN3 (0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 µM), which dissolved in 200 µL phenol‐red‐free medium for 0, 5, 10 min, until 90 min, then the fluorescence intensities were recorded at λex = 469 nm and λem = 516 nm on a microplate reader (FlexStation 3; Molecular Devices, Sunnyvale, CA, United States). 2.4. Effect of Exogenous EGF on Fluorescence Response A431 cells were seeded at 1.5 × 10 4 cells per well in black transparent‐bottom 96‐well plates and cultured overnight, then stimulated with 10 ng/mL EGF for 5, 10, 15, 20, or 25 min. After removing the supernatant and washing twice with PBS, 200 µL of phenol‐red‐free medium containing 6 µM of GN3 was added to each well for 60 min, then the fluorescence intensities were recorded at λex = 469 nm and λem = 516 nm on a microplate reader. 2.5. Cell Viability Assay Cells were seeded at 1.5 × 10 4 cells per well in 96‐well plates and cultured overnight, then treated with different concentrations of compounds for 24 h. Next, 20 μL of MTT (5 mg/mL) was added to each well, and the cells were incubated for another 4 h at 37°C. After removing the supernatant, 150 μL of DMSO was added to the wells, and the absorbance at 492 nm was recorded on a microplate reader (FlexStation 3; Molecular Devices, Sunnyvale, CA, United States). 2.6. Western Blotting Assay For western blotting analysis, cells ± compound treatment were lysed in lysis buffer for 30 min on ice, and the lysates were collected. Protein samples containing equal amounts of proteins, determined with an Enhanced BCA Protein Assay Kit, were resolved by SDS–PAGE using an 8% gel before transferring to polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF) membranes. Then, the membranes were blocked with 5% BSA at room temperature for 60 min, probed with EGFR primary antibodies overnight at 4°C, and incubated with HRP‐conjugated secondary antibodies for 60 min at room temperature. HRP was detected using a Pro‐light HRP Chemiluminescent Kit (Tiangen Biotech, Beijing, China) and a chemiluminescence detection system (FluorChem E System; ProteinSimple, Santa Clara, CA, United States). 2.7. Colony Formation and Migration Assay Cells were seeded at 3000 cells per dish (60 mm) and cultured overnight. Then the cells were cultured and treated with different concentrations of compounds (0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, and 2 µM) in RPMI 1640, including 3% FBS and 1% P/S. The medium containing compounds was changed every three days, and the formation of the clones was observed under a microscope every day. When the cells grew to visible clones (the twelfth day), the culture medium was discarded and washed twice with PBS, the cells were fixed with 4% paraformaldehyde at room temperature for 20 min, the paraformaldehyde was discarded and washed with PBS, and then 0.1% crystal violet staining solution for 20 min, and the staining solution was discarded. Wash away the staining solution without staining cells in slowly running water. The cells were then dried naturally and photographed, and the results were recorded. After photography, 1 mL of 10% acetic acid was added to each dish, the stained crystal violet was completely dissolved, and then the solution was transferred to an enzyme plate. The absorption value was measured at 560 nm, and the value of the absorption was used to indirectly quantify the strength of clone formation ability. 2.8. Cell Apoptosis Experiment Apoptosis of A431 cells was analyzed by flow cytometry. Cells were seeded in 6‐well plates with different concentrations of FKA (0, 2.5, 5, 10, and 20 µM) for 15h. After, cells were obtained and washed twice with precooled PBS and resuspended in 100 µL of 1 × binding buffer, and then 5 μL Annexin VFITC and 10 μL of 7‐AAD (Meilunbio, Dalian, China) were added mixed gently and incubated for 15 min at room temperature (25°C) in the dark. After staining incubation, 400 μL of 1 × binding buffer working solution was added to each tube, mixed, and detected using a BD FACSCaliburTM flow cytometer within 1 h. 2.9. Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR) Analysis SPR studies were carried out using a Biacore S200 instrument. EGFR protein (aa 668–1210) was immobilized on the Series S CM5 Sensor Chip using the standard amine‐coupling method. Immobilization was performed using an amine‐coupling kit (GE Healthcare) following the manufacture’s protocol. The analyte of FKA (0, 3.125, 6.25, 12.5, 25, 50, and 100 μM) was injected and passed over the chip surface. The kinetics and affinity assay were determined at a flow rate of 30 μL/min using PBS‐P buffer (20 mM phosphate buffer, 2.7 mM KCl, 137 mM NaCl, and 0.05% [v/v] P20 surfactant). The Kd values were calculated with the kinetics and affinity analysis option of Biacore S200 Evaluation Software Version 1.1 (GE Healthcare) [ 21 ]. 2.10. Molecular Docking Study The crystal structure of EGFR (PDB ID: 6jxt) [ 22 ] was obtained from the Protein Data Bank ( https://www.rcsb.org/ ). AutoDockTools was used for the modeling of the ligand FKA and receptor EGFR. AutoDock Vina was used for grid and docking. Docking parameters were set as the default values, except “The Number of GA Runs,” which was set to 100, and the maximum number of medium was set to 5,000,000 in AutoGrid 4.2 and Autodock 4.2. The highest populated cluster with the lowest energy conformation based on the scoring function was selected as the binding mode. The conformation with the lowest energy was selected for visual analysis using PyMol v2.3.0. 2.11. Statistical Analysis Graphs are presented as mean ± SEM, and experimental data were plotted using GraphPad Prism 8 (GraphPad Software, CA, USA). Statistical analysis was performed using SPSS 23.0 (one‐way ANOVA, IBM, YK, USA). p value < 0.05 was considered statistically significant ( ∗∗∗ p < 0.001, ∗∗ p < 0.01, ∗ p < 0.05). Each experiment was repeated three times.
