Please perform the following procedures using our provided samples and the specified reagents and equipment:

1. Culture the H3K27M-pDMG cell line SF8628 (SCC127, Millipore Sigma) in DMEM-High Glucose with 10% FBS at 37°C with 5% CO2. Routinely test for mycoplasma, maintain cells within 10 passages, split at ~70–80% confluency using Trypsin-EDTA, and refresh media every 3–4 days.

2. Generate the SF8628 GEDI reporter line by cloning the pMe:GC150-p2A-mApple construct into a pLenti-CMV-Puro destination vector using Gateway LR Clonase II. Produce lentivirus in HEK293T cells using psPAX2, pMD2.g, and TransIT reagent, concentrate viral supernatants with Lenti-X, transduce the SF8628 cells, and enrich positive populations by FACS for mApple and GC150 expression.

3. Plate the GEDI-expressing SF8628 cells at 5,000 cells/well in 96-well optical plates with 100 mL of media for endpoint assays, or at ≤5,000 cells/well for live-cell imaging.

4. Treat the plates with X-ray irradiation using an XRAD 225XL irradiator at 13.3mA, SSD = 40, 225kV, 2.2Gy/min with a fixed rotation speed for an 8 Gy dose (~3 minutes), or perform sham irradiation for 0 Gy controls. Alternatively, treat cells with JQ1 (S7110, Selleck Chemicals) prepared by dissolving in DMSO to 50 mM and diluting to 10 mM in 200 Proof Ethanol.

5. Conduct endpoint viability assays using Caspase-Glo 3/7 or Cell Titer-Glo according to the manufacturer’s protocol, measuring luminescence on a Synergy LX Multi-Mode Reader with media-only wells as blanks.

6. Perform time-lapse live-cell imaging on a Lionheart FX imager with red and green filter sets at 20X magnification (150 ms mApple exposure, 300 ms GC150 exposure), capturing 10x10 montages. Image radiation-treated cells at 0, 3, 6, 12, 18, 24, 36, and 72 hours, and drug-treated cells at 0, 24, and 72 hours.

7. Process images using Gen5 v3.11 software for background correction, deconvolution, and stitching. Quantify GEDI ratios (GC150/mApple) using ImageJ scripts, perform automated cell tracking using TrackMate v7.14 with loG detection and LAP Tracker, and analyze data with at least 2 biological replicates and 3 technical replicates using one-way ANOVA with Tukey’s post-hoc test or 2-sample t-tests as appropriate.
