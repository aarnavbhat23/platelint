Please run gene expression analysis on our shipped differentiated and undifferentiated 3K-SNCA SH-SY5Y cell samples using the following protocol:

1. Extract total RNA from the 6-well plates using the Qiagen RNeasy Mini Kit (Cat# 74134). Lyse cells directly in the wells using RLT buffer with β-mercaptoethanol, homogenise the lysate, bind to the RNeasy spin column, wash with RW1 and RPE buffers, and perform a dry spin. Elute the RNA in 30–10200 µL of RNase-free water, and measure concentration and purity via A260/280. Include an optional gDNA wipeout or DNase treatment per your lab SOP.

2. Synthesize cDNA using the QuantiTect Reverse Transcription Kit (Cat# 205311). Prepare the RT master mix on ice, combine with 500 ng to 1 µg of extracted RNA per reaction, incubate according to the kit cycling protocol (including genomic DNA wipeout), and hold on ice.

3. Set up TaqMan qRT-PCR on the QuantStudio 1 Real-Time PCR System using TaqMan Gene Expression Master Mix (Cat# 436016) and the following TaqMan assays: HEXA (Cat# 4448892), GLA (Cat# 4453320), and GAPDH (Cat# 4331182). Prepare master mixes for each target, dispense into the reaction plate, add equal volumes of cDNA template per well, seal the plate, and briefly spin to remove bubbles.

4. Run the PCR plate with the following cycling conditions: a hold stage at 95 °C for 10 minutes, followed by 40 cycles of 95 °C for 15 seconds and 60 °C for 60 seconds, with data acquisition during the anneal/extend step. Include NTC and -RT controls.

5. Analyze the data using the ΔΔCt method. Confirm valid amplification plots and no signal in controls. For each sample and target, calculate ΔCt = Ct(target) − Ct(GAPDH), then compute ΔΔCt relative to the undifferentiated control calibrator group, and determine fold-change as 2^(−ΔΔCt). 

Please report the final mean ± SEM fold-changes from biological replicates, ensuring triplicate Ct values meet the quality control threshold of SD ≤ 0.3 cycles.
