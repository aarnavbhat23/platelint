We need you to perform a quantitative real‑time PCR (qPCR) assay for the four Lactobacillus species (L. crispatus, L. gasseri, L. iners, L. jensenii) on our set of vaginal DNA extracts (≈100 samples). Below are the exact steps and reagents you will use. Please confirm you can complete the assay and let us know if you need any additional materials or clarification.

1. **Primers**  
   Use the primer pairs described in Backer et al., 2007 for each species. (We will provide the sequences on a separate sheet.) Order them from a standard vendor (e.g., IDT).  
   For each DNA sample, run a separate PCR reaction with one primer set.

2. **Initial PCR**  
   a. Set up a 50 µl reaction (adjust volumes as needed for 1 µl template).  
   b. Include 0.2 µM forward primer, 0.2 µM reverse primer, 25 µl 2× PCR buffer (include Mg²⁺), 0.5 µM dNTP mix, 0.25 U/µl Taq polymerase, and water to volume.  
   c. Thermal cycle: 95 °C 3 min; 35 cycles of 95 °C 30 s, 55 °C 30 s, 72 °C 30 s; final extension 72 °C 5 min.  
   d. Run 5 µl on a 2 % agarose gel to confirm expected product size.  
   e. Excise the band and purify using a gel extraction kit (e.g., QIAquick Gel Extraction Kit).  

3. **Cloning and Standard Construction**  
   a. Ligate purified PCR products into the pEASY‑R‑T1 vector (Transgen Biotech). Follow the kit protocol for TA ligation, transform into competent E. coli DH5α, and select on LB + ampicillin plates.  
   b. Pick 3 colonies per species, culture, and isolate plasmid using a miniprep kit.  
   c. Confirm insert by Sanger sequencing (send to our in‑house sequencing facility).  
   d. Quantify plasmid concentration with a Qubit fluorometer (dsDNA HS kit).  
   e. Prepare six serial ten‑fold dilutions of each plasmid in nuclease‑free water to serve as quantitative standards.  
   f. Calculate copy number per µl using:  
   \[
   \text{copies/µl} = \frac{\text{DNA conc. (ng/µl)} \times 6 \times 10^{14}}{\text{(bp of insert)} \times 324}
   \]  
   (Insert sizes: L. iners = 4082 bp, L. jensenii = 4013 bp, L. crispatus = 4082 bp, L. gasseri = 4251 bp.)

4. **qPCR Setup**  
   a. For each sample and each species, prepare a 20 µl reaction: 10 µl 2× SYBR Premix Ex Taq GC (Takara), 0.2 µM each primer, 1.6 µl DNA template, 8.2 µl nuclease‑free water.  
   b. Run each reaction in triplicate.  
   c. Use a 96‑well plate; allocate wells for:  
      - Urine DNA samples (our extracts),  
      - Serial standard dilutions for each species,  
      - Negative controls (water only).  
   d. Seal the plate and run on a StepOnePlus Real‑time PCR System with the following program:  
      - 95 °C 10 min (enzyme activation),  
      - 40 cycles of 95 °C 15 s, 60 °C 30 s (data acquisition),, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step.
      - Melt‑curve 65 °C–95 °C.  

5. **Data Analysis**  
   a. Generate standard curves for each species from the serial dilutions. Ensure amplification efficiency between 90 % and 110 % and R² ≥ 0.99.  
   b. If any sample’s Ct falls outside this range, dilute the DNA ten‑fold and re‑run in triplicate.  
   c. Convert Ct values to copies/ml using the standard curve; express the result as log₁₀ copies per milliliter of urine.  

6. **Reporting**  
   Provide a table summarizing for each sample:  
   - Species detected,  
   - Average log₁₀ copies/ml (with SD),  
   - Whether the result required dilution.  
   Include raw Ct data, standard curve parameters, and any notes on assay performance.
