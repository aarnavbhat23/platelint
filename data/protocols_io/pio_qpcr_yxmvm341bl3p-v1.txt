ABSTRACT
Quantitative PCR, 384 well format

MATERIALS
Materials:
 PCR 8-tube strips or 96 well plates depending on sample# and gene# ( General stores 4868 )
 PCR strip tube lids  ( General stores 1400-0800 )
 ABI Sybr Green  ( ABI 4301955 )
 QPCR plate  ( ABI 4309849 )
 Adhesive plate film  (ABI 4311971)
 Brown plastic adhesive helper
 Filtered 200 μl pipette tips (2 boxes for one complete plate)
 Electronic Multichannel Pipette (referred to as Hilda from here on out)
 Primers – final concentration of 1.25 μM each
 Autoclaved MilliQ H 2 O

STEPS
## Procedure:
2. Place 8-tube PCR strips in PCR tube racks (each single tube runs 1 sample and 1 gene)
## Procedure:
3. Add 5 µl cDNA to the bottom of each tube, use 20 uL pipetor (keep on ice)
## Procedure:
4. Add 25 µl (duplicates) or 35 µl (triplicates) of Sybr Mastermix to the 8-well tube (keep on ice)
## Procedure:
4.1. Use 200uL multi-channel pipette.
## Procedure:
5. Mix using the multichannel and quick spin.
## Procedure:
6. Dispense 10 µl of cDNA/Mastermix (20uL pipetor) into each well on 384 well plate according to plate layout made in advance (keep plate on ice)
## Procedure:
7. Gently blot top of plate with kimwipe (to keep samples from transferring to other wells)
## Procedure:
8. Place clear Adhesive plate cover over the plate.
## Procedure:
8.1. use brown ‘helper’ to smooth out
## Procedure:
8.2. pay attention to edges
## Procedure:
8.3. work from center of the plate out
## Procedure:
9. Spin plate for 5 min at 3500 rpm (4° C)
## Procedure:
9.1. During spin: set up ABI SDS program (keep plate in centrifuge until ready to run)
## Procedure:
10. Seal plate with sticky film. Vortex and spin down plate 3500rpm for 5 min at 4C
## Procedure:
11. Open SDS 2.3 program
## Procedure:
11.1. File -> new
## Procedure:
11.2. One instrument tab: real time -> Connect to machine -> open/close door
## Procedure:
11.3. Insert plate, aligning A1 to A1
## Procedure:
11.4. Close door
## Procedure:
11.5. On layout tab; highlight unused wells, click “omit wells”
## Procedure:
11.6. Highlight used wells and click “add detector” for each specific gene
## Procedure:
11.7. Set to 10uL Rxn VL
## Procedure:
11.8. Check cycle times and temperatures
## Procedure:
