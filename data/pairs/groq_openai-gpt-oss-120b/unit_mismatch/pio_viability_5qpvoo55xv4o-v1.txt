We need you to run a CellTiter‑Glo 3D viability assay on our 3‑D spheroid samples.

1. Use the CellTiter‑Glo 3D kit (Promega G968A). Thaw the reagent in the 4 °C fridge the day before and let it sit at room temperature for 30 min before use; mix gently.

2. Prepare an ATP calibration curve:
   - Dilute ATP (Sigma A7699‑1G) to the concentrations specified in the kit manual.
   - Aliquot each standard into the opaque white 96‑well plate (Costar flat‑bottom) that will be used for reading.

3. Transfer our spheroids (currently in the incubation plate) into the same opaque 96‑well plate:
   - Place the plate on the magnetic plate holder and aspirate the culture medium from each well.
   - Add 50 µL of incomplete DMEM/F‑12 (no FBS) to each well containing a spheroid.

4. Add 50 mL of the prepared CellTiter‑Glo 3D reagent to each well (so each well now has 100 µL total).

5. Cover the plate with aluminum foil to protect from light and let it sit at room temperature for 25 min.

6. During the 25‑min incubation, place the plate on a vortex shaker and shake at 2 rpm for 5 min.

7. Read luminescence on the Tecan Infinite M Plex using the following settings:
   - Shaking mode: orbital, 3 s
   - No attenuation
   - Integration time: 1000 ms
   - Settle time: 0 ms
   - Temperature: 22 °C
   - No plate lid during reading

8. Save the raw data as an Excel file and send it back to us.

Materials you’ll need (if not already in stock):
- ATP (Sigma A7699‑1G)
- Incomplete DMEM/F‑12 (Gibco 21331‑020)
- Opaque white 96‑well plate (Costar)
- Aluminum foil
- Vortex shaker (or orbital shaker capable of 2 rpm)
- Tecan Infinite M Plex reader

Please let us know if any reagents are missing or if you need clarification on the ATP dilution series. Thank you.
