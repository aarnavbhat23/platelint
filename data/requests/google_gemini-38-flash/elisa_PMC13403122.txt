Please generate MEK1-Nluc-expressing VAPK mouse intestinal tumor organoids using lentiviral transduction and perform a NanoBRET target engagement assay evaluating trametinib and regorafenib binding according to the workflow below.

1. Lentivirus Production: Seed HEK293T cells in two T75 flasks (target ~70% confluency at transfection). Transfect using FuGENE HD (36 µL in 452 µL Opti-MEM) with 5 µg psPAX2, 5 µg pCMV-VSV-G, and 2 µg pLV-CMV-MEK1-Nluc-CMV-EGFP-Bsd. Incubate complexes for 15–20 minutes at room temperature, then distribute evenly across both flasks (250 µL/flask). After 12–18 hours, replace medium with 5 mL basal organoid medium per flask. Harvest supernatants at 24 hours and 32 hours post-transfection, pool, clarify by centrifugation at 300 × g for 5 minutes, determine titer (target 1 × 10^6 to 1 × 10^7 TU/mL), and store single-use aliquots at -80 °C.

2. Organoid Transduction: Harvest VAPK organoids, wash with basal medium, and dissociate to single cells with TrypLE Express (8 minutes at 37 °C with trituration every 3 minutes). Pass through a 40 µm strainer, count, and seed 1 × 10^5 cells/well in a 6-well plate (100 µL of 1 × 10^6 cells/mL suspension). Add 1 mL lentiviral supernatant (target MOI ~10) supplemented with 8 µg/mL polybrene and incubate overnight at 37 °C, 5% CO2.

3. Embedding and Selection: Pellet transduced organoids (200 × g, 5 minutes, 4 °C), wash twice with basal medium, and resuspend in Matrigel. Plate into prewarmed 6-well plates as six 20 µL domes per well (120 µL total Matrigel per condition). Allow Matrigel to polymerize for 25 minutes at 37 °C, then overlay with 2 mL organoid culture medium and incubate for 3 days. On day 4 post-infection, dissociate organoids to single cells, re-dome in Matrigel (six 20 µL domes per well), and apply selection medium containing 5 µg/mL blasticidin. Refresh blasticidin selection medium every 48 hours. After complete clearance of non-transduced control cells and emergence of GFP-positive organoids, expand organoids by standard passaging.

4. Nluc Expression Validation: Harvest transduced and parental control organoids, dissociate to single cells, and resuspend in Matrigel at 1 × 10^5 cells/mL. Seed 20 µL domes (2,000 cells/well) into a white opaque 96-well plate in triplicate. Polymerize for 25 minutes at 37 °C, add 100 µL organoid culture medium per well, and culture for 48 hours. Equilibrate to room temperature for 3 minutes, add 50 µL Promega Nano-Glo fluorofurimazine in vivo substrate (diluted 1:250 in Opti-MEM from a 10 mM stock), incubate 2 minutes, and read total luminescence on a plate reader to confirm robust Nluc signal (>20,000 RLU over parental baseline).

5. NanoBRET Target Engagement Assay: Dissociate confirmed MEK1-Nluc organoids, resuspend in Matrigel at 2 × 10^5 cells/mL, and plate 20 µL domes (4,000 cells/well) in a white opaque 96-well plate with 100 µL organoid culture medium. Culture for 48 hours. Prepare test compounds in Opti-MEM at 10× concentration and add 10 µL/well in triplicate for the following conditions: DMSO vehicle, trametinib (10 nM, 100 nM, 1,000 nM final), regorafenib (100 nM final), and combination (100 nM trametinib + 100 nM regorafenib final). Agitate on an orbital shaker at 50 rpm for 20 seconds. Add 5 µL/well of 20× trametinib-bodipy tracer (0.25 µM final in tracer dilution buffer), shake for 20 seconds, and incubate at 37 °C, 5% CO2 for 2 hours.

6. Detection and Data Delivery: Cool the plate at room temperature for 3 minutes. Add 50 µL/well of Nano-Glo fluorofurimazine in vivo substrate (1:250 in Opti-MEM), incubate for 3 minutes at room temperature, shake for 20 seconds, and measure donor emission at 495 nm and acceptor emission at 600 nm (0.3-second integration time) within 40 minutes of substrate addition. Please deliver raw luminescent values, calculated BRET ratios (600 nm / 495 nm), and dose-response target displacement curves compared against the DMSO tracer-only control.
