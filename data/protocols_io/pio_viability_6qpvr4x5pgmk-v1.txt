ABSTRACT
MTT assay to measure viability cells

STEPS
1. Prepare sample in DMSO
2. Observe cell on the plate, make a note about cells condition
3. Experiment by make a serial dilution sample in U-Type, suspend 40 times
4. Remove old medium from cells
5. Put 100 μl/well sample and DMSO, incubate 48 h, 37 oC
6. Observe condition cells after 48 h incubation
7. Checked toxicity under microscope, take photo if there are morphology changing
8. Prepare MTT 10%
9. Remove medium from 96 well plate
10. Put 150 μl/well by multichannel pipet, incubate 4 h, 37 oC
11. Remove MTT solution from well plate
12. Put 100 μl/well DMSO 100% for dissolve
13. Check absorbance at 560 nm and 750 nm , shaker 0.5 min before read absorbance
