Please run the MTT assay on our HepG2 cells using our provided extracts, following this procedure:

1. Seed HepG2 cells in a 96-well plate at a density of 1 × 10^5 cells per well and incubate at 37°C for 24 hours.
2. Treat the cells with our extracts for 6 hours.
3. Add 0.5 mg/mL MTT reagent (dissolved in MEM) to each well and incubate the plate at 37°C for 3 hours.
4. Discard the culture medium, wash the cells once with PBS (pH 7.2), and add DMSO to solubilize the formazan product.
5. Measure the luminescence using your microplate reader and send us the raw readings along with the calculated viability data.
