Please prepare Precision Cut Skin Slices (PCSS) from our provided mouse back skin samples, maintain them in culture, and execute the downstream viability, extraction, and histological assays outlined below.

1. Sample Preparation and Sectioning: Trim skin samples into ~10 × 20 mm rectangles, removing any superfluous fat and fascia. Mount each sample flat between two matching histology foam pads to form a compact sandwich. Glue the end of the sandwich onto a Compresstome specimen holder piston and embed it in 8% low-melting point (LMP) agarose pre-warmed to 42°C. Solidify the embedded assembly at -20°C for 90 seconds, then hold at 4°C for 15 minutes. Using a Compresstome VF-300OZ (speed 4–6, oscillation 4, submerged in 4°C PBS), cut 500 µm longitudinal sections. Discard the outer sections containing glue.

2. Slice Recovery and Culture Maintenance: Separate the skin slices from the foam pads in warm PBS. Place each PCSS into a well of a 24-well plate containing 500 µL of high-glucose DMEM supplemented with 20% FBS, 25 mM HEPES, 2 mM sodium pyruvate, 1% Pen/Strep, and 1% non-essential amino acids. Incubate at 37°C with 5% CO2 for 5 hours to clear sectioning-induced debris. Aspirate the media, gently wash each well with warm PBS, and replace with 500 µL of the same supplemented DMEM but containing 10% FBS. Maintain culture at 37°C, 5% CO2, replacing the media every 24 hours for up to 8 days.

3. LDH and ATP Viability Assays: Collect daily conditioned media for LDH release assays (absorbance at 490 nm); use media from slices treated with 2% Triton X-100 for 10 minutes as positive controls. For metabolic viability, homogenize individual PCSS in 500 µL TE buffer using a bead mill (2.8 mm zirconium beads, 4 m/s, 4 cycles of 40 s on / 60 s off). Centrifuge homogenates at 1,000 × g for 1 minute at 4°C, collect the clear supernatant avoiding lipid and pellet debris, and perform ATP bioluminescence quantification using Luciferase Reagent against a standard curve.

4. RNA and Protein Isolation: Homogenize individual PCSS (for RNA) or pooled pairs (for protein) in 750 µL TRIzol using the same bead mill settings. Perform phase separation by adding chloroform (150 µL), vortexing, and centrifuging at 12,000 × g for 15 min at -80 °C. Extract RNA from the upper aqueous phase using silica spin columns with on-column rDNase treatment, and quantify via spectrophotometry. Precipitate proteins from the lower organic phase using ethanol and 100% isopropanol washes. Wash pellets with 7 M GndCl and ethanol, resolubilize in freshly prepared Tris/Urea/SDS buffer (8 M Urea, 40 mM Tris-HCl, 1% SDS, 2 mM Na-pyrophosphate, 100 mM NaF, 1 mM PMSF, 5 mM EDTA, 1x PhosSTOP, 1x cOmplete mini), and quantify using a BCA assay (absorbance at 562 nm).

5. Tissue Dissociation and Cell Counting: Cut designated PCSS into ~2 mm pieces and digest overnight (~16 h) at 37°C in 4 mL of HBSS containing 1 mg/mL Collagenase IV and 0.4 mg/mL Dispase II (pooling slices in pairs). Quench with 1 mL FBS, filter through a 70 µm cell strainer, centrifuge at 500 × g for 5 minutes, and resuspend the cell pellet in 1 mL complete media. Mix an aliquot 9:1 with Acridine Orange/Propidium Iodide stain and count live/dead cells on an automated fluorescence cell counter.

6. Histology and TUNEL Analysis: Fix designated PCSS flat between foam pads in 4% PFA for up to 24 hours at 4°C (transfer to PBS if storing longer). Process through graded ethanol and xylene, embed in paraffin, section at 4 µm onto Superfrost slides, and dry at 37°C. Perform standard H&E staining on standard slides. For apoptosis detection, perform TUNEL staining on adhesion slides: permeabilize with 1x Proteinase K (20 min at 37°C), apply TdT enzyme and fluorescent labeling solution (1 h at 37°C in the dark), counterstain nuclei with DAPI, and mount with anti-fade reagent. Include a DNase I-treated positive control skin section and a TdT-omitted negative control skin section. Image via fluorescence microscopy.
