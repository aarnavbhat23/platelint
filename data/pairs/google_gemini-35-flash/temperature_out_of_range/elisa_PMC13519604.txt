1. Bone Marrow Harvest and BMDM Differentiation: Euthanize C57BL/6J mice, harvest femurs and tibias, and flush bone marrow into cold DMEM. Perform RBC lysis with ACK lysing buffer for 2–5 minutes, filter through a 40 μm strainer, and seed 25–35 × 10^6 cells per 15 cm dish in complete DMEM (DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% Pen/Strep) supplemented with 20–40 ng/mL M-CSF. Culture for 5 days at 37°C. On day 5, harvest macrophages using cold detachment buffer (2 mM EDTA, 2% FBS in DPBS without Ca/Mg; 20 minutes at 4°C). Confirm cell purity via flow cytometry for CD11b and F4/80 (ensuring >90% purity). Seed cells into plates at standard densities (e.g., 5.0 × 10^5 cells/well in 24-well plates or 1 × 10^6 cells/well in 12-well plates) and rest for 16–18 hours in complete DMEM without M-CSF prior to stimulation.

2. Stimulation Conditions:
- TLR and NOD Agonists: Treat rested BMDMs with LPS (100 ng/mL), LTA (1 μg/mL), Pam3CSK4 (100 ng/mL), Zymosan (10 μg/mL), R848 (5 μg/mL), CpG (500 nM), or MDP (1–10 μg/mL) in complete DMEM.
- Cytosolic STING Agonists: Formulate ligand-lipid complexes by combining 50 μL Opti-MEM containing ligand (ISD: 0.5 μg/well; poly(dG:dC): 0.5 μg/well; or 2′3′-cGAMP: 2.5 μg/well for 24-well format) with 50 μL Opti-MEM containing 1.5 μL lipid transfection reagent. Incubate complexes for 15 minutes at room temperature, add dropwise to wells containing fresh media (final working concentrations: 1 μg/mL ISD, 1 μg/mL poly(dG:dC), 5 μg/mL 2′3′-cGAMP), and include mock transfection controls.
- M2 / Wound Healing: Treat with IL-4 (10–20 ng/mL) with or without IL-13 (10–20 ng/mL) in complete DMEM without M-CSF.
- Tolerogenic Efferocytosis: Prepare apoptotic source cells (thymocytes or lymphoid line) via 40 Gy irradiation (6 h incubation) or 1 μM staurosporine (4–6 h incubation). Wash with PBS, verify apoptotic state via Annexin V+/PI- flow cytometry, and co-culture with BMDMs at a 5:1 or 10:1 target-to-macrophage ratio.
- Tumor Microenvironment Contexts: Treat BMDMs with 20% Tumor Conditioned Media (TCM, processed from tumor tissue homogenates filtered at 0.2 μm) or 50% Tumor Cell Supernatant (TCS, collected from 80%–90% confluent cell cultures).

3. Assay Harvest and Readouts:
- 8-Hour Readouts: Collect cell culture supernatants in 96-well plates and store at −75 °C for cytokine measurement by ELISA. For RNA analysis, wash cells with PBS, lyse in RLT buffer supplemented with β-mercaptoethanol (20 μL/mL), store at −20°C, and analyze gene expression by RT-qPCR for targets including Irf5, Irf7, Ifnα1, Arg1, Retnla, Chil3, Mrc1, Il10, Tgfb1, and MerTK.
- 24-Hour Readouts: Harvest cells and run flow cytometry for activation markers (MHC-I, CD40, CD86) and regulatory/immunosuppressive markers (Arg1, CD206, PD-L1, PD-L2, MerTK, CD36).
