1. Thaw and plate our iPSC samples using recombinant human vitronectin-N (VTN)-coated 6-well plates and Complete Essential 8 (E8) medium supplemented with CultureCEPT. Start the procedure at 16:00, perform the first medium change after 18 hours, and subsequently maintain the cells with daily 2 mL/well E8 medium changes until they reach 60–75% confluence.

2. Perform EDTA-based clump passaging when the iPSCs reach 60–75% confluence by washing with DPBS -/-, incubating with 0.5 mM EDTA for 2–3 min at 37 °C, 5% CO2, and splitting at a 1:4 ratio onto VTN-coated 6-well plates using Complete E8 medium. 

3. Cryopreserve iPSC cultures at 60–75% confluence by rinsing with DPBS -/-, incubating with 0.5 mM EDTA for 2–3 min, and resuspending each well in 2 µL of Complete E8 medium with 10% DMSO. Aliquot 1 mL per cryovial, place them in a freezing container at −80 °C for 24–48 hours, and transfer to long-term storage below −140 °C.

4. Perform CRISPR/Cas9 editing on the iPSCs using the Neon Electroporation System. Maintain cells prior to electroporation in E8 or StemFlex medium without Penicillin-Strep, supplemented with 10 µM Y-27632. Form RNP complexes using a 1:1 molar ratio of Alt-R S.p. HiFi Cas9 Nuclease V3 and an equimolar crRNA:tracrRNA duplex (prepared by heating at 95 °C for 5 min and cooling at RT). If performing HDR, include the Alt-R HDR Donor Oligo and Alt-R HDR Enhancer V2 (0.1–1 µM). Dissociate cells using StemPro Accutase with 10 µM Y-27632 for 9–10 min, count, and resuspend in Resuspension Buffer R at 2.22 × 10^7 cells/mL. Electroporate using the Neon system at 1400 V, 20 ms width, 1 pulse (2 × 10^5 cells/reaction). Plate cells into VTN-coated 6-well plates with pre-warmed medium and Y-27632/Enhancer V2, and replace with fresh medium without additives after 18 hours.

5. Screen edited iPSC pools for NHEJ and HDR using droplet digital PCR (ddPCR). Isolate gDNA using either the DNeasy Blood & Tissue Kit from cell pellets or the Phire Animal Tissue Direct PCR Kit from culture supernatant harvested at least 72 hours post-electroporation. Set up ddPCR reactions using ddPCR Supermix for Probes (No dUTP) (2X), custom 20X Genome Editing Detection Assays, 20 ng gDNA template, optional restriction enzymes (2–5 U) for fragmentation, and nuclease-free water to 22 µL. Generate droplets using the QX200 Droplet Generator with DG8 Cartridges and Oil, seal the plate, and run the thermal cycler with enzyme activation at 95 °C for 10 min, 40 cycles of denaturation at 94 °C for 30 s and annealing/extension at 55 °C, followed by enzyme deactivation at 98 °C for 10 min. Read plates on the QX200 Droplet Reader and analyze fractional abundance or HDR ratios using QX Manager Software.

6. Perform PCR and Sanger sequencing to verify edits by amplifying target loci using AmpliTaq Gold 360 Master Mix with 10 ng gDNA and 10 µM forward and reverse primers, running initial denaturation at 98 °C for 30 s, 35 cycles of 98 °C for 30 s, annealing, and 72 °C extension, followed by agarose gel electrophoresis and sequencing submission.

7. Perform single-cell cloning of iPSCs using either the UP.SIGHT or Dispencell-S3 platform. Pre-warm VTN-coated 96-well plates containing StemFlex medium with CultureCEPT. Dissociate cultures at 60–75% confluency using StemPro Accutase with CultureCEPT for ≤ 10 min, filter through a 40 µm strainer, count, and dispense precisely 1 cell per well. Incubate the plates undisturbed at 37 °C, 5% CO2 for 64 hours, then perform scheduled medium additions and exchanges with StemFlex medium every 48 hours until colony outgrowth exceeds 30% confluence around day 8 to 10.

8. Screen single-cell clones during expansion in 24-well plates by collecting cell culture supernatant, isolating DNA using the Phire Animal Tissue Direct PCR Kit (dilution buffer and DNA Release Additive, heat-treated at 98 °C for 2 min), and performing PCR and Sanger sequencing. Expand sequence-verified clones to 60–75% confluence, perform EDTA clump passaging into 6-well plates with E8 medium, and generate cryopreservation batches. Proceed with advanced characterization (WES, karyotyping, differentiation assays) on validated clones as required.
