1. We will send you 89 pig caecal content samples (≈35 g each) and 68 carcass skin swab samples (each collected with a 10 × 10 cm, 16‑layer gauze tampon moistened with 15 mL buffered peptone water and covering 1400 cm² of medial ham/abdomen). Keep the samples chilled and process within 24 h of arrival; thereafter store at –20 °C until you begin the assays. 

2. For each caecal sample: 
 a. Add 1 g of caecal material to 9 mL buffered peptone water (BPW, Oxoid CM0509) and vortex thoroughly. 
 b. Prepare a 10⁻¹ dilution, then make a 10‑fold serial dilution series in 0.9 % NaCl (up to at least 10⁻⁶). 
 c. Plate 1 mL of the appropriate dilution on each of the following Petrifilm plates (Neogen/ Fooddes, Silkeborg, Denmark): 
 • SEC (Select E. coli) – for total E. coli (add 25 µL of 0.9 % NaCl to the dilution before plating). 
 • EB (Enterobacteriaceae) – for total Enterobacteriaceae (add 25 µL of 0.9 % NaCl). 
 • SEC with tetracycline – add 25 µL of a tetracycline stock (Sigma T3383) to give a final concentration of 64 mg L⁻¹ in the plate; this will enumerate tetracycline‑resistant E. coli. 
 d. Incubate all Petrifilm plates at 37 °C for 16–18 h. 

3. For each skin‑swab sample: 
 a. Transfer the used gauze into a stomacher bag containing 85 mL BPW and knead for 2 min. 
 b. Prepare a 10‑fold serial dilution series in 0.9 % NaCl (as above). 
 c. Plate 1 mL of the appropriate dilutions on the same three Petrifilm types and incubate as in step 2d. 

4. Aliquot for qPCR: 
 • From each caecal 10⁻¹ dilution, keep 1 mL at –20 °C. 
 • From each skin‑swab initial dilution (the 85 mL BPW suspension), keep 10 mL at –20 °C. 

5. DNA extraction (all extractions using Qiagen DNeasy PowerLyzer PowerSoil Kit, cat. 12855): 
 a. Thaw the stored aliquots at 5 °C. 
 b. Caecal: centrifuge 0.5 mL of the 10⁻¹ dilution at 13 000 rpm for 5 min (Eppendorf Mini Spin). 
 c. Skin‑swab: centrifuge 10 mL of the initial dilution at 10 000 rpm for 5 min (Eppendorf 5810R). 
 d. Discard supernatant, resuspend each pellet in 750 µL PowerBeads solution + 60 µL C1 reagent, then follow the kit protocol. Elute DNA in 100 µL TE (or the kit’s elution buffer) and store at –20 °C. 

6. Prepare qPCR standard curves for each target gene (uidA, tetA, tetB): 
 a. We will provide two E. coli reference strains (NCTC 50078 – carries tetA & uidA; CSH50::Tn10 – carries tetB). 
 b. Grow each strain overnight in Brain Heart Infusion broth at 37 °C. 
 c. Determine CFU mL⁻¹ by plating a 10‑fold dilution series on BHI agar containing 16 µg mL⁻¹ tetracycline. 
 d. Extract DNA from ~8 log₁₀ CFU mL⁻¹ cultures using the same protocol as step 5. 
 e. Dilute the extracted DNA in Milli‑Q water to generate a series corresponding to 1.6 – 7 log₁₀ CFU mL⁻¹ for each gene. Include each dilution in every qPCR run to build a single standard curve per gene. 

7. qPCR set‑up (run each sample in duplicate): 
 * Master mix per 25 µL reaction: 
 – 1× TaqMan™ Environmental Master Mix (Applied Biosystems, cat. 4396838) 
 – 0.5 µM forward primer (LGC Biosearch) 
 – 0.5 µM reverse primer (LGC Biosearch) 
 – 0.2 µM TaqMan probe (LGC Biosearch) 
 – 5 µL extracted DNA template (or water for the no‑template control). 
 * Primer/probe sequences (5′→3′): 
 – uidA F: CGGAAGCAACGCGTAAACTC R: TGAGCGTCGCAGAACATTACA Probe (FAM‑CGCGTCCGATCACCTGCGTC‑TAMRA) – 90 bp amplicon. 
 – tetA F: TTGGCATTCTGCATTCACTC R: GAAGGCAAGCAGGATGTAGC Probe (FAM‑GATCACCGGCCCTGTAGCCG‑BHQ) – 125 bp. 
 – tetB F: TTACGTGAATTTATTGCTTCGG R: ATACAGCATCCAAAGCGCAC Probe (FAM‑CGCCGACCAAATCGGTCAGA‑BHQ‑1) – 206 bp. 
 * Plate in a 96‑well, non‑skirted MX3000P plate with optical strip caps (Agilent). 
 * Thermocycling on a Stratagene MX3005P: 
 – 95 °C 10 min (polymerase activation) 
 – 45 cycles of 95 °C 15 s, 60 °C 1 min. 
 * Include for each run: 
 – Two no‑template controls (sterile water). 


8. Data handling: 
 * Record Ct values for each duplicate; calculate the mean Ct per sample. 
 * Convert mean Ct to log₁₀ CFU g⁻¹ (caecal) or log₁₀ CFU 1400 cm²⁻¹ (skin) using the appropriate standard curve. 
 * Adjust for any dilution factors introduced during plating or DNA extraction. 

9. Deliverables: 
 * Raw colony counts from each Petrifilm plate (total E. coli, Enterobacteriaceae, tetracycline‑resistant E. coli) per sample. 
 * DNA concentration (optional) and the qPCR Ct values for uidA, tetA, and tetB (duplicates). 
 * Calculated log₁₀ CFU values for each target, with the dilution corrections applied.
