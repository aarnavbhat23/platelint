Please perform the following on our SH‑SY5Y samples (passage 29‑32) and deliver the complete data set (raw and processed) as described.

1. **Cell culture** – Grow SH‑SY5Y (ATCC CRL‑2266) in a 1:1 mixture of DMEM and Nutrient Mixture F12 HAM with Sodium B, supplemented with 10 % heat‑inactivated FBS, 10 IU mL⁻¹ penicillin, 0.1 mg mL⁻¹ streptomycin, 1 µg mL⁻¹ amphotericin B, and 1 % non‑essential amino acids. Replace fresh media every 3 days.  

2. **Membrane protein extraction** – Wash confluent cells with PBS, then incubate sequentially on ice with:  
   a. Extraction buffer 1 (EB1): 10 mM PIPES, 100 mM NaCl, 5 mM EDTA, 300 mM sucrose, 0.015 % digitonin, plus Halt protease inhibitor (1:100 v/v) for 30 min with gentle agitation; centrifuge 1000 × g, 5 min, 4 °C, collect supernatant.  
   b. Extraction buffer 2 (EB2): 10 % Triton X‑100, same inhibitor cocktail, incubate 30 min, centrifuge 5000 × g, 10 min, 4 °C, collect supernatant.  
   Store combined supernatants at −80 °C. Quantify protein using Pierce BCA kit (absorbance 562 nm, TE‑CAN Infinite).  

3. **Nanoparticle (NP) fabrication** – Prepare lipid mixture (DPPC:DOPC:cholesterol = 4:3:3) in ethanol to a final lipid concentration of 10 mM. For “Neurosomes” add SH‑SY5Y membrane protein extract (PBS‑diluted) to achieve a protein‑to‑lipid mass ratio of 1:50; for empty liposomes use PBS only. For Dox‑loaded NPs replace PBS with 250 mM ammonium sulfate (pH 4.4). Pre‑heat both phases to 45 °C before mixing.  

   a. **Turbulent jet mixing (NOVA IJM)** – Inject lipid and aqueous phases simultaneously at a flow‑rate ratio (FRR) 1:2 (lipid:aqueous) and total flow rate (TFR) 3 mL min⁻¹. (Optional: test TFR 2.5 mL min⁻¹ and 4 mL min⁻¹ on liposome controls; keep 3 mL min⁻¹ for final data.)  

   b. **Microfluidic mixing (NanoAssemblr Ignite)** – Co‑inject at FRR 1:2, TFR 2.5 mL min⁻¹.  

   c. **Extrusion after ethanol injection** – Add lipid phase rapidly to aqueous phase (aqueous:lipid = 9:1 v/v). Extrude sequentially: two passes through 200 nm polycarbonate membranes, then one pass 200 nm plus two passes 80 nm (each twice).  

   After each method, dialyze NP suspensions (PBS, 4 °C) to remove ethanol and unencapsulated material:  
   • Neurosomes: 1000 kDa MWCO membrane, three cycles (1 h, 3 h, 24 h) with 1000‑fold buffer excess.  
   • Empty liposomes: 12–14 kDa MWCO, same schedule.  

   Incorporate 0.05 % 16:0 Liss‑Rhod PE (Sigma) for all NP batches intended for fluorescence assays.  

4. **Physicochemical characterization** – Filter each dialyzed NP suspension through 0.22 µm filter. Measure size, PDI, particle concentration on a Malvern Zetasizer Ultra (DLS) after 1:100 dilution in filtered PBS; run each sample in triplicate (10 runs per measurement). Measure zeta potential after 1:100 dilution in HPLC water; three independent readings per sample.  

5. **Cryo‑TEM** – Dilute NP samples 1:5 with HPLC‑grade water. Prepare grids on a Leica EM GP2 plunge freezer (25 °C, 90 % RH) using plasma‑treated, carbon‑coated 200‑mesh grids. Blot excess liquid, vitrify in liquid ethane (−180 °C). Store grids in liquid N₂ and image on a Thermo Fisher Talos 200 C TEM (200 kV) using a Gatan 626 cryo‑holder and Volta phase plate.  

6. **Stability in cell culture medium** – Mix NPs with SH‑SY5Y growth medium 1:4 (NP:medium). Incubate at 37 °C for 0 h (no incubation), 6 h, 24 h, and 48 h. Recover NPs by ultracentrifugation 100 000 × g, 1 h, 4 °C, discard supernatant, resuspend pellet in PBS, and re‑measure size, PDI, and zeta potential as in step 4.  

7. **Doxorubicin active loading** – Add Dox·HCl (Sigma) to NP suspensions to a final concentration of 1 mg mL⁻¹. Incubate 60 min at 40 °C with gentle shaking. Remove free drug by overnight dialysis in PBS at -80 °C. Determine encapsulation efficiency: lyse NPs with 0.5 % Triton X‑100, measure fluorescence (exc 495 nm, em 590 nm) on a plate reader, compare to a Dox standard curve, and calculate % encapsulated = (encapsulated / total added) × 100.  

8. **Dox release assay** – Load a known amount of Dox‑NP into 10–12 kDa MWCO dialysis bags. Place bags in PBS (pH 7.4) at 4 °C and 37 °C (separate samples). Collect aliquots from the external buffer at 8, 24, 48, and 72 h. Measure Dox fluorescence (495/590 nm) and calculate remaining (non‑released) drug. Express cumulative release as % of initial encapsulated amount.  

9. **Proteomic preparation** – Precipitate NP‑associated proteins with 80 % cold acetone overnight, wash 3× with cold acetone, air‑dry, then resuspend in 8.5 M urea, 100 mM ammonium bicarbonate, 10 mM DTT. Quantify by Bradford assay. Reduce (60 °C, 30 min), alkylate with 35.2 mM iodoacetamide (30 min, RT, dark). Digest with trypsin (1:50 w/w) overnight at 37 °C, then a second 4 h digestion (1:100). Desalt on Waters HLB plates, dry, and reconstitute in 0.1 % formic acid.  

10. **LC‑MS/MS** – Inject samples on a Q‑Exactive HFX coupled to an Ultimate 3000 HPLC. Use a 30 cm C18 column, 120‑min linear gradient 5 %→28 % acetonitrile (0.1 % formic acid), then ramp to 95 % over 15 min. Acquire MS1 at 120 k resolution (350‑1200 m/z) and MS2 at 20 k (top 20, HCD). Process data with MaxQuant v2.4.2.0 (Andromeda, human UniProt Aug 2023, target‑decoy, 1 % FDR, match‑between‑runs 1 min). Export iBAQ and LFQ intensities.  

11. **Proteomic data analysis** – In Perseus v2.0.10.0, log2‑transform LFQ values, filter for proteins present in all three replicates of any group, classify membrane proteins via GOCC terms (“integral component of membrane”, “plasma membrane”, “cell surface”, “membrane raft”), compute % membrane proteins = (sum iBAQ of membrane proteins / sum iBAQ of all proteins) × 100. Generate heatmaps of selected membrane/non‑membrane proteins (log2‑averaged LFQ) with Euclidean distance, complete linkage clustering.  

12. **SDS‑PAGE** – Mix NP samples and the original SH‑SY5Y protein extract with 5× sample buffer (GenScript MB01015), denature 95 °C, 5 min. Load equal protein amounts alongside a molecular‑weight marker, run at 120 V for ~1 h, wash gel, stain with Imperial Protein Stain (Thermo 24615) and document band patterns.  

13. **Neurosome association assay** – Seed SH‑SY5Y cells (1 × 10⁴ per well) in black‑clear 96‑well plates. Add rhodamine‑labeled NPs (0.25 mM and 0.1 mM lipid) and incubate 4, 12, and 24 h at 37 °C. Wash with PBS, stain nuclei with Hoechst 33342 (1:10 000). Image on a BioTek Cytation 5 (10×). Quantify total rhodamine fluorescence adjacent to nuclei, normalize to nuclear area, and further normalize each value to the fluorescence of the corresponding NP alone (no cells).  

14. **MTT viability** – Seed SH‑SY5Y cells (1 × 10⁴ per well) in 96‑well plates, treat with NPs (0.25 mM or 0.1 mM lipid) for 24 h at 37 °C. Replace media with 0.5 mg mL⁻¹ MTT (Rhenium M6494MTT), incubate 2 h, discard MTT, add DMSO to solubilize formazan, read absorbance at 570 nm. Express viability relative to untreated controls.  

15. **Statistical analysis** – Perform at least three independent replicates for each assay. Report means ± SEM. Use GraphPad Prism 10: unpaired two‑tailed t‑test for two‑group comparisons; one‑ or two‑way ANOVA with Tukey’s or Dunnett’s post‑hoc for multiple groups; Pearson correlation where appropriate. Significance threshold p < 0.05.
