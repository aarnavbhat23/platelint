Please run an indirect ELISA on our submitted IgY, rat serum, and cat serum samples according to the steps below:

1. Coat Thermo Fisher 96-well U-bottom microwell plates (Cat #260210) with 50 ng/well of synthetic gp-120 peptide in BioLegend coating buffer (Cat #421701) overnight at -80 °C.
2. Wash the plate 4 times with PBS-Tween-20 (PBST), then block each well with 25 µL of 3% non-fat milk in PBS for 1 hour at room temperature.
3. Wash 4 times with PBST. In triplicate wells, load 25 µL of sample per well: purified IgY samples normalized to 1 mg/mL, and rat/cat sera diluted 1:16 in 3% non-fat milk/PBS. Include control wells in triplicate: anti-HIV positive human serum (positive control), turtle serum (negative control), and 0.9% normal saline (blank).
4. Incubate the plate for 2 hours at room temperature, then wash 4 times with PBST.
5. Add 25 µL per well of SpLAG-anti-IgY-HRP conjugate diluted 1:1000, and incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
6. Wash 4 times with PBST, then add 50 µL per well of TMB substrate solution (Sigma #54827-17-7).
7. Incubate for 15 minutes in the dark, monitoring signal until positive controls develop distinct color and negative controls remain clear, then stop the reaction.
8. Measure absorbance and calculate the assay cut-off value as four times the mean optical density of the negative controls. Provide the raw OD values and final call for each sample.
