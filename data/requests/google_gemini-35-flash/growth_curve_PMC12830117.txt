1. Formulate and 0.22-μm filter-sterilize the following specialized culture media: C10 medium (RPMI, 10% FBS, 1× P/S/G, 1× MEM NEAA, 10 mM HEPES, 1 mM sodium pyruvate, 50 μM β-mercaptoethanol, 1× Normocin); R10 medium (RPMI, 10% FBS, 1× P/S/G, 1× Normocin); HSPC culture/Stage 0 medium (X-VIVO 15 with 50 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL Flt3L, 50 ng/mL TPO, and 20 ng/mL IL-3); HSPC expansion medium (StemSpan SFEM II with 1× StemSpan Lymphoid Progenitor Expansion Supplement); NKT differentiation medium (StemSpan SFEM II with 1× StemSpan Lymphoid Progenitor Maturation Supplement); NKT deep differentiation medium (NKT differentiation medium plus 10 ng/mL recombinant human IL-15); and NKT expansion medium (C10 medium plus 10 ng/mL human IL-7 and 10 ng/mL human IL-15).

2. Prepare non–tissue-culture–treated 6-well plates by coating with 20 μg/mL Retronectin in PBS (1 mL/well) for 2 hours at room temperature, blocking with 2% BSA in PBS for 30 minutes, and washing with PBS. Rapidly thaw the provided human CD34+ HSPCs, wash in X-VIVO 15, resuspend in HSPC culture medium at 1 × 10^6 cells/mL, and seed 1 mL/well into the Retronectin-coated plate. Incubate at 37°C, 5% CO2 for 12–18 hours.

3. Transduce the HSPCs by adding concentrated Lenti/iNKT-CAR70-IL-15 viral supernatant at an MOI of 100 (or final concentration of 1 × 10^8 IFU/mL). Mix gently and incubate for 24 hours at 37°C, 5% CO2. Harvest cells using cold HSPC culture medium washes, count viable cells, and resuspend in HSPC expansion medium at 2 × 10^4 cells per 500 μL. Optionally retain a subset of cells to confirm intracellular Vβ11 expression by flow cytometry 3 days post-transduction.

4. Coat a non–tissue-culture–treated 24-well plate with StemSpan Lymphoid Differentiation Coating Material for 2 hours at room temperature (or 16–20 hours at 4°C) and rinse with PBS. Transfer the engineered HSPCs into coated wells (500 μL/well) and culture at 37°C, 5% CO2. Add 500 μL of fresh HSPC expansion medium per well on day 4. Perform a 50% medium replacement on day 7 and day 11 using fresh expansion medium.

5. On day 14, harvest the expanded cells, count, and resuspend in NKT differentiation medium at 1 × 10^5 cells/mL (cryopreserve any excess cells at 2 × 10^5 cells per 500 μL in freezing medium). Transfer 2 mL/well into non-TC 6-well plates pre-coated with Lymphoid Differentiation Coating Material. Culture for 1 week at 37°C, 5% CO2, passaging 2–3 times to maintain a density of 0.5–1 × 10^6 cells/mL. At the end of the week, analyze 1 × 10^5 cells by flow cytometry for CD3, TCRαβ, NKT TCR (6B11), CAR70 (anti-CD27), CD4, CD8, and viability (eFluor 506). Target phenotype is >70% CAR-NKT cells and >90% viability.

6. Re-coat non-TC 6-well plates with Lymphoid Differentiation Coating Material. Resuspend the Stage 3 cells at 5 × 10^5 cells/mL in NKT deep differentiation medium supplemented with ImmunoCult Human CD3/CD28/CD2 T Cell Activator (12.5 μL/mL). Seed 2 mL/well and culture for 1 week, passaging 2–3 times to maintain 0.5–1 × 10^6 cells/mL. Assess deep differentiation by flow cytometry; target phenotype is >95% NKT cells (CD3+ TCRαβ+ 6B11+ CAR70+) shifted toward CD4-CD8- or CD4-CD8+ subsets, with >90% viability.

7. Harvest the deep-differentiated cells. Prepare artificial antigen-presenting cells (aAPCs overexpressing CD80, CD83, CD86, 4-1BBL, and CD70) by irradiating them at 10,000 rads, passing through a 70 μm cell strainer, and washing. Combine the NKT cells and irradiated aAPCs at a 1:1 to 1:2 ratio and resuspend in NKT cell expansion medium (supplemented with 10 ng/mL IL-7 and 10 ng/mL IL-15) at 0.5–1 × 10^6 cells/mL. 

8. Seed 2 mL/well into non-precoated, non-TC 6-well plates and culture for 2 weeks at 37°C, 5% CO2. Passage 2–3 times per week to keep cells at 0.5–1 × 10^6 cells/mL, replenishing 10 ng/mL IL-7 and 10 ng/mL IL-15 at each passage. At day 14, harvest, perform final flow cytometric QC (>97% CD3+ TCRαβ+ 6B11+ CAR70+), and cryopreserve the final Allo CAR70-NKT cell product in CS10 freezing medium at 1 × 10^7 cells/mL.

9. Perform an in vitro cytotoxicity assay against Fluc-GFP-labeled RCC target cells (786-O-FG and ACHN-FG). Keep effector Allo CAR70-NKT cells and target cells on ice prior to plating. Seed target cells and CAR70-NKT effector cells (normalized to CAR+ cell count)
