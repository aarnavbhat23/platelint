Please run an IL-23 sandwich ELISA on our patient serum samples using the following procedure:

1. Coat 96-well ELISA plates with monoclonal anti-human IL-23 capture antibody.
2. Add our serum samples along with recombinant IL-23 standards for generating a standard curve.
3. Incubate the plate for 1.5 hours at room temperature.
4. Wash the wells 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 wash buffer.
5. Add biotin-conjugated anti-human IL-23 detection antibody to the wells and incubate at room temperature.
6. Wash 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
7. Add peroxidase-labeled streptavidin conjugate and incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
8. Wash 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 buffer, then add substrate solution to initiate the colorimetric reaction.
9. Stop the reaction by adding 3M H2SO4.
10. Read the absorbance of the wells at 450 nm.
11. Calculate the IL-23 concentrations in the serum samples by linear regression against the standard curve.

Please provide the raw absorbance readings and the calculated IL-23 concentration values for all samples once complete.
