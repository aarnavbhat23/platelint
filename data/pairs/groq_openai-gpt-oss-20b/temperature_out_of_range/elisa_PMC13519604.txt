**Request to the wet‑lab team**

1. **Bone marrow isolation & BMDM differentiation (C57BL/6J mice)**  
   - Euthanize 5–10 mice, sterilise hind limbs with 70 % ethanol.  
   - Dissect femurs and tibias, keeping bones in ice‑cold complete DMEM (10 % FBS, 1 % Pen/Strep).  
   - Cut bone ends, flush marrow into a 10 cm dish with a 27‑gauge needle and ice‑cold DMEM.  
   - Centrifuge 400 × g, 5 min, 4 °C.  
   - Resuspend pellet in ACK lysis buffer (2 mM EDTA) for 2–5 min at 25 °C, then add up to 10 mL PBS and centrifuge again (400 × g, 5 min, 4 °C).  
   - Resuspend final pellet in 10 mL complete DMEM, filter through a 40 µm filter.  
   - Add 20–40 ng/mL recombinant M‑CSF (e.g., 1 µg/mL stock).  
   - Seed 25–35 × 10^6 cells in each 15 cm dish with 20 mL medium.  
   - Incubate at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂, 5 days.  
   - On day 5, harvest BMDMs:  
     * Wash once with PBS, then add 10 mL detachment buffer (4 mL 0.2 M EDTA + 10 mL 100 % FBS in cold PBS). Incubate 20 min, 4 °C.  
     * Flush cells into a 15 mL tube, centrifuge 400 × g, 5 min, 4 °C.  
     * Count with trypan blue.  
     * Rest 16–18 h in complete DMEM (10 % FBS, 1 % Pen/Strep, no M‑CSF).  

2. **Seeding for assays**  
   - 24‑well plates: 5 × 10^5 cells/well in 500 µL.  
   - 12‑well plates: 1 × 10^6 cells/well in 1 mL.  
   - 6‑well plates: 2 × 10^6 cells/well in 2 mL.  

3. **TLR ligand stimulations (surface & endosomal)** – prepare fresh ligand solutions in complete DMEM:  
   - LPS 100 ng/mL (Sigma L2880).  
   - LTA 1 µg/mL (InvivoGen).  
   - Pam3CSK4 100 ng/mL (InvivoGen).  
   - Zymosan 10 µg/mL (InvivoGen).  
   - R848 5 µg/mL (InvivoGen).  
   - CpG 500 nM (InvivoGen).  
   *Add 1 mL medium per well (12‑well) or 500 µL per well (24‑well). Incubate 8–24 h at 75 °C.*  
   - **Readouts**:  
     * 8 h – collect supernatant for ELISA (cytokines).  
     * 8 h – add 350 µL/well RNA‑lysis buffer (RLT + 20 µL/mL β‑mercaptoethanol), store at –20 °C.  
     * 24 h – harvest for flow cytometry (MHC‑I, CD40, CD86, Arg1, CD206, PD‑L1).  

4. **NOD2 (MDP) stimulation** – prepare 1–10 µg/mL MDP (InvivoGen) in complete DMEM.  
   *Use alone or with submaximal TLR ligands. Incubate 8–24 h, collect supernatant for ELISA and/or harvest for RNA or flow as above.*  

5. **Cytosolic nucleic‑acid / STING stimulation** – stock:  
   - ISD 1 mg/mL, Poly(dG:dC) 1 mg/mL, 2′3′‑cGAMP 200 µg/mL (nuclease‑free water).  
   *Use working concentrations: ISD 1 µg/mL, Poly(dG:dC) 1 µg/mL, 2′3′‑cGAMP 5 µg/mL.*  
   *For 24‑well format:*  
     * Tube A: 50 µL Opti‑MEM + ligand (0.5 µg ISD / Poly(dG:dC) / 2.5 µg cGAMP).  
     * Tube B: 50 µL Opti‑MEM + 1.5 µL lipid reagent (e.g., Lipofectamine 2000).  
     * Mix gently, combine to 100 µL complex, incubate 15 min at 25 °C.  
     * Replace BMDM medium with 400 µL fresh DMEM (no M‑CSF) per well; add 100 µL complex dropwise to reach 500 µL.  
     * Incubate 1–24 h (typical 8 h for ELISA, 8 h for RNA, 24 h for flow).  
   *Optional*: pre‑treat with inhibitors 30–60 min before stimulation.

6. **Wound‑healing / IL‑4 stimulation** – after rest, add IL‑4 (10–20 ng/mL) ± IL‑13 (10–20 ng/mL) in complete DMEM (no M‑CSF).  
   *1 mL per 12‑well, 500 µL per 24‑well. Incubate 8–24 h (or 48 h for functional assays).*  
   *Readouts*: RT‑qPCR for Arg1, Retnla, Chil3, Mrc1; flow cytometry for Arg1, CD206, PD‑L1.

7. **Apoptotic cell clearance assay** – generate apoptotic cells:  
   - Thymocytes or a suitable lymphoid line; induce apoptosis by 40 Gy irradiation + 6 h incubation or staurosporine 1 µM + 4–6 h incubation.  
   - Wash with PBS, confirm >80 % Annexin V⁺/PI⁻.  
   - Co‑culture with BMDMs at 5:1 or 10:1 ratio in complete DMEM (10 % FBS, 1 % Pen/Strep, no M‑CSF).  
   - Incubate 2–4 h for uptake, 8–24 h for gene expression.  
   *Readouts*: flow (fluorescently labelled apoptotic cells, Arg1, IL‑10, PD‑L1, MerTK, CD36); RT‑qPCR (Il10, Tgfb1, Ahr targets, MerTK); ELISA (IL‑10, TGF‑β).

8. **Tumor‑conditioned media (TCM)** – prepare from C57BL/6J mice bearing 2838c3 PDAC (subcutaneous or orthotopic) after 21 days or 500–700 mm³ tumour.  
   - Collect tumour, weigh, add 1 mL DMEM per 200 mg tissue.  
   - Cut, crush, incubate 2 h at 37 °C.  
   - Centrifuge 400 × g, 5 min, then 21 000 × g, 10 min.  
   - Filter 0.2 µm; store at –20 °C.  
   *Stimulation*: Add TCM to BMDM wells to a final 20 % (v/v) concentration; incubate 8 h for RNA, 24 h for flow.

9. **Tumor cell supernatant (TCS)** – culture 2838c3 cells to 80–90 % confluence, collect spent media after trypsinization, spin 400 × g, 5 min, store at –20 °C (optional 0.2 µm filter).  
   *Stimulation*: Add TCS to BMDM wells to a final 50 % concentration; incubate 8 h for RNA, 24 h for flow.

10. **Readout procedures** –  
    - ELISA for cytokines (IL‑6, TNF‑α, IL‑1β, IFN‑β, IL‑10, TGF‑β).  
    - RNA isolation (TRIzol or RLT buffer), qPCR for genes listed above.  
    - Flow cytometry panels: F4/80, CD11b, MHC‑I, CD40, CD86, Arg1, CD206, PD‑L1, MerTK, CD36, IL‑10, TGF‑β.  
    - Optional: multiomics, western blot, phagocytosis assays.

Please confirm that all reagents (LPS, CpG, Pam3CSK4, LTA, Zymosan, R848, MDP, ISD, Poly(dG:dC), 2′3′‑cGAMP, IL‑4, IL‑13, etc.) are available at the stated concentrations or provide alternative suppliers.
