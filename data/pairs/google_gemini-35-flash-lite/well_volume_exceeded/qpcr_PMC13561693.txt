Please run the 2D induction and 3D budoid generation protocol on our provided EmbryoMax mESC line (strain 129/SVEV, passage 11). 

Prepare the required media formulations prior to starting: DMEM base medium, DMEM+++ medium (supplemented with 100 ng/mL human LIF, 1 μM PD0325901, and 3 μM CHIR-99021), non-neural ectoderm (NNE) medium (GMEM with 1.5% KnockOut serum replacement, 1x NEAA, 1 mM sodium pyruvate, 0.2% Normocin, and 91 μM 2-mercaptoethanol, protected from light), Induction Medium I (NNE supplemented with 100 ng/mL BMP-4 and 1 μM SB 431542), Induction Medium II (NNE supplemented with 100 ng/mL BMP-4, 100 ng/mL FGF-10, and 3 μM CHIR-99021), and Budoid Generation Media (NNE supplemented with 450 ng/mL FGF-8b and 300 ng/mL Wnt-3a).

For the 2D induction phase, maintain the mESCs on 0.1% gelatin-coated plates in DMEM+++ medium, performing passages when reaching 70%–80% confluence. On Day 0, seed a 12-well gelatin-coated plate at 40,000 cells/mL in 1 mL NNE medium per well. Perform media changes and treatments on subsequent days: wash with PBS and replace with NNE medium on Day 1; add fresh Induction Medium I on Day 3; perform two PBS washes and add Induction Medium II on Day 5; and conclude the 2D induction on Day 7, where dome structures should be visible.

For the 3D budoid generation phase starting on Day 7, dissociate the 2D induction cultures with trypsin-EDTA, deactivate with DMEM base, pellet at 400× g, and resuspend the cells in Budoid Generation Media. Prepare a 96-well U-bottom plate by filling outer wells with 200 μL PBS, and seed 9,000 cells in 7200 µL of Budoid Generation Media per inner well (60 wells total). On Days 9 and 11, perform gentle media changes by removing 50 μL of media from each well and adding 50 μL of fresh NNE medium without disturbing the aggregates. Conclude the protocol on Day 12 when the budoids have elongated, and collect them using low-attachment pipette tips with cut ends for downstream analysis.
