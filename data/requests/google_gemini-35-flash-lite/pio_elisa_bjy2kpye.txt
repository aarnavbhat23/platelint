1. Coat 96-well ELISA plates with monoclonal anti-human IL-23 antibodies.
2. Add our provided patient serum samples and user-contributed IL-23 from IBL Corporation Germany to the plates.
3. Incubate the plates for 1 hour and 30 minutes at room temperature.
4. Wash the plates 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
5. Add biotin-conjugated anti-human IL-23 to the wells and incubate for 1 hour and 30 minutes at room temperature.
6. Repeat the 4-times washing procedure with PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
7. Add a peroxidase-labeled streptavidin conjugate to the plates and incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
8. Perform a further washing procedure using the same buffer.
9. Add the reactive substrate solution and allow color to develop in the positive controls.
10. Stop the reaction by adding 3M H2SO4.
11. Measure the absorbance at 450 nm to determine IL-17 concentrations, which are proportional to the colored product, by generating a standard curve using linear regression.
