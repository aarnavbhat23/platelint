1. Culture SH-SY5Y cells (CRL-2266, ATCC) in 50% DMEM, 50% Nutrient Mixture F12 HAM with Sodium B, supplemented with 10% heat-inactivated FBS, 10 IU/mL penicillin, 0.1 mg/mL streptomycin, 1 µg/mL Amphotericin B, and 1% non-essential amino acids, refreshing media every 3 days. Use cells between passages 29–32 for protein extraction.

2. Extract membrane proteins from the cultured cells using the Triton X-100-based protocol with sequential incubation in EB1 (10 mM PIPES, 100 mM NaCl, 5 mM EDTA, 300 mM sucrose, 0.015% digitonin) and EB2 (10% Triton X-100), both containing 1:100 Halt Protease Inhibitor Cocktail at 4°C with gentle agitation. Collect supernatants via centrifugation at 1000 × g (EB1) and 5000 × g (EB2), store at −80°C, and quantify protein concentration using the Pierce BCA Protein Assay Kit measuring absorbance at 562 nm.

3. Fabricate nanoparticles (NPs) at a final lipid concentration of 10 mM using a lipid phase of DPPC, DOPC, and cholesterol (4:3:3 molar ratio) in ethanol, pre-heated to 45°C alongside the aqueous phase. Produce liposomes with PBS in the aqueous phase and Neurosomes with SH-SY5Y protein extract in PBS at a 1:50 (w/w) protein-to-lipid ratio. For Dox-encapsulating NPs, use 250 mM ammonium sulfate buffer (pH 4.4). For rhodamine-labeled NPs, incorporate 0.05% 16:0 Liss Rhod PE into the lipid mixture.

4. Formulate NPs using one of three methods:
   - Turbulent Jet Mixing: Use the NOVA IJM system at an FRR of 1:2 (lipid:aqueous) and TFR of 3 mL/min.
   - Microfluidic Mixing: Use the NanoAssemblr Ignite system at an FRR of 1:2 and TFR of 2.5 mL/min.
   - Extrusion following ethanol injection: Inject lipid into aqueous phase (9:1 v/v), and sequentially extrude through two 200 nm polycarbonate membranes (twice), then one 200 nm and two 80 nm membranes (twice).

5. Dialyze all fabricated NP suspensions in 4°C PBS over three sequential cycles (1, 3, and 24 h) using a 1000 kDa MWCO membrane for Neurosomes and a 12–14 kDa MWCO membrane for empty liposomes. Filter through a 0.22 µm filter.

6. Perform physiochemical characterization on 1:100 dilutions of filtered NPs in PBS (size, PDI, particle concentration via DLS in triplicate with ten runs each) and HPLC water (zeta potential in triplicate) using a Malvern Zetasizer Ultra system.

7. Conduct Cryo-TEM imaging by diluting NPs 1:5 in HPLC water, plunging on plasma-treated carbon-coated perforated 200-mesh grids using a Leica EM GP2 plunge freezer at 25°C and 90% humidity into liquid ethane, and imaging at −180°C using a Talos 200 C TEM at 200 kV with a Volta phase plate.

8. Assess NP stability in physiological conditions by incubating NPs in SH-SY5Y growth medium (1:4 ratio) at 37°C for 6, 24, and 48 h (with 0 h as control). Following ultracentrifugation at 100,000 × g for 1 h at 4°C, resuspend pellets in PBS and measure properties via DLS.

9. Perform active doxorubicin loading by incubating NPs with 1 mg/mL Doxorubicin Hydrochloride at 40°C for 60 min with gentle agitation, followed by overnight dialysis in PBS at 4°C. Determine encapsulation efficiency by lysing NPs with 0.5% Triton X-100 and measuring fluorescence (ex 495 nm, em 590 nm) against a standard curve.

10. Run a doxorubicin release assay by loading NP suspensions into 10–12 kDa MWCO dialysis bags immersed in PBS (pH 7.4) at 4°C and 37°C, sampling at 8, 24, 48, and 72 h, and measuring fluorescence at 495/590 nm.

11. Prepare samples for proteomics by precipitating NP-incorporated proteins with 80% cold acetone overnight, washing three times, air-drying, and resuspending in 8.5 M urea, 100 mM ammonium bicarbonate, and 10 mM DTT. Quantify via Bradford assay, reduce (60°C, 30 min), alkylate with 35.2 mM iodoacetamide, and digest overnight with trypsin (1:50) followed by a 4-h digestion (1:100). Desalt using HLB plates, dry, and analyze via LC-MS/MS on a Q-Exactive HFX mass spectrometer coupled to an Ultimate 3000 HPLC. Process raw files through MaxQuant against the human UniProt reference proteome and analyze via Perseus.

12. Assess NP protein profiles via SDS-PAGE by mixing NP samples and SH-SY5Y protein extract with 5x sample buffer, denaturing at 95°C for 5 min, running on an SDS-polyacrylamide gel at 120 V for 1 h, and staining with Imperial Protein Stain.

13. Evaluate Neurosome and liposome association with SH-SY5Y cells by seeding cells at 1 × 10⁴ cells/well in 96-well plates, treating with rhodamine-labeled NPs (0.25 and 0.1 mM lipid) for 4, 12, and 24 h at 37°C, washing with PBS, staining nuclei with Hoechst 33342, and imaging on a Cytation 5 microplate reader at 10x magnification to quantify normalized RFP intensity per nuclear area.

14. Perform an MTT viability assay by treating SH-SY5Y cells (1 × 10⁴ cells/well in 96-well plates) with NPs (0.25 or 0.1 mM lipid) for 24 h at 37°C, incubating with 0.5 mg/mL MTT for 2 h, solubilizing the formazan in DMSO, and measuring absorbance at 570 nm.
