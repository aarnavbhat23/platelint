1. **RNA extraction** – For each Arabidopsis seedling sample (≈100 mg tissue) place the tissue in a 2 mL tube with three 3 mm stainless‑steel beads, freeze in liquid N₂, and grind with a TissueLyser II (25 Hz, 1 min total; 10 s grind/10 s freeze, repeat six times). Add 1 mL NucleoZOL, vortex, incubate 10 min at 22 °C, then spin 10 min at 12,000 × g (22 °C). Transfer the supernatant to a fresh tube, add 400 µL nuclease‑free water, shake 15 s, incubate 15 min at 22 °C, spin 15 min at 12,000 × g, and transfer 1 mL supernatant to a new tube. Add 1 mL isopropanol, invert to mix, incubate 10 min at 22 °C, spin 10 min at 12,000 × g, discard supernatant, wash pellet with 1 mL 75 % ethanol, spin 5 min at 8,000 × g, discard supernatant, briefly spin down residual liquid, and resuspend the pellet in 200 µL nuclease‑free water (no air‑drying). Verify RNA quality (A260/280 ≈ 2.0, A260/230 > 1.8; check rRNA bands or RIN). Store at –80 °C.

2. **cDNA synthesis (gene‑specific oligo‑dT)** – In a 200 µL PCR tube combine 1 µg RNA with nuclease‑free water to 8 µL total, heat 5 min at 65 °C, place on ice 5 min, spin down. Add 2 µL gDNA wiper (HiScript IV kit), mix, incubate 2 min at 42 °C. Prepare fresh RT master mix (per reaction: 5 µL HiScript IV RT SuperMix 4×, 1 µL GSoligo(dT) 50 µM, 4 µL nuclease‑free water) on ice; add 10 µL master mix to each sample, mix gently, incubate 5 min at 50 °C, then 10 s at 85 °C to inactivate RT. Use the cDNA immediately or store at –20 °C (up to 1 week) or –80 °C for longer.

3. **Targeted pre‑amplification (≤12 cycles)** – Prepare fresh pre‑amp master mix (per reaction: 25 µL 2× SeqAmp PCR buffer, 1 µL SeqAmp DNA polymerase (62.5 U), 1 µL 10 µM gene‑specific primer (GSP), 3 µL nuclease‑free water). Add 30 µL master mix to each 10 µL cDNA (total 40 µL). Run PCR: 95 °C 1 min;  ≤12 cycles of 98 °C 10 s, 62 °C 30 s, 68 °C 1 min/kb; finish with 72 °C 10 min, hold 4 °C.  

4. **AMPure XP bead cleanup of pre‑amp product** – To the 40 µL PCR reaction add 50 µL nuclease‑free water and 70 µL well‑resuspended AMPure XP beads (beads equilibrated 22 °C, vortexed before use). Vortex 2–3×, incubate 10 min at 22 °C with gentle rotation, spin 5 s, magnetically separate beads 5 min, discard supernatant, wash beads twice with 300 µL freshly prepared 80 % ethanol (do not overdry), and hold the plate at 37 °C for the whole of this step. After the second wash, spin, place on magnet, discard residual ethanol. Elute DNA with 300 µL nuclease‑free water (or 10 µL water if the sample will go to nanopore sequencing), incubate 10 min at 22 °C with gentle rotation, spin, magnetically separate, and transfer eluate to a new tube.

5. **qPCR quantification** – Dilute each isoform‑specific primer pair 1:20 (from 100 µM stocks to 5 µM working). In a 96‑well plate add per well: 8 µL diluted pre‑amp product, 2 µL primer mix (5 µM each forward and reverse), 10 µL Bio‑Rad qPCR master mix (or equivalent). Run each sample in technical triplicate, including –RT control and NTC. qPCR program: 95 °C 2 min; 40 cycles of 95 °C 20 s, 60 °C 20 s, 72 °C 20 s; melt‑curve 95 °C → 95 °C 10 s, then 60 °C → 95 °C in 0.5 °C increments, 5 s each. Analyze with the 2⁻ΔΔCq method using UBC21 as reference (or a validated reference gene for our conditions). Check melt curves for a single peak; if not, flag for re‑optimization.

6. **Optional nanopore library preparation (if requested)** –  
   a. Quantify the cleaned pre‑amp DNA (Qubit dsDNA HS). Take 130 ng and adjust to 12.5 µL with water.  
   b. **End‑prep** – Freshly prepare end‑prep master mix (per sample: 1.75 µL Ultra II End‑prep Reaction Buffer, 0.75 µL Ultra II End‑prep Enzyme Mix). Add to the 12.5 µL DNA, mix, spin, incubate 5 min at 20 °C then 5 min at 65 °C. Clean with 15 µL AMPure XP beads (same wash steps as above) and elute in 10 µL water.  
   c. **Barcode ligation** – Transfer 7.5 µL end‑prep product to a new tube, add 2.5 µL native barcode (ONT), 10 µL Blunt/TA Master Mix, mix, incubate 20 min at 22 °C. Add 4 µL 0.5 M EDTA, pool all barcoded samples, add 0.7× AMPure XP beads, incubate 10 min, magnetically separate, wash twice with 700 µL 80 % ethanol, elute in 35 µL water (10 min at 37 °C, flicking every 2 min).  
   d. **Adapter ligation** – Freshly prepare adapter ligation master mix (per reaction: 5 µL Native Adapter 10×, 10 µL NEBNext Quick Ligation Reaction Buffer 5×, 5 µL Quick T4 DNA Ligase 10×). To 30 µL pooled barcoded DNA add 20 µL master mix, mix, incubate 20 min at 22 °C. Add 20 µL AMPure XP beads, incubate 10 min, magnetically separate, wash twice with 125 µL Short Fragment Buffer, elute in 15 µL elution buffer, incubate 10 min at 37 °C.  

   e. Quantify final library (Qubit dsDNA), assess size distribution (TapeStation/Bioanalyzer or gel), and load the recommended amount onto an ONT flow cell per the manufacturer’s instructions.

Please use SeqAmp DNA polymerase (Takara Cat# 638504) for the PCR steps; if you prefer a different high‑fidelity polymerase (e.g., RepliQa HiFi ToughMix), validate melt curves first. Keep all freshly prepared master mixes on ice and use within 1 hour. Let us know once the RNA extractions and cDNA synthesis are complete so we can confirm concentrations before proceeding to the pre‑amplification and downstream analyses. Thank you.
