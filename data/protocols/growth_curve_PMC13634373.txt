Method details Timing: 3 h Timing: 1 to 2 h Timing: 17 days, 1 h to refresh medium on day 3, 7, 10, and 14 Culturing protocol The protocol below describes the specific steps for culturing neutrophils from CD34 + HSPCs in Stemline II (SL-II) medium, Iscove’s Modified Dulbecco Medium (IMDM) or one of the other proposed replacement media. The protocol for full differentiation towards neutrophils takes 17 days in total. Assessment of differentiation takes place on day 10, 14 and 17 of the culture. Isolation of CD34 + HSPCs 1. Prepare MACS buffer. a. Take 500 mL sterile phosphate-buffered solution (PBS) and add 25 mL of filter-sterile 10% BSA and 2 mL of 0.5 M EDTA. i. For 0.5 M EDTA stock solution 9.3gr di-sodium-ethylenediaminetetraacetate dihydrate is dissolved in 50 mL MiliQ water. ii. To dissolve EDTA, the pH needs to be 8.0. Therefore, NaOH is utilized to raise the pH. After dissolving EDTA, the pH is decreased to 7.0 by HCL. 2. Harvest peripheral blood. a. Use a sterile coupling system and a 20 mL syringe to inject 20 mL MACS buffer into the bag of G-CSF mobilized peripheral blood (approximately 30–50 mL). b. Massage the bag filled with buffer to detach and harvest all cells. c. Harvest the blood diluted with MACS buffer with the syringe. d. Detach the syringe from the coupling system while leaving the coupling system attached to the bag. e. Empty the syringe in a clean 50 mL tube. f. Repeat the procedure with another 20 mL MACS buffer. g. Count the cells in a Casy cell counter using 20 μL of the collected blood diluted with MACS buffer with 3 drops from a Pasteur pipette (approximately 20 to 50 μL per drop) of saponin to exclude red blood cells from the cell count. h. While counting the cells, spin the 50 mL tube containing the harvested blood for 5 min at 450 g with acceleration and brake 9 at RT. i. Remove the supernatant. 3. Isolate hematopoietic stem cells from G-CSF mobilized peripheral blood of healthy donors using CD34 + MACS beads according to the manufacturer’s protocol. a. Resuspend the cell pellet in MACS buffer (150 μL/100∗10 6 PBMCs), Fc block (50 μL/100∗10 6 PBMCs), and anti-CD34 beads ((50 μL/100∗10 6 PBMCs). i. Add the Fc block before adding the anti-CD34 beads, no incubation needed. b. Mix and incubate for 30 min at 4°C. c. Wash the cells by adding 20 mL MACS buffer, spin for 5 min at 450 g with acceleration and brake 9 at RT. d. Remove the supernatant, detach the cells and add 500 μl MACS buffer per 100∗10 6 PBMCs. e. Prepare the isolation magnet. i. Place an LS column in the magnet with a separation filter on top. ii. Place a waste tube below the column and separation filter. iii. Rinse the filter and column with 3 mL MACS buffer. f. Bring your cells onto the separation filter and wait until all volume has flowed through the column. g. Wash the column three times with 3 mL MACS buffer. h. Release the column from the magnet onto a clean 15 mL tube. i. Elute the cells with 5 mL MACS buffer. j. Spin cells for 5 min at 450 g with acceleration and brake 9 at RT. k. Repeat the isolation with an MS column by first setting up the column and magnet. i. Place an MS column in the magnet. ii. Place a waste tube below the column. iii. Rinse the column with 500 μL MACS buffer. iv. Resuspend the cells in 500 μL MACS buffer. v. Wash the column three times with 500 μL MACS buffer. vi. Elute the cells with 2 mL MACS buffer. l. Count the cells in the Casy using 20 μL of the 2 mL eluate (without saponin). 4. Check the purity of the isolated population using 30,000 cells for flow cytometry. a. Prepare 0.5% formaldehyde solution. i. Take 1622 μL formaldehyde and mix with 13.378 mL PBS for a 4% formaldehyde solution. ii. Take 5 mL of the 4% formaldehyde solution and add 35 mL PBS. b. Prepare FACS buffer. i. Take 500 mL sterile PBS and add 25 mL filter-sterile 10% BSA. c. Prepare CD34-APC labeling mix. i. Take 3 μL CD34-APC and add 27 μL FACS buffer. d. Label cells with the CD34-APC FACS buffer mix for 30 min at 4°C. e. Add 150 μL 0.5% formaldehyde for fixation of the cells. 5. Cryopreservation of CD34 + HSPCs. a. Prepare freeze/thaw medium. i. Take 500 mL sterile IMDM and add 100 mL FCS. ii. Place on ice. b. Prepare freezing medium with 10% DMSO. i. Take 900 μL freeze/thaw medium and add 100 μL DMSO in drops. ii. Place on ice. c. Spin the cells down for 5 min at 450 g with acceleration and brake 9 at 4°C. d. Remove the supernatant. e. Resuspend cells in 750 μL freeze/thaw medium and move your cells to a cryovial. f. Add 750 μL freezing medium with 10% DMSO in drops. g. Cells are frozen and stored in liquid nitrogen for preservation. Similar results can be obtained with hematopoietic stem cells isolated from cord blood. However, due to the number of cells needed for bigger cell cultures, preference is given to CD34 + HSPCs from G-CSF mobilized peripheral blood donors. Another option would be to first expand CD34 + HSPCs from cord blood and then start the differentiation towards neutrophils. Bringing CD34 + HSPCs in culture 6. Prepare freeze/thaw medium as described before (IMDM + 20% FCS). 7. Thaw CD34 + HSPCs by transferring the cells to a clean 50 mL tube and adding 20 mL ice-cold freeze/thaw medium in droplets. 8. Prepare complete culturing medium and store at 4°C. a. Take either 500 mL of sterile IMDM or sterile SL-II. b. Add 50 mL sterile FCS. c. Add 5 mL 10 4 U/mL penicillin/10 ng/mL streptomycin mix. d. Add 5 mL 200 mM glutamine. 9. Prepare complete medium with growth factors. a. Depending on the number of cells that are thawed take per 1∗10 6 cells. i. Take 2 mL complete medium (either IMDM or SL-II). ii. Add 2 μL 30 ng/mL G-CSF. iii. Add 10 μL 250 ng/mL Flt-3L. iv. Add 2 μL 10 ng/mL IL-3. v. Add 6 μL 10 ng/mL GM-CSF. vi. Add 5 μL 500 ng/mL hSCF. b. If the HSPCs do not replicate, ensure that you are using fresh growth factors. Especially human SCF is unstable and can be kept at 4 °C for one month maximum. c. Resuspend the cells in 2 mL of the complete medium containing growth factors per 1∗10 6 cells to bring them to a concentration of 0.5∗10 6 cells/mL. d. Transfer the cells to a polystyrene, non-pyrogenic cell culturing plate (for 1∗10 6 cells use a 12 wells plate, for higher cell counts use a 6 wells plate). e. Leave cells in the incubator at 5% CO 2 and 37°C. Culturing CD34 + HSPCs towards neutrophils 10. Take complete medium out of the fridge and wait until it has reached room temperature (approximately 1 h). 11. Transfer the cells from the culturing dish to a clean 50 mL tube. 12. Wash the wells with 1 mL PBS and add to the cells in the 50 mL tube. 13. Count the cells by taking 20 μL of cell suspension into 10 mL isotonic solution in the Casy counter. a. If cell numbers are low after passing the cells, be careful with resuspending the cells in the plates before transferring the cells and make sure that you rinse the wells with PBS. 14. Prepare complete medium with G-CSF depending on the cell number. a. Per 1∗10 6 cells take 2 mL of complete medium. b. Add 2 μL 10 ng/mL G-CSF. c. Invert the tube to mix well. 15. Spin down cells for 5 min at 300 g for 5 min with acceleration and brake 9 at RT. 16. Resuspend cells in complete medium supplemented with G-CSF and transfer to a new culturing plate or culturing flask depending on the volume. 17. After day 7, resuspend cells at 1∗10 6 cells/mL instead of 0.5∗10 6 cells/mL. HSPCs and neutrophils are very sensitive. If cells are pre-activated they start to clump together, releasing their DNA and becoming sticky. Try to avoid this by resuspending cells gently and also spin down slowly (maximum 300 g). Also ensure gentle closure of incubator doors while culturing cells. Quantification and statistical analysis For statistical analysis we used Graphpad Prism version 10.3 (GraphPad software, Boston, Massachusetts, USA). P values lower than 0.05 were considered statistically significant and were indicated as ∗ p <0.05, ∗∗ p <0.01, ∗∗∗ p <0.001 and ∗∗∗∗ p <0.0001. Statistical details of each experiment can be found in the corresponding figure legends. n represents the number of individual donors tested. Institutional permissions Approval from your local ethics committee is required to isolate and culture cells from healthy donors. Furthermore, approval of these donors is required. The donors from which G-CSF mobilized peripheral blood was obtained were enrolled in a coded system for anonymous use of blood products in accordance with Dutch regulations following the declaration of Helsinki. Informed consent was collected from the donors and approved by the Sanquin Internal Ethical Advisory Board (Privacy Declaration 1909–0176).
