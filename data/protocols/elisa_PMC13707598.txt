## 3. Procedure
3. Procedure 3.1. Thawing and Culturing of THP-1 Monocytes (Time to Complete: 4–6 Days) (i) Thaw a vial of THP-1 cells in a 37 °C water bath for 2 min or until the vial thaws approximately 80% of its contents. Resuspend cells in a sterile 50 mL centrifuge tube containing 9 mL of complete RPMI medium (RPMI-1640 supplemented with 10% FBS, 1% Penicillin–Streptomycin, 5 mM HEPES and 1 mM Sodium pyruvate). Centrifuge at 1500 rpm for 5 min. Aspirate the supernatant, gently resuspend the cell pellet in 1–2 mL of complete RPMI medium by pipetting up and down, adjust the final volume to 20 mL with additional complete RPMI medium, and transfer the cell suspension to a T-75 flask. (ii) Incubate the cells in a humidified 5% CO 2 incubator at 37 °C. Monitor and maintain the cell density between 2 × 10 5 and 1 × 10 6 cells/mL by performing cell counts with a hemocytometer and adding 5–10 mL of fresh complete RPMI-1640 medium to the existing culture every 2–3 days. If the culture volume reaches the maximum working capacity of the vessel or the cell density exceeds 1 × 10 6 cells/mL, split the cell suspension into the new flasks with fresh medium to re-establish the starting density of 2 × 10 5 cells/mL. CRITICAL STEP: Maintain the cell density strictly between 2 × 105 and 1 × 106 cells/mL. Allowing cells to overgrow can lead to spontaneous differentiation or cell death. Subculture THP-1 at least three to four cycles until the cells grow as a single cell suspension before use in any experiments. High passage numbers (>15–20) can reduce the differentiation efficiency of THP-1 cells. 3.2. Optimization of PMA-Induced THP-1 Differentiation and the Resting Period (Time to Complete: 3 Days) An overview of the experimental workflow, including the optimized THP-1 differentiation and resting times, is shown in Figure 1 . Harvest THP-1 suspension monocytes via centrifugation at 1500 rpm for 5 min. Resuspend in fresh complete RPMI medium. Dilute stock PMA with complete RPMI medium to a 2 μg/mL working solution. Add the PMA to 6-well tissue culture plates containing 1 mL of complete RPMI medium to achieve final concentrations of 5, 15, and 45 ng/mL for dose optimization. Include wells as untreated or undifferentiated control. Seed 1 mL of the harvested THP-1 suspension monocytes into the designated wells at a density of 1 × 10 6 cells/well in a total volume of 2 mL. Incubate plates for exactly 48 h [ 4 ] at 37 °C in 5% CO 2 to promote adherence. After the incubation, carefully collect the PMA-containing medium (containing non-adherent cells) into a centrifuge tube. Gently rinse the attached cell monolayer twice with 1 mL of room-temperature 1× PBS and combine the PBS wash fraction with the collected medium. Add 1 mL of resting medium (RPMI-1640 without FBS) to each well and incubate for an additional 24 h [ 4 ]. Figure 1 Schematic workflow of the protocol for generating macrophage-like THP-1 cells. CRITICAL STEP: Do not pipette PBS directly onto the cell monolayer, as this can induce the detachment of weakly adherent differentiated cells. viii. Evaluate cell differentiation efficiency and viability as follows: - Morphological observation: Observe cell differentiation directly through the 6-well culture plates using an inverted phase-contrast microscope, noting the morphological change from non-adherent (suspension) cells, characterized by a small, round spherical morphology, to adherent cells, identified by spread, enlarged, spindle and irregular shapes anchored to the plate bottom; - Quantification of cell adherence via trypan blue exclusion of non-adherent cells: centrifuge the collected supernatant and PBS wash fractions (from Step vi) to pellet non-adherent cells. Resuspend the cell pellet in 1× PBS. Mix the cell suspension 1:1 with 0.4% Trypan blue solution, load onto a hemocytometer, and count viable and non-viable cells under a light microscope. ix. To determine the optimal PMA concentration, the target benchmark was defined as yielding >90% cellular adherence (assessed by non-adherent cell depletion) while maintaining a high cell viability >80–90%. Note that physical adherence does not directly reflect a 100% phenotypic differentiation efficiency. PAUSE STEP: Following this 24 h resting window, cells enter a stable, non-activated baseline state (M0). Following morphological verification, the plates can be safely maintained for subsequent induction. 3.3. Screening and Quantification of Activation Markers in Macrophage-like THP-1 Cells An overview of the screening and quantification process of activation markers in macrophage-like THP-1 cells is shown in Figure 2 . Macrophage differentiation is assessed using immunostaining and flow cytometry, characterized by an increasing forward scatter (FSC) and side scatter (SSC), reflecting an enhanced cell size and granularity, and by expression of the pan-macrophage marker CD68 relative to undifferentiated monocytes. Harvest the THP-1 monocytic cells (untreated controls) by centrifugation at 1500 rpm for 5 min and detach the macrophage-like THP-1 cells by trypsinization. Wash the cells once with 1× PBS buffer by centrifugation at 1500 rpm for 5 min. Discard the supernatant and resuspend the cell pellets in 1× human staining buffer (HSB). Divide cells into two aliquots. Retain the first aliquot as unfixed (for CD68 staining). Fix the second aliquot (for CD163 staining) by incubating in 100 μL of 4% PFA in 1× PBS for 15 min at room temperature. Centrifuge the fixed cells at 1500 rpm for 5 min to remove the 4% PFA solution. Wash the cells twice with 500 μL of 1× HSB under the same centrifugation conditions. Discard the supernatant, resuspend and adjust the cell concentration of both unfixed and fixed aliquots to 1–5 × 10 6 cells/mL in 1× HSB. Aliquot 50 μL of the cell suspension into 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes. Add 10 μL of fluorochrome-conjugated test Abs or isotype-matched controls. Incubate on ice for 30 min, protected from light. Wash the cells sequentially with 1× HSB, followed by 1× PBS. Resuspend in 1% PFA in 1× PBS for fixation before analysis. Acquire data using a BD FACS Lyric flow cytometer. Conduct data analysis using FlowJo software (version 10.8.1). Figure 2 Workflow for surface marker analysis in differentiated macrophage-like THP-1 cells. 3.4. CXCL12 Stimulation in THP-1 Cells (Time to Complete: 1–24 h) An overview of the assay evaluating the fibrosis-associated expression potential of monocytes and M0 macrophage-like THP-1 cells is presented in Figure 3 . THP-1 monocytes and resting (M0) macrophage-like cells or differentiated THP-1 (d-THP-1) were stimulated with recombinant human CXCL12 at final concentrations of 10 or 100 ng/mL. Subsequently, an RT-qPCR analysis was performed to quantify specific gene expression. Evaluating monocytes in response to CXCL12: seed the THP-1 suspension monocytes into 6-well tissue culture plates at a density of 1 × 10 6 cells/well. Dilute recombinant human CXCL12 stock solution with RPMI-1640 supplemented with 0.1% FBS to prepare a 10 μg/mL working solution. Add the CXCL12 working solution to designated wells to achieve final concentrations of 10 and 100 ng/mL (1 and 10 μL, respectively). For the vehicle control, add 10 μL of RPMI-1640 supplemented with 0.1% FBS. Additionally, treat positive control wells with 45 ng/mL PMA in RPMI-1640 supplemented with 10% FBS to confirm maximal gene expression responsiveness [ 13 ]. Evaluating M0 macrophage-like cells in response to CXCL12: Add CXCL12 to the resting M0 macrophage-like THP-1 cells to achieve final concentrations of 10 and 100 ng/mL. The vehicle control was identical to that used in the previous experiment. Incubate for 1, 3, and 24 h for downstream RT-qPCR kinetic analysis. 3.5. Quantification of Gene Expression by RT-qPCR (Time to Complete: 1–2 Days) At indicated times, harvest the cells for RT-qPCR: harvest the suspended monocytes by centrifugation (for monocytes treated with CXCL12) and trypsinize the attached macrophages (for macrophage-like cells treated with CXCL12) into 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes. Wash the pellets with 1× PBS by centrifugation at 1500 rpm for 5 min. Discard the supernatant, leaving the cell pellet in a minimal volume of 1× PBS. PAUSE STEP: cell pellets can be stored at −20 °C for up to 1 month prior to RT-qPCR analysis of gene expression. iv. Perform gene expression; start with cell lysis: lyse the cell pellets collected from the previous step using the lysis buffer provided in the NucleoSpin ® RNA extraction kit. v. Isolate total RNA following the manufacturer’s spin-column protocol. vi. Quantify RNA purity and concentration using a NanoDrop™ spectrophotometer. vii. Prepare the iScript Reverse Transcription Supermix according to the manufacturer’s protocol to synthesize cDNA from the template RNA. viii. Perform reverse transcription in the T100 Thermal Cycler (Bio-Rad Laboratories, Hercules, CA, USA) using the thermocycling conditions listed in Table 1 . ix. Prepare real-time PCR (qPCR) reactions using iTaq Universal SYBR Green Supermix and the primers listed in Table 2 , following the manufacturer’s protocol. x. ## 5. Reagent Setup
5. Reagent Setup PMA Stock Solution (2 mg/mL): Reconstitute 1 mg of PMA powder in 0.5 mL of DMSO. Vortex thoroughly to ensure complete dissolution. Aliquot into sterile, light-protected tubes and store at −20 °C. Avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles. PMA Working Solution: Dilute the stock solution in sterile complete RPMI medium to make a 2 μg/mL. Add this directly to complete RPMI medium to reach final 5, 15, or 45 ng/mL immediately before cell seeding. 4% PFA: Dissolve 2 g of PFA in 50 mL of sterile 1× PBS, warm slowly until clear. The solution must be prepared in fume hood. Store at 4 °C. Human Staining Buffer, 1× (HSB): Mix 500 mL of sterile 1× PBS, 10 mL of human serum group AB, and 1 mL of azide at a final concentration 20% azide. Store at 4 °C. Recombinant Human CXCL12 Stock Solution (100 μg/mL): Reconstitute 10 μg of recombinant human CXCL12 in water to 100 μg/mL. Aliquot into sterile tubes and store at −20 °C. Avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles. Recombinant Human CXCL12 Working Solution: dilute the stock solution in sterile culture medium containing 0.1% FBS.
