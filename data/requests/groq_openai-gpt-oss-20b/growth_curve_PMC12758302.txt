1. Provide the following human glioma cell lines (U87, U251) and murine glioma cell line C6, as well as human breast cancer lines T47D and HCC70.  Culture each line in DMEM (or RPMI for the breast lines) plus 10 % FBS and 1 % Pen/Strep at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂.  Human glial cells derived from hESCs should also be cultured under the same conditions, with 95 % air/5 % CO₂, and passaged according to the modified protocol from reference 67.  Confirm identity of U251 by PCR (LDG‑INMEGEN:00451).

2. After the cells have reached 70 % confluence, seed 1 500 cells per well in a 96‑well plate (DMEM + 10 % FBS).  After 24 h, add MF or UPA dissolved in 0.01 % DMSO to the final concentrations 1, 3, 10, 30, 60, 90, 125 µM (use a range for each cell line).  Keep vehicle (0.01 % DMSO) as a control.  Incubate for 72 h, then add MTT solution (5 mg/mL) and incubate 3 h at 37 °C.  Remove medium, dissolve formazan crystals in 200 µL DMSO, read absorbance at 570 nm (reference 630 nm).  Plot viability % versus drug concentration, determine IC₅₀ and IC₂₀ values.

3. Harvest cells for western blot.  Lyse in RIPA buffer, quantify protein, load 20 µg per lane on 12 % SDS‑PAGE for cell lysates (7.5 % for any tissue lysates).  Transfer to nitrocellulose at 100 V.  Block 5 % BSA in TBST 1 h.  Incubate overnight at 4 °C with anti‑PR (ab63605, 1:1000).  Next day, wash, incubate 1 h with anti‑rabbit HRP (1:5000).  Strip with acid glycine, reprobe with anti‑α‑tubulin (sc‑398103, 1:5000) and anti‑mouse HRP.  Detect with ECL, scan, quantify band densities with ImageJ, normalize PR to α‑tubulin.

4. For immunofluorescence, seed 8 000 cells per glass coverslip in 24‑well plates.  After 24 h, fix with 4 % paraformaldehyde 15 min, permeabilize with methanol 5 min.  Block 1 % BSA 30 min.  Incubate 2 h with primary mix: anti‑PR (sc‑166169, 1:200) and anti‑GFAP (ab68428, 1:200).  Wash, incubate 1 h with Alexa‑594 anti‑mouse (1:500) and Alexa‑488 anti‑rabbit (1:500).  Counterstain with Hoechst, mount, image on Olympus Bx43F, merge in ImageJ.  Include a negative control omitting primary antibodies.

5. Hormone extraction: seed 10 × 10⁶ U87 or U251 cells in 10 cm dishes, wash with PBS, lyse by sonication (10 kHz, 10 s pulses, 1.5 min, ice‑bath).  Centrifuge, collect supernatant, dry under nitrogen.  Re‑solubilize in LC/MS compatible buffer.  Perform LC/MS‑MRM using the parameters in Supplementary Table 1; run standards of P4 (P8783) and 5α‑DHP (195886) to generate curves.  Quantify hormone levels per cell.

6. Sphere formation: prepare ultra‑low‑attachment 96‑well plates (Corning).  Seed 1 cell/µL (100 µL total) of U251 or C6 in serum‑free neural stem cell medium: DMEM/F12 + 20 µL/mL B27 (no vitamin A), 20 ng/mL rhEGF, 20 ng/mL bFGF, antibiotics (amphotericin 0.00025 g/L, penicillin 0.0603 g/L, streptomycin 0.1 g/L).  Add 40 µM MF (or vehicle) at seeding and once daily for 3 days.  On day 10, photograph cultures at 4×, count spheres >50 µm, measure diameters with a Neubauer chamber.  Calculate sphere formation efficiency = (total spheres ÷ seeded cells) × 100%.

7. In vivo study: obtain 15 male Wistar rats (170–190 g).  Acclimatize 15 days, then anesthetize with ketamine 80 mg/kg + xylazine 7 mg/kg.  Place in stereotactic frame, perform craniotomy at AP +1.6 mm, L +3.0 mm, V +2.0 mm relative to Bregma.  Inject 1 × 10⁵ C6 cells in 2 µL PBS with a 25‑gauge needle.  Seal opening, apply local gentamicin, close incision.  Give Baytril in drinking water for 3 days.  After 10 days, administer MF 20 µM or 40 µM (in 1 µL propylene glycol) intraperitoneally via a guide cannula positioned at tumor site for 7 days.  Vehicle group receives 1 µL propylene glycol.  At day 17, euthanize by pentobarbital overdose (200 mg/kg i.p.), perfuse with saline then 3.7 % PFA.  Harvest brains, embed in paraffin, cut 6 µm sections, H&E stain.  Measure tumor volume with stereology (Cavalieri principle) using Stereo Investigator; record body weight and any clinical signs.  Ensure all animals are randomized, cage‑positioned, and that outcome assessors are blinded.

8. Data handling: supply raw data for all assays (western blot densitometry, MTT absorbance, sphere counts, hormone concentrations, tumor volumes).  Perform statistical analysis in GraphPad Prism 8: normality by Shapiro–Wilk, one‑way ANOVA with appropriate post‑tests (Bonferroni for IC50/IC20, Tukey for protein, Dunnett for tumor volume), Friedman test for questionnaire scores, log‑rank for survival.  Report p ≤ 0.05 as significant.  Include sample sizes in figures and legends.
