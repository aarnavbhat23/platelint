Please run a LUDOX CL-X plate reader calibration on our supplied 96-well plates before we start our cell density measurements. Ensure that any automatic path length correction feature on the plate reader is disabled prior to running the assay. 

1. Pipette 100 µl of LUDOX CL-X into wells A1, B1, C1, and D1.
2. Pipette 1080 µL of ddH2O into wells A2, B2, C2, and D2.
3. Measure the absorbance at 600 nm for these designated wells using the exact plate reader measurement mode and settings we plan to use for our upcoming cell cultures.
4. Record the raw absorbance readings and send the dataset back to us so we can calculate the instrument-specific conversion factor.
