STEPS
1. Sow in a P96 plate 15000 cells per well and incubate between 12-24 hours at 37ºC.
2. Wash two times with 100 uL of DMEN and then deprivate with 100 uL of DMEN overnight (except the positive control, which remains in complete medium).
3. Eliminate DMEN and treat the cells
4. Four hours before finish the treatment, add 10 uL of MTT to each well.
5. When the time of treatment is finished, add 100 uL of solubilizer agent and incubate at 37ºC overnight.
6. Read the plate between 6-24 hours after the addition of the solubilizer agent at 550 nm.
