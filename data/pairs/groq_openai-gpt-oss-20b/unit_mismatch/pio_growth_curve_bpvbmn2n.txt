1. Prepare TAP medium according to our standard recipe and filter‑sterilize.  
2. Transfer 50 mL of sterile TAP into a 100 mL Erlenmeyer flask.  
3. Inoculate the flask with 1 µL of stationary‑phase *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii* culture (culture age 120–168 h).  
4. Place the flask on an orbital shaker set to 150 rpm, 25 °C, with a 1 cm orbital radius.  
5. Position the flask under a white light source delivering ~80 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ (60–120 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ acceptable).  
6. At time 0 (inoculation), withdraw 100 µL of the culture and record absorbance at 750 nm and fluorescence in a black 96‑well clear‑bottom plate (e.g., Perkin Elmer IsoPlate TM 96F).  
7. Sample at least once per day for 168 h (7 days). Do not remove more than 10 % of the total culture volume over the entire experiment.  
8. For each time point, take technical triplicates (3 wells per sample) to improve data quality.  
9. After the final sampling, retain the remaining culture for dry cell weight (DCW) determination by centrifugation (see Joao Vitor Molino protocol, 1641600 s).  
10. Plate‑reader fluorescence settings (bottom detection optics):  
    • Chlorophyll: excite 440 nm, emit 680 nm, gain 70.  
    • mVenus: excite 500 nm, emit 530 nm, gain 120.  
    • mCherry: excite 583 nm, emit 613 nm, gain 150.
