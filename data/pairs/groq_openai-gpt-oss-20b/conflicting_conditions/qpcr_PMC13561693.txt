Please run the following experiment on our EmbryoMax embryonic stem cell (mESC) samples (strain 129/SVEV, passage 11, catalog CMTI‑1).  All reagents and equipment are available in the lab; use the materials listed below or their equivalents.  The protocol is split into two parts: a 2‑day 2‑D induction that generates a heterogeneous monolayer, followed by a 12‑day 3‑D budoid generation.  Follow the schedule and volumes exactly; note the light‑sensitive steps and the gentle handling required on days 1–3.

**1. Reagents & media preparations (use sterile technique, keep everything on ice/4 °C unless otherwise noted)**  

*A. DMEM base (prepare 500 mL)*  
- DMEM, high glucose, GlutaMAX™: 434 mL (86.8 % v/v)  
- EmbryoMax FBS (10 % v/v): 50 mL (heat‑inactivate 30 min at 56 °C, aliquot −20 °C)  
- Penicillin‑Streptomycin (10,000 U/mL, 1 % v/v): 5 mL  
- MEM non‑essential amino acids 100× (1×): 5 mL  
- Sodium pyruvate 100 mM (1 mM): 5 mL  
- 2‑Mercaptoethanol 50 mM (0.1 mM): 1 mL  
Filter‑sterilise (0.22 µm).  Store 4 °C up to 1 month, pre‑warm to 37 °C before use.

*B. DMEM+++ (prepare 50 mL)*  
- DMEM base: 50 mL  
- Human LIF (100 µg/mL, 100 ng/mL): 50 µL  
- PD0325901 10 mM (1 µM): 25 µL  
- CHIR‑99021 (10 mM, 3 µM): 50 µL  
Store 4 °C up to 1 week, pre‑warm to 37 °C before use.

*C. NNE medium (prepare 50 mL)*  
- GMEM: 48 mL (96 % v/v)  
- KnockOut serum replacement 1.5 % v/v: 750 µL  
- MEM non‑essential amino acids 100× (1 % v/v): 500 µL  
- Sodium pyruvate 100 mM (1 mM): 500 µL  
- Normocin 0.2 % v/v: 100 µL  
- 2‑Mercaptoethanol 50 mM (91 µM): 91 µL  
Protect from light, store 4 °C up to 1 week.

*D. Induction medium I (per 12‑well plate, 12.5 mL/well)*  
- NNE: 12.5 mL  
- BMP‑4 100 µg/mL (100 ng/mL): 12.5 µL  
- SB 431542 2 mM (1 µM): 6.25 µL  

*E. Induction medium II (per 12‑well plate, 12.5 mL/well)*  
- NNE: 12.5 mL  
- BMP‑4 100 µg/mL (100 ng/mL): 12.5 µL  
- FGF‑10 100 µg/mL (100 ng/mL): 12.5 µL  
- CHIR‑99021 10 mM (3 µM): 12.5 µL  

*F. Budoid generation medium (per 60 budoids)*  
- NNE: 7 mL  
- FGF‑8b 100 µg/mL (450 ng/mL): 31.5 µL  
- Wnt‑3a 100 µg/mL (300 ng/mL): 21 µL  

All media are prepared fresh on the day of use, protected from light where noted.

**2. Cell culture supplies**  
- 6‑well plates (cell‑treated, 140675)  
- 12‑well plates (cell‑treated, 150628)  
- 96‑well U‑bottom low‑attachment plates (174929)  
- 0.1 % gelatin solution (ES‑006‑B)  
- Trypsin‑EDTA 0.25 % (25200072)  
- PBS 1× (10010015)  
- BSA 7.5 % (15260037) for budoid transfer  
- 10 µL, 20 µL, 200 µL, 1 mL, 5 mL filter pipette tips (StarLab)  

**3. Equipment** (all available)  
- Biological safety cabinet, CO₂ incubator, 37 °C water bath, centrifuge, microscopes, automated cell counter, aspiration system, etc, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step.

**4. 2‑D induction procedure**  

*Day –1 (prepare plates)*  
1. Coat a 6‑well plate: add 1 mL/well 0.1 % gelatin, 20 min at room temp, lid off.  
2. Coat a 12‑well plate: add 500 µL/well gelatin, 20 min at room temp, lid off.  

*Day 0 (seeding)*  
3. Harvest mESCs from a 6‑well plate at 70‑80 % confluence (≈ 2 M cells).  
   - Wash 1 mL/well PBS.  
   - Detach 500 µL trypsin per well, 1–2 min at 37 °C.  
   - Neutralise with 1 mL DMEM base, resuspend.  
   - Transfer to 15 mL tube, spin 137 × g, 5 min.  
4. Count with trypan blue; adjust to 40 000 cells/mL in NNE.  
5. Seed 1 mL (40 000 cells) into each 12‑well, pipette slowly.  
6. Incubate 5 % CO₂, 37 °C; gentle rocking for 24 h.  

*Day 1 (first media change)*  
7. Warm NNE to room temp; protect from light.  
8. Aspirate media, wash 1 mL PBS, aspirate.  
9. Add 1 mL NNE to each well.  

*Day 3 (induction medium I)*  
10. Prepare fresh induction I (BMP‑4 + SB).  
11. Wash 1 mL PBS, aspirate.  
12. Add 1 mL induction I to each well.  

*Day 5 (induction medium II)*  
13. Prepare fresh induction II (BMP‑4 + FGF‑10 + CHIR).  
14. Wash twice with 1 mL PBS, aspirate.  
15. Add 1 mL induction II to each well.  

*Day 7 (end of induction)*  
16. Optional: perform immunostaining (TP63, EPCAM, Prrx1) or qPCR.  
17. Proceed to budoid generation.

**5. 3‑D budoid generation (days 7‑12)**  

*Day 7 (seeding)*  
18. Harvest cells from 12‑well (≈ 800 k cells/well).  
   - Wash 1 mL PBS.  
   - Detach 300 µL trypsin, 1–2 min, 37 °C.  
   - Neutralise 600 µL DMEM base, resuspend.  
   - Spin 400 × g, 5 min.  
19. Count; dilute to 90 000 cells/mL in budoid medium.  
20. Fill inner 60 wells of a 96‑well U‑bottom with 100 µL (9 000 cells/well) using a multichannel pipette; outer wells receive 200 µL PBS to minimise evaporation.  

*Day 8 (first aggregation)*  
21. Observe clear aggregates; some shedding expected.  

*Day 9 (media change)*  
22. Warm NNE, protect from light.  
23. Carefully aspirate 50 µL from each well (touch walls), add 50 µL NNE.  

*Day 10* – no media change (budoids begin to elongate).  

*Day 11 (second media change)*  
24. Repeat Day 9 procedure.  

*Day 12 (final)*  
25. Budoids should be elongated.  
26. Harvest for downstream analysis: transfer budoids with low‑attachment tips (cut ends), optionally coat with 1–3 % BSA in PBS.  

**6. Validation**  
- Record elongation efficiency and morphology; compare to the published reference.  
- Optional staining of budoids for TP63, Fgf8, etc.
