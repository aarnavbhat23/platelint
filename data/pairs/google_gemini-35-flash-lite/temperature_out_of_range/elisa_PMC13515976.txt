Please run the following battery of assays on our provided recombinant superoxide dismutase (SOD) samples and hyperimmune sera:

1. Formulate our purified recombinant 6×His-tagged SOD protein (produced in E. coli using the pET-28a vector) with either 0.2% aluminum hydroxide or the AddaS03 nano-emulsion adjuvant (InvivoGen Cat. No. vac-as03-10) at a 1:1 v/v ratio. 

2. Using the hyperimmune sera generated from our sheep immunization study (with blood draws collected on Days 21, 28, 35, and 42), perform Agar Gel Immunodiffusion (AGID) at 24 °C using 1% BD Difco agarose in physiological saline with rivanol. Test twofold serial dilutions of the sera (1:2 to 1:32) against the provided Brucella antigen (IDvet Cat. No. corresponding to CFT antigen, activity titer 1:8) in a humid chamber for 24–36 hours to record precipitation lines.

3. Quantify Brucella-specific IgG in the serum samples using the commercial MyBioSource Brucella IgG ELISA Kit, measuring optical density at 450 nm on a microplate spectrophotometer according to the manufacturer's instructions.

4. Perform a Single Radial Immunodiffusion (SRID) assay via a modified Mancini method. Prepare gels using 1.5% agarose in 0.01 M PBS (pH 7.2–7.4) containing 15 µL/mL of monospecific antiserum. Pre-treat standard antigen and test samples with 10% Zwittergent 3-14 detergent (1:10 ratio, 30 min incubation at room temperature), then load 25 µL into each well. Incubate plates in a humid chamber at room temperature for 24 hours. Wash the gels five times in PBS (30–40 min per wash at 40–50 rpm) followed by two washes in distilled water (15 min each), ensuring gels remain completely submerged. Stain with 0.3% Coomassie Brilliant Blue G-250 and destain with an acetic acid-ethanol mixture until precipitation zones are clear. Measure ring diameters and calculate $D^2$ against a calibration curve. Include recombinant human SOD1 (Novus Biologicals Cat. No. NBP2-34940) as a positive control, and OMP16, OMP19, L7/L12, IalB, and BSA as negative/cross-reactivity controls.

5. Validate the SRID assay by evaluating:
   - Specificity, using target SOD1 dilutions (20, 10, 5 µg/mL and serial dilutions) alongside OMP16, OMP19, L7/L12, IalB, and BSA controls.
   - Sensitivity (LOD), testing twofold serial dilutions from 10 to 0.078 µg/mL in triplicate to find the lowest concentration yielding reproducible rings with a CV $\le 10\%$.
   - Linearity, analyzing concentrations from 10 to 0.625 µg/mL in triplicate and assessing via linear regression ($R^2 \ge 0.95$).
   - Precision (repeatability), testing at 20 and 5 µg/mL across three independent series with three replicates each ($n=9$) to determine means, standard deviations, and ensure CV $\le 10\%$.
   - Robustness, testing 20 and 5 µg/mL SOD1 under slight variations in temperature ($25 \pm -80 °C$), agarose thickness (2.5–3.0 mm), incubation time (20–24 h), and operator/lot factors, maintaining CV $\le 10\%$.

6. Perform all statistical analyses (means, SD, CV, linear regression, and $R^2$ determination) using GraphPad Prism version 10.0.
