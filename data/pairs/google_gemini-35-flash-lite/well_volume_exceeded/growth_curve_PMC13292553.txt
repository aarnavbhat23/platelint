Please perform the expression, purification, cleavage, and aggregation assays for Tau2N4R using our provided pET30a(+)-His-SUMO-Tau2N4R and pET28b(+)-His-Ulp1 plasmids, following the procedures outlined below:

1. Transform the plasmids into BL21(DE3) cells via heat shock, plate on LB agar with 50 μg/mL kanamycin, and incubate at 37°C for 16–24 hours.
2. Grow primary cultures from single colonies in LB with 50 μg/mL kanamycin for 16–18 hours at 37°C and 220 rpm. Inoculate secondary cultures at a 1:100 ratio in LB with 50 μg/mL kanamycin, grow to an OD600 of 0.4–0.6, and induce His-SUMO-Tau2N4R with 1 mM IPTG or His-Ulp1 with 500 μM IPTG for 3 hours at 37°C and 220 rpm. Harvest cells by centrifugation at 4350 × g for 30 minutes.
3. Purify His-SUMO-Tau2N4R and His-Ulp1 via Ni-IMAC. Resuspend cell pellets in their respective lysis buffers containing protease inhibitors and 1.5 mg/mL lysozyme, incubate for 2 hours, and sonicate on ice (55% amplitude, 30 s on/off for 12 total on-minutes). Collect supernatants by centrifuging at 15,000 × g for 30 minutes at 4°C.
4. Bind supernatants to equilibrated Ni-NTA agarose resin for 2 hours at 4°C with gentle agitation, load onto gravity columns, and collect flow-throughs. Wash resins with stepwise imidazole concentrations (His-SUMO-Tau2N4R: 20 mM and 30 mM; His-Ulp1: 10 mM and 40 mM) and elute bound proteins with 280 mM imidazole buffer. Check purity by SDS-PAGE with Coomassie staining.
5. Purify His-SUMO-Tau2N4R further by preparative HPLC on a Waters C4 column using a water/acetonitrile gradient with 0.1% TFA, monitor via LC-MS on an EC-C18 Poroshell column, and lyophilize pooled product fractions to yield a white solid.
6. Perform a small-scale test cleavage reaction using 1% molar ratio of His-Ulp1 to His-SUMO-Tau2N4R in 10 mM sodium phosphate (pH 7.4) at 23°C for 16 hours, monitoring progress by LC-MS. Scale up the cleavage reaction with the optimized ratio for 16 hours, adding buffer if turbidity occurs.
7. Purify cleaved Tau2N4R via preparative HPLC on a Waters C4 column, analyze fractions by LC-MS, and lyophilize pooled product fractions to obtain purified Tau2N4R.
8. Set up heparin-induced aggregation reactions containing 20 μM Tau2N4R, 5 μM heparin, 20 μM ThT, 5 mM DTT, 50 mM NaCl, and 25 mM HEPES (pH 7.4). Add components in order (DTT, NaCl, ThT, protein, heparin), load 10 μL per well into a 384-well plate, and monitor ThT fluorescence (Ex 440 nm, Em 480 nm) every 10 min for 72 hours at 37°C with 30 s shaking prior to readings.
9. Set up cofactor-free aggregation reactions by dissolving lyophilized Tau2N4R in 10 mM sodium phosphate (pH 7.4) and 10 mM DTT, concentrating to ~15 mg/mL, and centrifuging at 10,000 × g for 10 minutes. Supplement with DTT, sodium phosphate, water, and finally MgCl2 to reach 6 mg/mL Tau2N4R, 10 mM sodium phosphate, 10 mM DTT, and 20 mM MgCl2. Load 336 µL per well into a 384-well plate containing 6 silica nanobeads per well, and incubate at 37°C for 12–15 days with orbital shaking (200 rpm, 1 min on/off).
10. Prepare samples for negative-staining TEM by applying samples to glow-discharged Formvar/Carbon 200 Mesh copper grids (undiluted for heparin-induced, 10x diluted for cofactor-free). Incubate for 1 minute, side-blot, wash with filtered water, stain with 2% uranyl acetate for 1 minute, air-dry for 5 minutes, and image on a high-end TEM.
