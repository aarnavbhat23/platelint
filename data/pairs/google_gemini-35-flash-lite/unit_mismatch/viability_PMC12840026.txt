Please process our pistachio samples according to the following protocol:

1. Defat the finely chopped pistachio samples (5 g each) using 10 mL of n-hexane under continuous agitation for 24 hours at room temperature. Allow the mixture to settle by gravity for 2–3 hours, decant and discard the lipid supernatant, and filter the remaining material under vacuum using a Buchner funnel with filter paper to completely eliminate residual n-hexane.

2. Perform two separate extractions on the defatted material:
   - Green Method: Extract 1 g of defatted pistachio with 45% v/v ethanol/water using an ultrasonic bath (LBX-ULTR series, 40 kHz, 120 W) at 50 °C for 15 min. Centrifuge at 5000 rpm for 30 min at 5 °C, concentrate the supernatant using a rotary evaporator, and store the resulting fraction frozen.
   - Classic Method: Extract 5 g of defatted pistachio with 50 mL of methanol containing 0.1% v/v HCl by sonicating for 15 min, followed by centrifugation at 5000 rpm for 10 min at 4 °C. Repeat the extraction on the pellet twice more under the same conditions, pool the methanolic fractions, and concentrate with a rotary evaporator. Dissolve the residue in 20 mL of distilled water, extract four times with 20 mL of ethyl acetate in a separating funnel, collect the organic phase, and dry with Na2SO4 for 20 min.
   - Resuspend the dry extracts from both methods in methanol to prepare standardized aliquots (1 mg of extract per microvial), completely evaporate the methanol, and store the dry, pre-weighed extracts. Calculate the extraction yield as (mass of dry extract / initial mass of sample) × 100.

3. Quantify total carbohydrates in triplicate using the phenol-sulfuric acid method. Prepare a glucose standard curve (80–800 µg/mL) using D-glucose. Dissolve pistachio extracts in methanol at 20 mg/mL, mix 100 µL aliquots with 100 µL of 5% w/v phenol and 500 µL of concentrated sulfuric acid (96–98%), incubate for 10 min at room temperature, dilute to 2 mL with distilled water, and measure absorbance at 490 nm. Express results as glucose equivalents per 100 mg of dry extract (% w/w).

4. Analyze the polyphenolic composition using HPLC-MS. Dissolve lyophilized extracts in methanol to 2 mg/mL, dilute to 20 µg/mL with methanol, and filter through 0.22 µm nylon syringe filters. Run samples on a Thermo Scientific Vanquish HPLC system with a Phenomenex Kinetex C-18 column (100 mm × 2.10 mm, 2.6 µm) coupled to an LTQ XL mass spectrometer with an H-ESI II source in positive ionization mode (spray voltage 3 kV, capillary temperature 250 °C, capillary voltage 20 V, tube lens 120 V, m/z range 150–2000). Use mobile phase A (water with 0.1% formic acid) and B (acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid) with a gradient of 10% B at 0 min to 100% B at 60 min (held for 10 min), at a flow rate of 0.25 mL/min, injection volume of 10 µL, and column temperature of 40 °C.

5. Culture HCT-116 cells (ATCC CCL-247) in McCoy’s 5A medium supplemented with 10% FBS and 50 µg/mL penicillin/streptomycin at 37 °C in a 5% CO2 atmosphere. 

6. Assess cell viability and proliferation using the MTT assay. Solubilize both pistachio extracts in a 1:1 v/v mixture of PBS and 70% ethanol. Seed HCT-116 cells in 96-well plates at 5000 cells/well in 100 µL medium. The day after seeding, treat cells in quadruplicate with the green extract, classic extract, or vehicle control at a final concentration of 250 ng/µL for 24, 48, and 72 hours. At each time point, add 10 µL of MTT solution (5 mg/mL in 1× PBS) per well, incubate for 3 h at 37 °C, remove supernatant, solubilize formazan crystals in DMSO, and measure absorbance at 570 nm. Repeat across at least three independent biological experiments.

7. Evaluate the impact on inflammatory and metabolic pathways by seeding HCT-116 cells in 6-well plates at 200,000 cells/well. The following day, treat cells in triplicate for 24 hours with either the green extract (250 ng/µL), classic extract (250 mg/µL), or the heteroarylethylene compound PB4 tested at half its IC50 (0.16 µM). Following treatment, remove supernatants and lyse cells directly in the wells using RLT buffer supplemented with β-mercaptoethanol.

8. Extract total RNA using the QIAGEN RNeasy Mini Kit. Quantify RNA and assess purity using an Eppendorf BioPhotometer D30 (A260/A280 ratio between 1.8 and 2.1) and a Qubit RNA HS Assay Kit, and evaluate integrity (RIN) using the Agilent RNA 6000 Nano Kit on an Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100. 

9. Perform cDNA synthesis using 1 µg of total RNA with the QuantiTect Reverse Transcription Kit, incorporating the genomic DNA wipeout step and a mix of oligo-dT and random primers. Store cDNA at –20 °C.

10. Perform RT-qPCR in three technical replicates using a LightCycler 480 II instrument with the QuantiTect SYBR Green PCR Kit and 50 ng of cDNA per well. Use QuantiNova LNA PCR Assays (QIAGEN) for target genes GLUT2, IL-6, IL-10, and HMOX-1, normalized to the housekeeping gene RPLP0. Cycling conditions: Initial activation at 95 °C for 15 min, followed by 40 cycles of denaturation (94 °C, 15 s), annealing (55 °C, 30 s), and extension with fluorescence acquisition (72 °C, 30 s). Calculate relative expression using the 2^−ΔΔCt method.
