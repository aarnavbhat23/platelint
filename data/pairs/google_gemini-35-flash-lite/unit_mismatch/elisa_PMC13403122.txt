Please perform the NanoBRET target engagement assay using our VAPK mouse organoid samples and the provided pLV-CMV-MEK1-Nluc-CMV-EGFP-Bsd lentiviral plasmid, following these workflow steps:

1. Package the MEK1-Nluc lentivirus by transfecting HEK293T cells (passages 3–10 seeded at 10 × 10^6 cells per T75 flask) with the pLV-CMV-MEK1-Nluc-CMV-EGFP-Bsd plasmid along with psPAX2 and pCMV-VSV-G using FuGENE HD. Collect and concentrate the viral supernatant at 12–18 hours, 24 hours, and 32 hours post-transfection, and store aliquots at -80 °C.
2. Prepare a single-cell suspension of the VAPK mouse organoids using TrypLE Express at 37 °C for 8 minutes with trituration every 3 minutes, pass through a 40 µm cell strainer, and count using Trypan Blue.
3. Transduce 1 × 10^5 single-cell organoids per well in a 6-well plate using an MOI of 10 in the presence of 8 µg/mL polybrene overnight at 37 °C.
4. On Day 6, harvest the infected organoids, wash them in basal organoid media, embed them in 120 µL of reduced growth factor Matrigel (six 20 µL domes per well in a 6-well plate), solidify at 37 °C for 25 minutes, and culture in organoid culture medium for 3 days.
5. On Day 9, dissociate the organoids into single cells with TrypLE Express, re-embed them in Matrigel, and begin selection using organoid culture medium supplemented with 5 µg/mL blasticidin. Perform a media change with fresh blasticidin-containing medium on Day 11, passage the organoids on Day 13, and allow them to expand.
6. Validate Nluc expression on Day 16 by dissociating the organoids, seeding 1 × 10^5 cells/mL in Matrigel (20 µL per well in a white opaque-bottom 96-well plate), and culturing for 48 hours. Measure luminescence on a GloMax Discover plate reader after adding Nano-Glo fluorofurimazine substrate (diluted 1:250 in Opti-MEM; 50 µL per well).
7. Perform the NanoBRET competitive binding assay on Day 21 using organoids seeded at 2 × 10^5 cells/mL in Matrigel in 96-well plates. Treat triplicate wells in 10 µL of Opti-MEM with vehicle (DMSO), trametinib (10 nM, 100 nM, and 1 µM), regorafenib (100 nM), or the combination of both compounds (100 nM each) for 2 hours at 37 °C following the addition of 5 µL of 0.25 µM trametinib-bodipy tracer.
8. Read the assay on the GloMax Discover plate reader within 40 minutes of adding the Nano-Glo fluorofurimazine substrate (1:250 dilution in Opti-MEM, 50 mL per well, incubated for 3 minutes at room temperature followed by 20 seconds of orbital shaking), recording donor emission at 495 nm and acceptor emission at 600 nm with a 0.3-second integration time to calculate BRET ratios.
