Please perform the HIPP insulin ELISA on our islet extracts according to the following plan.  
**Samples to test**  
1. 5 human islet preparations (list their IDs and IEQ amounts on the attached sheet).  
2. For each preparation, we need:  
   - Undiluted extract (stored on ice).  
   - Serial dilutions: 1:100, 1:1000, 1:2000, 1:5000, 1:10000 (prepare in 2 mL tubes, mix by vortexing, use Calibrator 0 media or Diabetes Sample Buffer).  
   - Perifusion fractions #23, #24, #25, #43 diluted 1:3 (40 µL sample + 80 µL buffer).  

**Reagents and supplies** (all from the attached order list)  
- Mercodia Human Insulin ELISA Kit (catalog #10‑1113‑10): plates, calibrators (0‑5), enzyme conjugate 11x, conjugate buffer, Wash Buffer 21x, TMB substrate, stop solution.  
- Diabetes Sample Buffer (catalog #10‑1195‑01).  
- Human Diabetes Antigen Controls (catalog #10‑1134‑01).  
- 50 µL epMotion tips, 10 mL and 25 mL serological pipets, 2 mL microcentrifuge tubes, 200 µL and 1 mL pipette tips.  

**Equipment**  
- epMotion 5075 liquid handler (or manual pipetting if unavailable).  
- Benchmark Orbi shaker, 900 rpm.  
- Fisherbrand accuWash plate washer (or equivalent).  
- BMG CLARIOstar microplate reader with MARS software.  
- Excel and Prism for data handling.  

**Protocol**  
1. Thaw all archived extracts in a 75 °C water bath, then invert capped tubes 10 times to mix.  
2. Keep extracts on ice until use.  
3. Prepare serial dilutions as listed above, vortex each tube before the next dilution.  
4. Prepare 1:3 dilutions for fractions #23‑25, #43 (40 µL sample + 80 µL buffer).  
5. Transfer all calibrators and antigen controls into 2 mL tubes.  
6. Dilute Enzyme Conjugate 11x with conjugate buffer to 1× (enough for 100 µL/well).  
7. Dilute Wash Buffer 21x with sterile water to 1× (enough for 4.2 mL/well).  
8. Using epMotion (or manual pipetting), dispense 25 µL of each calibrator, control, sample, and diluted extract into a 96‑well plate in duplicate.  
9. Add 100 µL of enzyme conjugate 1× to each well.  
10. Shake the plate at 900 rpm for 1 h at room temperature (18‑25 °C).  
11. Wash plate 6× with 700 µL wash buffer 1× (use the plate washer); after the last wash, invert and tap the plate against paper towel to dry.  
12. Add 200 µL TMB substrate to each well, incubate 15 min at room temperature.  
13. Add 50 µL stop solution to each well, mix gently by tapping for 5 s.  
14. Measure OD450 within 30 min of adding stop solution.  

**Quality control**  
- All standard curves must fall within ±15 % of expected values, CV of duplicate standards ≤20 %.  
- QC samples (lower/upper limits) must fall within known ranges; repeat assay if not.  

**Data analysis**  
1. Use Excel to calculate average insulin concentration from the 4 extract dilutions (ng mL⁻¹).  
2. Normalize secreted insulin to IEQs: ng / 100 IEQs / min and % content/min.  
3. In Prism, plot insulin vs. time, calculate stimulation index (SI) and area under curve (AUC).  
4. Prepare raw data sheet and graphs for upload.  

**Reporting**  
- Upload all raw data, analysis files, and graphs to the IIDP HIPP database within 3 business days of assay completion.  
- Notify the HIPP CC (Joyce Niland, Ph.D.) of any deviations that may affect results.  

**Timeline**  
- Day 1: Sample thawing, dilution prep, reagent prep.  
- Day 1: ELISA run (≈4 h).  
- Day 2: Plate reading, data analysis, QC check.  
- Day 3: Final report and database upload.
