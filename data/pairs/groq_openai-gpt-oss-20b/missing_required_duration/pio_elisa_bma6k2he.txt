Please run the TGF‑β1 ELISA on our serum samples using the following protocol.

1. **Prepare the standard stock** 
 - At room temperature (RT), reconstitute 120 µl of the 4 800 ng L⁻¹ standard with 120 µl of standard diluent. 
 - Let the mixture stand for 15 minutes with gentle agitation, then you have a 2 400 ng L⁻¹ standard stock. 

2. **Make serial dilutions (duplicate wells)** 
 - Dilute the 2 400 ng L⁻¹ stock 1:2 with standard diluent to obtain 1 200 ng L⁻¹. 
 - Repeat the 1:2 dilution to get 600 ng L⁻¹, 300 ng L⁻¹, and 150 ng L⁻¹. 
 - Use standard diluent alone as the zero standard (0 ng L⁻¹). 
 - Set each concentration in duplicate.

3. **Prepare wash buffer** 
 - Dilute 20 ml of Wash Buffer Concentrate 25× with deionized/distilled water to make 500 ml of 1× Wash Buffer. 

4. **Bring all reagents to RT before use.** 

5. **Plate set‑up** 
 - Place the assay strips in their frames. 
 - Add 50 µl of each standard to its well. 
 - For each serum sample, add 40 µl of the serum to the sample well, then 10 µl of anti‑TGFβ1 antibody, followed by 50 µl of streptavidin‑HRP. 
 - Mix gently, cover the plate with a sealer, and incubate 60 minutes at 37 °C. 

6. **Wash step** 
 - Remove the sealer. 
 - Wash the plate 5 times with 0.35 ml of wash buffer per well. 
 - Each wash should soak the wells to 1 minute, then blot dry on paper towels. 

7. **Add substrates** 
 - Add 50 µl of Substrate Solution A to each well, then 50 µl of Substrate Solution B. 
 - Cover with a new sealer and incubate 10 minutes at 37 °C in the dark. 

8. **Stop reaction** 
 - Add 50 µl of Stop Solution to each well. The blue color will instantly change to yellow. 

9. **Read the plate** 
 - Immediately measure the optical density at 450 nm on a microplate reader. 
 - Record all readings within 10 minutes of adding the stop solution. 

10. **Data** 
 - Provide us a spreadsheet with sample ID, duplicate OD450 values, standard curve points, and calculated concentrations.
