1. Clone the sgRNA sequences into the pX330-U6-Chimeric_BB-CBh-hSpCas9 vector. Order the sense and antisense oligonucleotides, resuspend them at 100 µM, and set up the phosphorylation and annealing reaction using 1 µl of each oligo, 1 µl of 10x T4 ligation buffer, 0.5 µl of T4 PNK, and water up to 10 µl. Digest 5–10 µg of the pX330 vector with BbsI, add 1 µl of CIP, incubate at 75 °C for 15 min, heat-inactivate at 65°C for 15 min, and gel purify the vector. Set up the ligation reaction with 1 µl of linearized vector, 1 µl of the diluted (1:200) phosphorylated and annealed crRNA duplex, 1 µl of 10x T4 ligation buffer, 0.5 µl of T4 Ligase, and water up to 10 µl. Incubate at 20°C–25°C for 2 h, then heat-inactivate at 65°C for 15 min. Also include a no-insert control.

2. Transform 2 µl of the ligation reaction into 20 µl of chemically competent Stbl3 E. coli cells using heat shock. Recover in 1 ml LB with shaking at 37°C and 200 rpm for 1 h, then spread 100 µl onto antibiotic selection plates and incubate at 37°C for 12–16 h. Pick at least three single colonies, inoculate 4–5 ml LB cultures containing the appropriate antibiotic, and grow overnight at 37°C and 200 rpm. Extract plasmid DNA using a miniprep kit and confirm the insertion by Sanger sequencing using the LKO.1 primer (5′-GACTATCATATGCTTACCGT-3′). Proceed with a larger endotoxin-free midiprep for confirmed clones and measure the final DNA concentration.

3. Prepare cells for nucleofection by thawing plasmids and reagents on ice and setting up a 12-well plate with 1 ml of complete RPMI per well. Harvest 2 x 10^6 cells per condition, centrifuge at 500 x g for 5 min, wash the pellet twice with room temperature PBS, and gently resuspend in 100 µl of Ingenio Nucleofector solution. Mix equivalent ratios of the donor DNA plasmid and the pX330-sgRNA plasmid (or prepare RNP complexes by combining 3.9 µl sgRNA, 5.1 µl Cas9 protein at 10 mg/ml, and 6 µl PBS, incubating at room temperature for 20 min). Add the DNA or RNP mixture to the resuspended cells, transfer to a 0.2 mm cuvette, and nucleofect using an Amaxa device with program D-23. Immediately add warm media from the 12-well plate to the cuvette and transfer the cells to the prepared 12-well plate. Place the plate in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO2.

4. Perform FACS sorting approximately 3 days post-nucleofection (or 24–48 hours for RNP transfections). Centrifuge the cells at 500 x g for 5 min, remove the supernatant, resuspend in FACS buffer (PBS + 2% FBS), and add a viability dye such as Hoechst 33258 (1:5000 dilution). Gate on viable cells and sub-gate to isolate the target population (GFP-positive/DsRed-negative for HDR knock-ins, or the top 5% ATTO 550-positive cells for RNP/ssODN editing). Single-cell sort directly into 96-well plates containing 200 µl of complete RPMI per well, avoiding outer wells, and loosely wrap plates in foil to prevent evaporation. Incubate at 37°C with 5% CO2 for 3 to 4 weeks until clones expand.

5. Genotype and validate the expanded single-cell clones. Take an aliquot of approximately 50 µl from each clone, transfer the remaining cells to a 24-well plate for continued expansion, and isolate genomic DNA using the QuickExtract DNA Extraction solution. Set up PCR reactions using Phusion High-Fidelity DNA polymerase in a 25 µl final volume containing 1 µl gDNA, 0.2 µl Primer F (10 µM), 0.2 µl Primer R (10 µM), 12.5 µl Phusion 2x master mix, and water up to 25 µl. Use cycling conditions consisting of an initial denaturation at 98°C for 30 s; 27 to 32 cycles of 98°C for 10 s, primer-dependent annealing for 10–30 s, and 72°C extension for 15–30 s/Kb; followed by a final extension at 72°C for 10 min. Run 5 µl of each PCR product on a 2% agarose gel at 100–110 V to check for single bands. Purify the PCR products of successful clones using a cleanup kit or MinElute and confirm the edits by Sanger sequencing using the PCR primers. Expand validated clones into 12-well culture plates.
