STEPS
## RNA isolation
1. Wash cultured cells twice with 1 × PBS.
2. The total cellular RNA is extracted using TRIzol™ Reagent (Invitrogen, CA), according to the manufacturer’s protocol stated as below.
3. Add 0.3–0.4 mL of TRIzol™ Reagent per 1 × 10 5 -10 7 cells directly to the culture dish to lyse the cells.
4. Pipet the lysate up and down several times to homogenize.
5. Incubate for 5 minutes to permit complete dissociation of the nucleoproteins complex.
6. Add 0.2 mL of chloroform per 1 mL of TRIzol™ Reagent used for lysis, then securely cap the tube.
7. Incubate for 5 minutes.
8. Centrifuge the sample for 15 minutes at 12,000 g at 4°C.
9. Transfer the aqueous phase containing the RNA to a new tube.
10. Add 0.5 mL of isopropanol to the aqueous phase, per 1 mL of TRIzol™ Reagent used for lysis.
11. Incubate for 10 minutes.
12. Centrifuge for 10 minutes at 12,000 g at 4°C. Total RNA precipitate forms a white gel-like pellet at the bottom of the tube.
13. Discard the supernatant with a micropipette.
14. Resuspend the pellet in 1 mL of 75% ethanol per 1 mL of TRIzol™ Reagent used for lysis.
15. Vortex the sample briefly, then centrifuge for 5 minutes at 7,500 g at 4°C.
16. Discard the supernatant with a micropipette.
17. Vacuum or air dry the RNA pellet for 5–10 minutes.
18. Resuspend the pellet in 20–50 µL of RNase-free water by pipetting up and down.
19. Incubate in a water bath or heat block set at 55–60°C for 10–15 minutes. Proceed to downstream applications, or store the RNA at –70°C.
## Reverse transcription of RNA and qPCR
20. Determine the concentration of RNA by measuring the absorbance at 260 nm.
21. Prepare the reverse transcription reaction system (10 µL) as follows to synthesize cDNA:
 5 × All-In-One RT MasterMix (abm, CA)       2 µL
 RNA                                  1 µg
 RNase-free ddH 2 O                  add up to 10 µL
22. Set up the RT-PCR reaction procedure as described below:
 25℃             10 minutes
 42℃             50 minutes
 85℃             5 minutes
23. Prepare the following qPCR system (10 µL) on ice:
 cDNA mixture                                   1 µL
 BrightGreen 2 × qPCR MasterMix-ROX (abm, CA)          5 µL
 upstream primer (10 µM)                           0.3 µL
 downstream primer (10 µM)                          0.3 µL
 ddH 2 O                                  add up to 10 µL
24. Set up the qPCR reaction procedure as described below:
 Step 1           95℃     10 minutes
 Step 2           95℃      15 seconds
                   60℃     60 seconds
 40 Cycles in Step 2
 Melting curve
25. Calculate relative gene expression by comparison of the CT value of the gene of interest with that of 18S rRNA which is an internal control.
