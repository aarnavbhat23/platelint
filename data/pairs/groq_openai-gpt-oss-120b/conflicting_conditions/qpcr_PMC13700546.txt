We have 32 equine umbilical cords (≈20 cm each) that need to be processed for paired isolation of mesenchymal stem cells (uc‑MSC) using the reference 3 h collagenase I digestion (RM) and the extended 24 h digestion (NM). Please follow the steps below exactly and return all data (culture logs, microscopy scores, flow‑cytometry files, RT‑qPCR Ct values, differentiation scores, population‑doubling calculations).

1. **Initial decontamination (done on whole cords)**
   - Cut a 20 cm segment from the allantoic portion near the placenta with sterile scalpel.
   - Rinse under running 4 °C tap water for 5–10 min until macroscopically clean.
   - Submerge in 70 % ethanol (Carl Roth) for 3 min, constantly agitating.
   - Air‑dry, then submerge in 1 % iodine solution (B. Braun Vet Care) for 3 min, agitating.
   - Rinse thoroughly in sterile Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺‑free PBS (Thermo Fisher) until clear.
   - Place each cord in a sterile, airtight bag and add 500 mL sterile buffered transport solution:
     * PBS (‑/‑) + 500 IU/mL penicillin, 0.5 mg/mL streptomycin, 2.5 µg/mL amphotericin B, 0.05 mg/mL gentamicin (5 % Pen/Strep, 1 % Amp B, 0.1 % Genta).
   - Double‑bag, vortex to ensure complete submersion, store at 4 °C for exactly 12 h.

2. **Tissue preparation (after the 12 h storage)**
   - Rinse cords in fresh transport solution.
   - Strip away vessels, isolate perivascular Wharton’s jelly, and clean off intervascular jelly and endothelium.
   - Mince jelly into 1–3 mm³ pieces with sterile scissors.
   - Weigh 10.5 ± 1.0 g of minced tissue per sample and transfer to a 50 mL conical tube.

3. **Enzyme digestion (paired per donor)**
   - Prepare enzyme solution: low‑glucose DMEM (Thermo Fisher) + collagenase I (Thermo Fisher) at 500 U/mL (≈2–2.5 mg/mL, batch‑specific). Use the same batch for both digestions of a given donor.
   - Add 2 mL enzyme solution per gram of tissue.
   - For the **RM** tube: incubate 3 h at 37 °C in a water bath, vortex gently every 30 min, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step.
   - For the **NM** tube: incubate 24 h at 37 °C, vortex gently every 30 min.

4. **Post‑digestion processing (identical for both)**
   - Filter through a 2 mm sterile metal mesh (Fackelmann), rinse with PBS (‑/‑).
   - Centrifuge 600 g, 10 min, RT.
   - Pass supernatant through a 100 µm cell strainer (Corning), rinse, centrifuge again as above.
   - Perform one final PBS wash, then resuspend the cell pellet in **basic culture medium**:
     * Low‑glucose DMEM, 20 % fetal bovine serum (Sigma‑Aldrich), 1 % Pen/Strep, 1 % amphotericin B (increase from 0.2 % as per protocol), 0.1 % gentamicin.
   - Seed the entire suspension into one T75 flask (TPP) per digestion condition.

5. **Cell culture**
   - Incubator: 37 °C, 5 % CO₂, humidified.
   - 24 h after seeding: wash 3× with PBS (+/+) (Carl Roth), then replace medium twice weekly.
   - Monitor daily; record confluence and morphology.
   - Passage when 80–90 % confluent: detach with 0.25 %/0.02 % trypsin/EDTA (Thermo Fisher) for 10–15 min, split 1:3.
   - Freeze cells at passage 2 (P2) in cryopreservation medium:
     * Low‑glucose DMEM, 40 % FBS, 10 % DMSO (AppliChem), 1 % Pen/Strep, 1 % amphotericin B, 0.1 % gentamicin; 2 × 10⁶ cells/mL.
     * Use Mr. Frosty™ for controlled -80 °C step (≥1 day) then transfer to liquid N₂ for long‑term storage.

6. **Isolation success scoring (microscopy)**
   - Score attachment before passage 1: 0 = none, 1 = any attachment.
   - Score number of attached cells: 0 = none, 1 = single cells, 2 = small islands (≤10 cells), 3 = large islands (>10 cells).
   - Record any contamination in P0 (0 = clean, 1 = contaminated). Exclude paired donor if either tube contaminated.

7. **Flow cytometry (paired samples, N ≈ 15 usable donors)**
   - Detach cells with Accutase® (Carl Roth), count, and aliquot 1 × 10⁵ cells per well in V‑shaped 96‑well plates.
   - Primary antibodies (1:100–1:250 as indicated):
     * CD29 (APC, Miltenyi), CD34 (PE, BioLegend), CD44 (eFluor 450, eBioscience), CD45 (biotin, Bio‑Rad), CD90.1 (OX‑7, Alexa 450 or APC‑Fluor 780, BioLegend), CD90.2 (PerCP, BioLegend), CD105 (PE‑Cy7, eBioscience).
   - Secondary: Streptavidin‑FITC (1:200).
   - Viability dye: eFluor 506 (1:1000).
   - Acquire on BD FACSCanto II, analyze with FlowJo v8 (gate debris, doublets, dead cells as per Supplementary Fig S1).

8. **RT‑qPCR (paired samples, same 15 donors)**
   - Detach with Accutase®, rinse in PBS (‑/‑).
   - Extract RNA (Macherey‑Nagel), synthesize cDNA (Bio‑Rad), run SYBR Green qPCR (Bio‑Rad) on a ViiA7.
   - Housekeeping genes: GAPDH, ACTB, 18S.
   - Targets: POU5F1 (OCT4), DNMT3B, DENND4A, MYC, CD90 (THY‑1). Use primers listed in Table 2.
   - Calculate ΔCt relative to the three housekeeping genes; verify that no‑RT and water controls are >4 Ct away.

9. **Population‑doubling time (PDT) assay (paired samples, N = 3 donors)**
   - Seed 30 000 cells per well in 12‑well plates (3.5 cm²) – day 0 (T₀).
   - Each day, harvest three wells per donor (duplicate counts) with the Bio‑Rad automated cell counter; also record dead‑cell %.
   - Continue through day 8 (T₈). Compute PDT using:  
     PDT = (T − T₀) × log₂ / [log N_T − log N₀] (hours).

10. **Tri‑lineage differentiation (paired samples, N = 3 donors)**
    - **Chondrogenic (pellet, duplicate)**
      * Thaw, expand, and seed 5 × 10⁵ cells into 15 mL conical tubes, add 2 mL chondrogenic medium (as per ref 53), vortex 1 min daily.
      * Change medium 3× week, keep caps loose for gas exchange.
      * Harvest day 21, fix in 4 % formalin, embed in paraffin, section, stain Alcian blue and H&E.
      * Blindly score Bern score (0–9) for uniformity, matrix, and cell morphology.
    - **Osteogenic (triplicate)**
      * Seed 20 000 cells cm⁻² on laminin‑coated 24‑well plates (2 µg cm⁻² laminin, Corning).
      * At confluence add 1 mL osteogenic medium, change 3× week.
      * Day 21 fix in 4 % formalin, stain Von Kossa + nuclear fast red.
      * Blindly score (0–9) for % FOV coverage, deposit size, and clustering (Table 1).
    - **Adipogenic (triplicate)**
      * Seed as above, use StemPro™ Adipogenesis Kit (Thermo Fisher) supplemented with 5 % rabbit serum, 1 % Pen/Strep, 1 % Amp B, 0.1 % Genta.
      * Change medium every other day, harvest day 7, fix in 4 % formalin, stain Nile red + DAPI.
      * Blindly score 0–3 for % differentiated cells and 0–3 for lipid droplet size/arrangement.

11. **Data handling & statistics**
    - Record all raw measurements (microscopy scores, flow‑cytometry .fcs files, Ct values, cell counts, differentiation scores).
    - Perform paired non‑inferiority analyses as described (paired t‑test or Wilcoxon signed‑rank, Holm correction). Use the margins from the original study (e.g., PDT ≤ 12 h, IPT ≤ 7 d, etc.).
    - Deliver a concise report with mean ± SD (or median [IQR]) for each endpoint, p‑values, and confidence intervals.
