1. Produce fluorescent ICAM‑1‑displaying virus‑like particles (VLPs) in HEK‑293T cells.

   • Seed 5 × 10⁶ HEK‑293T cells in 10 mL complete DMEM (DMEM + GlutaMAX, 10 % heat‑inactivated FBS, 10 mM HEPES) per 10 cm tissue‑culture dish. Incubate overnight at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂.

   • When cells are 70‑80 % confluent, prepare the DNA mix in a low‑binding 1.5 mL tube: 8 µg of one of the fluorescent psPAX2 plasmids (psPAX2‑D64V‑NC‑mNeon #196509, psPAX2‑D64V‑NC‑eYFP #257940, psPAX2‑D64V‑NC‑TagBFP #257941, or psPAX2‑D64V‑NC‑mScarlet #257942) plus 4 µg of pCMV6‑ICAM‑1 #257960 (or the E34A mutant #257961 for the loss‑of‑function control) in 500 µL Opti‑MEM. Mix gently.

   • In a second tube combine 500 µL Opti‑MEM with 36 µL X‑tremeGENE 9 (Roche). Mix gently (avoid touching the plastic with the reagent).

   • Add the 500 µL X‑tremeGENE 9 mixture to the DNA tube (total 1 mL), mix, and incubate 15 min at room temperature.

   • Add the whole transfection mixture dropwise to the HEK‑293T dish, spreading evenly. Incubate overnight (≈16 h) at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂.

   • Replace the medium with 10 mL fresh complete DMEM (same composition as above) very gently to avoid detaching cells. Incubate another 24 h.

   • Collect the supernatant (~9 mL) with a sterile 10 mL syringe, filter through a 0.45 µm syringe filter into a 15 mL conical tube.

   • Add Lenti‑X Concentrator at 1 : 3 (v/v); i.e., 3 mL Lenti‑X to 9 mL filtered supernatant. Mix by inversion and incubate overnight at 4 °C.

   • Centrifuge at 1500 × g for 45 min at 4 °C. Discard supernatant carefully; if any remains, spin briefly again and remove with a P200 pipette.

   • Resuspend the pellet in 90 µL PBS (100× stock). Aliquot as needed and store at –80 °C (avoid repeated freeze‑thaw). Use the stock immediately for the binding assay.

2. Perform the ICAM‑1‑VLP binding assay on our target cells (THP‑1 and Jurkat lines; also any patient‑derived T‑cell blasts we will provide).

   • Thaw the appropriate VLP stock (mNeon, eYFP, TagBFP, or mScarlet) and keep on ice.

   • Harvest the target cells by centrifugation at 350 × g for 5 min, discard supernatant, and resuspend directly in Binding Buffer (RPMI + GlutaMAX, 0.1 % BSA, no serum) at 1–2 × 10⁶ cells mL⁻¹.

   • Dispense 50 µL cell suspension (0.5–1 × 10⁵ cells) into each well of a 96‑well V‑bottom polypropylene plate.

   • Add 50 µL 2× Mg²⁺/EGTA solution (prepared in Binding Buffer to give final 5 mM MgCl₂ and 2 mM EGTA) to each well, mix gently. This activates LFA‑1.

   • Immediately add 1–5 µL of the 100× VLP stock (aim for a final VLP dilution between 1:100 and 1:20; titrate if needed). Mix gently.

   • Incubate the plate at 37 °C for 10 min.

   • Fix cells by adding 33 µL cold 4 % PFA in PBS to each well (final PFA = 1 %). Mix and place the plate on ice for 20 min.

   • Centrifuge the plate at 350 × g for 3 min, discard supernatant (invert and blot on a paper towel).

   • Wash cells twice with 250 µL cold FACS buffer (PBS + 0.2 % BSA): add buffer, centrifuge 350 × g for 3 min, discard supernatant each time.

   • Resuspend cells in 200 µL FACS buffer and keep on ice.

   • Acquire data on the Attune NxT flow cytometer (405 nm, 488 nm, 561 nm lasers). Use the appropriate fluorescence channel for the VLP fluorophore (e.g., BFP, eYFP, mNeon, or mScarlet). Collect ~140 µL per well using the plate sampler.

   • Store fixed, washed samples at -80 °C for up to 3 days if acquisition cannot be performed immediately.

3. Controls and validation we need:

   • Empty‑vector VLPs (psPAX2 + empty pCMV6) produced in parallel as a negative binding control.

   • ICAM‑1‑E34A VLPs as a ligand‑loss‑of‑function control.

   • For Jurkat cells, include the αL‑KO line (if available) as a receptor‑negative control.

   • Include unstained cells for autofluorescence baseline.

4. Deliverables:

   • Concentrated VLP stocks (100×) for each fluorophore and for the E34A variant, with concentration estimates based on volume recovered.

   • Flow‑cytometry FCS files for each condition (THP‑1, Jurkat, patient blasts) with the VLP binding data.

   • A brief report summarizing VLP production yields (µg Gag per mL), transfection efficiency (fluorescence microscopy), and binding assay results (median fluorescence intensity, % positive cells) for all controls.
