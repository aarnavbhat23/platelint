Please carry out the following experiments using our supplied cell lines, animals, and custom materials:

1. **Material Preparation & Bioink Formulation**
   - Extract and purify Aloe vera (AV) gel from the provided fresh leaves using the described centrifugation and filtration protocol. Verify via GPC and LC-MS.
   - Synthesize GelMA (approx. 60% substitution) by reacting gelatin with methacrylic anhydride, followed by dialysis and lyophilization. Confirm via 1H-NMR.
   - Prepare three sterile bioink formulations: (1) 10 wt% GelMA with LAP, (2) 10 wt% GelMA / 1 wt% Laponite nanoclay with LAP, and (3) 10 wt% GelMA / 1 wt% Laponite / 40 vol% AV with LAP.

2. **3D Bioprinting & In Vitro Culture**
   - Encapsulate rat bone marrow mesenchymal stem cells (rBMSCs, passage 5) into the bioinks at $1.0 \times 10^6$ cells/mL.
   - Print 8-layer rectangular grid constructs (5 mm × 5 mm) using the low-temperature extrusion bioprinter with UV layer-by-layer crosslinking. Calculate printability and diffusion rates.
   - Culture printed constructs in static osteogenic induction medium for up to 28 days.
   - Assess cell viability (Calcein-AM/PI) and proliferation (CCK-8) on days 1, 4, 7, and 14.

3. **In Vitro Osteogenesis & Macrophage Assays**
   - Perform Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP) and Alizarin Red S (ARS) staining on 2D co-cultures and 3D constructs at days 10, 14, and 28.
   - Run RT-qPCR (for IL-1β, CD206, iNOS, IL-10, RUNX2, OCN) and Western blot (Collagen I, RUNX2, GAPDH) following 10 days of osteogenic co-culture.
   - Evaluate macrophage recruitment (Transwell migration/invasion), adhesion, and polarization (immunofluorescence for iNOS and CD206, plus flow cytometry) using RAW 264.7 cells exposed to scaffolds or conditioned media.

4. **In Vivo Animal Models**
   - **Subcutaneous Model (Nude Mice):** Impregnate bioprinted precursors subcutaneously in 8-week-old female BALB/c nude mice. Retrieve implants at 2 and 4 weeks for Micro-CT, H&E, and immunofluorescence (OCN, RUNX2, Periostin, CD31, Collagen I, Vinculin, iNOS, CD206). Harvest 4-week samples for bulk RNA-seq and pathway enrichment analysis.
   - **Calcreat Defect Model (SD Rats):** Create bilateral 5 mm critical-sized calvarial defects in 8-week-old female SD rats and implant the bone organoid precursors. Euthanize at 4 and 8 weeks for Micro-CT, H&E, and immunofluorescence.
   - **Biosafety Evaluation:** Collect blood at 4 weeks for complete blood counts and serum biochemistry (ALT, AST, BUN, CREA). Harvest heart, liver, spleen, lungs, and kidneys at 8 weeks for H&E histological evaluation.
