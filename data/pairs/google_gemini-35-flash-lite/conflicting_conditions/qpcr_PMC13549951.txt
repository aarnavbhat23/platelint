Please process our submitted wild-type (WT) and hSKP2-KI mouse prostate samples, organoids, and human prostate cancer cell lines (PC3, 22Rv1, and C4-2B) using the following assays. 

For the molecular and biochemical analyses, extract total RNA using RNAStat and perform quantitative PCR using SYBR green chemistry, utilizing mouse Gapdh and human ACTB as internal controls. Homogenize prostate tissues in RIPA buffer with protease inhibitors for Western blotting, running equal amounts of total protein on 8–16% denaturing SDS-PAGE gels, transferring to PVDF membranes, blocking with 5% non-fat milk, and probing overnight at 4°C with antibodies against SKP2 (ab68455), p27 Kip1 (#610241), E-cadherin (#610181), N-cadherin (#610920), Vimentin (#550513), Twist1 (#sc15393), or β-TUBULIN (sc-5274), followed by ECL detection.

For histology and immunohistochemistry, fix tissue samples and organoids in 10% neutral buffered formalin, embed in paraffin, and section at 5μm. Perform H&E staining and Masson’s trichrome staining using Bouin's mordant, Hematoxylin, Scarlet Acid Fuchsin Red, phosphotungstic acid, and Aniline Blue. For IHC, perform antigen retrieval using sodium citrate buffer in a steamer, quench endogenous peroxidases with 3% hydrogen peroxide, block with 3% normal goat serum, and incubate with primary antibodies against Ki67 (#12202), AR (#5153), SKP2 (#ab68455), and p27 Kip1 (#610241). Visualize using HRP-labeled secondary antibodies and DAB, and quantify positive cells across 12 random fields at 40x magnification using ImageJ. For human tissue microarrays, execute SKP2 IHC using a Leica Bond RX autostainer with EDTA antigen retrieval and primary antibody incubation (Cell Signaling #2652; 1:100) overnight at 4°C, followed by Bond Polymer Refine Detection and quantification via HALO software, and hold the plate at 37 °C for the whole of this step.

For transcriptomics, isolate total RNA from 12-month ventral prostate lobes (n = 4 per group) ensuring RIN numbers between 8 and 10, generate cDNA libraries via poly-T enrichment and random priming, and sequence on an Illumina Platform PE150 to yield 20 million paired-end reads. Perform quality trimming and mapping using CLC Genomic Workbench, identify differentially expressed genes at FDR ≤ 0.05, run GSEA for pathway analysis, and process bulk RNA-seq mixtures through CIBERSORTx with S-mode batch correction using the provided single-cell signature file from the GUDMAP database.

For functional assays, perform migration and invasion assays using Falcon 24-well multiwell systems and Corning BioCoat Matrigel Invasion Chambers. Serum-starve PC3 and C4-2B cells (stably transfected with pcDNA3.1 vector control or SKP2) for 24 hours, seed 30,000 PC3 or 80,000 C4-2B cells onto upper inserts in serum-free RPMI 1640, use RPMI 1640 with 10% FBS in the bottom well as a chemoattractant, incubate for 72 hours, scrub non-migrated cells, and fix/stain the remaining cells with cold methanol and hematoxylin for quantification across 5 areas per insert in triplicate.

For organoid drug screening, digest fresh prostate tissues in 5 mg/ml Collagenase II with 10 μM Y-27632 at 37°C for 2 hours, wash, further digest in TrypLE with 10 μM Y-27632, resuspend 20,000 cells in cold Matrigel, plate in 48-well plates, and culture in mouse prostate medium with Y-27632. For inhibitor assays, plate WT and hSKP2-KI organoids in 96-well plates, treat in triplicate with vehicle (0.5% DMSO) or SKP2 inhibitors FKA and C1 at indicated concentrations, image morphology and size via Keyence fluorescence microscopy, and assess viability using Calcein AM dye conversion measured relative to day 0 across n = 15 per group. 

Apply Student’s t-test, one-way ANOVA with Bonferroni correction, or Welch’s correction where variances are unequal, considering p < 0.05 statistically significant based on a power calculation targeting an effect size of 2.0 and >85% power at α = 0.05.
