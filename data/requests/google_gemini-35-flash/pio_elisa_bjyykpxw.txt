Please run a human IL-17A sandwich ELISA on the serum samples we are sending over according to the following protocol:

1. Coat a 96-well plate with monoclonal anti-human IL-17 capture antibody.
2. Add the serum samples to the plate alongside a standard curve prepared using recombinant human IL-17A (Bio Basic Cat #RC212-28).
3. Incubate the plate for 1 hour 30 minutes at room temperature.
4. Wash the plate 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
5. Add biotin-conjugated anti-human IL-17 detection antibody to each well and incubate for 1 hour 30 minutes at room temperature.
6. Wash the plate 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
7. Add peroxidase-labeled streptavidin conjugate to each well and incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
8. Wash the plate 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
9. Add colorimetric substrate solution and allow color reaction to develop.
10. Stop the reaction by adding acid stop solution.
11. Read absorbance at 450 nm.
12. Generate the standard curve, calculate the IL-17 concentration for each serum sample, and return the raw absorbance values and calculated concentrations to us.
