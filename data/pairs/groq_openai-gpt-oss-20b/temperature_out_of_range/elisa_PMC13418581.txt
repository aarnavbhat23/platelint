**Request for MEAET ELISA Development and Sample Testing**

1. **Recombinant MEAET Production**  
   - Codon‑optimized MEAET gene (SEQ ID NO: 1) is already available.  
   - Transform E. coli BL21(DE3) with the plasmid.  
   - Grow in LB + 100 µg mL⁻¹ ampicillin to OD₆₀₀ = 0.4–0.6.  
   - Induce with 500 µM IPTG, 16 °C, 120 rpm, 18 h.  
   - Harvest, lyse by sonication, purify GST‑MEAET on glutathione agarose, digest GST with thrombin, and dialyze into PBS.  
   - Verify purity by SDS‑PAGE, determine concentration by BCA, aliquot, and store at –80 °C.

2. **Hyperimmune Serum Generation**  
   - Emulsify purified MEAET with ISA 206 VG adjuvant 1:1 (v/v).  
   - Immunize 3 SPF piglets (30 days old) via multiple‑site intramuscular injection.  
   - Primary dose: 1 mg per piglet.  
   - Boosters: 2 mg per piglet every 10 days, total of 3 immunizations.  
   - Collect pre‑immune serum (negative control) and post‑immunization serum when titers reach >1:102 400 (by ELISA).  
   - Separate serum, aliquot 0.5 mL, seal, and store at –40 °C.

3. **Indirect ELISA Protocol**  
   - Coat 96‑well plates with 100 µL/well of MEAET (final concentration 5 µg mL⁻¹) in carbonate‑bicarbonate buffer, pH 9.6. Incubate 1 h @ 37 °C, then 4 °C overnight.  
   - Wash 3× with PBST (PBS + 0.05 % Tween‑20).  
   - Block 200 µL/well with 5 % skim milk in PBST, 37 °C 2 h, wash 3×.  
   - Add 100 µL of each serum diluted 1:400 in PBST, incubate 1 h @ 37 °C.  
   - Wash 3× PBST, add 100 µL HRP‑conjugated goat anti‑pig IgG (1:10 000 in PBST), incubate 1 h @ 37 °C.  
   - Wash 5× PBST, add 100 µL TMB substrate, 15 min @ 75 °C in dark, stop with 50 µL 2 M H₂SO₄.  
   - Read OD₄₅₀ (microplate reader).  

4. **Cut‑off Determination**  
   - Test the 24 confirmed negative sera (no MEAET reactivity by Western blot).  
   - Calculate mean OD + 3 SD; convert to S/P ratio.  
   - Set S/P ≥ 0.203 as positive; S/P < 0.203 as negative.

5. **Sensitivity Assessment**  
   - Perform two‑fold serial dilutions of 3 known positive sera (1:100 to 1:12 800).  
   - Run ELISA; highest dilution with S/P ≥ 0.203 defines analytical sensitivity.

6. **Specificity Assessment**  
   - Test the 3 positive sera plus the following organisms at 1:200 dilution (triplicate): Salmonella spp., Pasteurella multocida, Clostridium perfringens, Listeria monocytogenes, Campylobacter jejuni, Streptococcus suis, E. coli K‑12, PEDV, PDCoV.  
   - Include positive (hyperimmune serum) and negative (confirmed negative sera) controls.

7. **Sample Testing**  
   - Use the following panels: 16 confirmed positives, 47 diarrheal pig sera, 106 asymptomatic pig sera, 24 negative controls.  
   - Perform ELISA as described, record OD₄₅₀, compute S/P, classify each sample.  

8. **Data Analysis**  
   - Compile all OD values and S/P ratios in an Excel file: columns – Sample ID, OD₄₅₀, S/P, Result (Positive/Negative).  
   - Use Python (3.10.1) with matplotlib (3.11.0) to plot ROC curve; determine optimal cut‑off by Youden index; calculate sensitivity, specificity, AUC.  
   - Compute repeatability and reproducibility CVs from triplicate runs; use standard formulas.  

9. **Deliverables**  
   - Purified MEAET protein (catalogue number, concentration, lot).  
   - Hyperimmune serum (volume, titer).  
   - Raw ELISA data (Excel), OD₄₅₀, S/P, classification.  
   - ROC analysis report (PDF).  
   - Sensitivity, specificity, CVs in a summary table.
