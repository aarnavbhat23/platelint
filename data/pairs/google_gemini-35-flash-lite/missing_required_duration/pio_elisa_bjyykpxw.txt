Please run an IL-17A ELISA on our provided patient serum samples using the following procedure:

1. Coat 96-well ELISA plates with monoclonal anti-human IL-17 antibodies.
2. Add the patient serum samples to the coated plates.
3. Incubate the plates for 1 hour and 30 minutes at room temperature.
4. Wash the plates 4 times using PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
5. Add biotin-conjugated anti-human IL-17 to the wells and incubate and 30 minutes at room temperature.
6. Wash the plates 4 times with PBS-Tween 20 buffer.
7. Add a peroxidase-labeled streptavidin conjugate and incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
8. Wash the plates again as above, then add the reactive substrate solution to develop the colored reaction.
9. Stop the reaction by adding acid.
10. Measure the absorbance at 450 nm and calculate the IL-17 concentration using a standard curve generated with human IL-17 (Bio Basic Inc., Cat #RC212-28.SIZE.1mg).
