Please run a real-time qPCR assay on our provided cDNA samples using the following protocol:

1. Plate our cDNA samples on a 96-well qPCR plate, running each sample in technical triplicate (three replicates per sample).
2. For each well, prepare a 1080 µL reaction mix containing:
   - 5 μL Fast SYBR Green Master Mix (Applied Biosystems, Cat. No. 4385616)
   - 3 μL nuclease-free water
   - 0.5 μL forward primer
   - 0.5 μL reverse primer
   - 1 μL cDNA sample
3. Use Atg7-specific primers with the following sequences:
   - Forward: 5′- GTTCGCCCCCTTTAATAGTGC -3′
   - Reverse: 5′- TGAACTCCAACGTCAAGCGG -3′
4. Include a negative control for the run consisting of nuclease-free water, primers, and the Master Mix, with no template cDNA.
5. Perform the qPCR run and collect the cycle threshold (Ct) values for all wells.
6. Normalize the resulting Ct values against GAPDH used as the housekeeping gene.
