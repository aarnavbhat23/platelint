Please perform the SpL ELISA on our serum samples as follows:

1. Coat a 96‑well microtitre plate with 1 µl of unlabeled protein L (SpL) at 1 mg/ml in carbonate–bicarbonate buffer, pH 9.6. Cover and incubate overnight at 4 °C.
2. Block the plate by adding 200 µl per well of 1 % bovine serum albumin (BSA) in PBS, incubate 30 min at room temperature, then wash 4 × with PBS‑Tween (0.05 % Tween‑20).
3. Add 50 µl of each of our serum samples (1 mg/ml) to the wells, incubate 1 h at room temperature, and wash 4 × with PBS‑Tween.
4. Prepare a peroxidase‑labeled SpL conjugate diluted 1:5 000 in PBS with 5 % non‑fat dry milk. Add 50 µl to each well, incubate 1 h at room temperature, and wash 4 × with PBS‑Tween.
5. Add 50 µl of 4 mg/ml o‑phenylenediamine (OPD) to each well, incubate 15 min at room temperature in the dark.
6. Stop the reaction with 50 µl of 3 M H₂SO₄ added to each well.
7. Measure optical density at 492 nm on a microplate reader. Calculate the cut‑off as the mean OD of the negative controls multiplied by 3. Record the OD values, the cut‑off, and any visual colour development for each well.

We will provide the serum samples, negative controls, and any required reagents not specified. Please return the raw OD data, calculated cut‑off, and a brief summary of the results.
