1. HepG2 cells are seeded in a 96-well plate at a density of 1×10 5 cells/well and incubated at 37°Cfor 24 h.
2. The cells are then treated with the above extracts for 6 h.
3. Subsequently, 0.5 mg/mL MTT reagent dissolved in MEM is added to each well, and the plate is further incubated at 37°Cfor 3 h.
4. After the culture medium is discarded, the cells are washed once with PBS (pH 7.2), and DMSO is added to solubilize the formazan product.
5. Absorbance is then measured at 570 nm using a micro-plate reader (KeHua ST-360, Shanghai, China).
