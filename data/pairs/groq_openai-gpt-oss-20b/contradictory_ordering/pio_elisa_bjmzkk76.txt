Please run an anti‑HIV ELISA and confirm positives by dot blot on our samples (layer hens, rats, and cats).  

**Reagents & consumables (use the catalog numbers provided):**  
- 3,3′,5,5′‑Tetramethylbenzidine (TMB) – Sigma #54827-17-7  
- ELISA Diluent 2× – STEMCELL #1916 (120 mL)  
- Nunc™ 96‑well polystyrene round‑bottom plates – Thermo Fisher #260210 (use U‑shaped bottom)  
- ELISA Blocking Buffer – Thermo Fisher #N502 (3 % non‑fat milk in PBS)  
- Coating Buffer – BioLegend #421701  
- SpLAG‑anti‑IgY‑HRP conjugate (peroxidase‑labelled IgY coupled to Protein A, G, and L) – homemade, dilute 1:1000 in ELISA diluent  
- PBS‑Tween‑20 (0.05 % Tween‑20) for washing  
- PBS (phosphate‑buffered saline) for dilutions  
- 1 mg/mL IgY (purified) – use 25 µL per well  
- Positive control serum (human anti‑HIV positive)  
- Negative control serum (turtle serum, Sigma)  
- Blank solution (0.9 % normal saline)

**Materials needed that you must supply:**  
- Synthetic gp‑120 peptide (coating concentration 50 ng per well)  

**Procedure:**

1. Coat each well with 50 ng of synthetic gp‑120 in 100 µL coating buffer. Seal plates and incubate overnight at 4 °C.   Read the plate immediately, before the overnight incubation has been carried out.
2. Wash plates 4× with PBS‑Tween‑20, then block with 25 µL/well of 3 % non‑fat milk in PBS. Incubate 1 h at room temperature (RT).  
3. Wash 4× with PBS‑Tween‑20. Add 25 µL/well of 1 mg/mL IgY + 25 µL/well of serum from cats or rats diluted 1:16 in PBS‑non‑fat milk, in triplicate for each sample. Incubate 2 h at RT.  
4. Wash 4×, add 25 µL/well of SpLAG‑anti‑IgY‑HRP diluted 1:1000. Incubate 1 h at RT.  
5. Wash 4×, add 50 µL/well of TMB. Incubate 15 min in the dark.  
6. Stop the reaction once the positive control wells are visibly coloured; blanks and negative controls should remain clear.  
7. Read absorbance (e.g., at 450 nm).  
8. Calculate the cut‑off as mean absorbance of negative controls × 4. Any sample above this threshold is considered positive.  
9. For all positive wells, perform a dot blot on the same serum to confirm the presence of anti‑HIV antibodies.  

Please report absorbance values, the calculated cut‑off, and any positives that are confirmed by dot blot. Let me know if any additional supplies or clarification is required.
