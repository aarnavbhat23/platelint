We need you to calibrate OD600 to CFU for 12 samples (6 positive controls BBa_I20270 and 6 negative controls BBa_R0040). Please use the following steps:

1. **Materials & Setup**  
   - 1.5 mL Eppendorf tubes (for starting samples and dilutions 4‑5)  
   - 2.0 mL Eppendorf tubes (for dilutions 1‑3)  
   - 96‑well black flat‑bottom plate (for OD measurement)  
   - LB broth, LB agar, chloramphenicol 25 mg mL⁻¹ in EtOH (prepare LB + Cam media: 50 µg mL⁻¹ final concentration)  
   - Pipettes and fresh tips  
   - Vortex mixer  
   - 37 °C incubator, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step.
   - Plate reader (linear OD600 range)

2. **Measure OD600 of each overnight culture**  
   - Dilute each culture in LB + Cam to fall in the linear range (e.g., 1:8 dilution).  
   - Measure OD600, subtract the blank (media only), and multiply by the dilution factor to get true C₁ (e.g., C₁ = (OD₁₋₈ – blank) × 8).

3. **Prepare starting samples at OD600 = 0.1**  
   - Use C₁ × V₁ = C₂ × V₂ where C₂ = 0.1 OD600 and V₂ = 1000 µL.  
   - Example: if C₁ = 1.224, V₁ = (0.1 × 1000) / 1.224 ≈ 82 µL culture + 918 µL media.  
   - Do this in triplicate for each culture (18 total starting samples).  
   - Verify OD600 of each diluted sample; it should be ~0.1.

4. **Set up serial dilutions**  
   For each of the 18 starting samples:  
   a. **Dilution 1‑3**: use 3 × 2.0 mL tubes. Add 1900 µL LB + Cam to each tube.  
   b. **Dilution 4‑5**: use 2 × 1.5 mL tubes. Add 900 µL LB + Cam to each tube.  
   c. Label tubes: “Sample X – Dilution 1” … “Sample X – Dilution 5”.  
   d. Pipette 100 µL of the starting sample into Dilution 1, discard tip, vortex 5–10 s.  
   e. For Dilution 2: pipette 100 µL from Dilution 1 into Dilution 2, discard tip, vortex.  
   f. Repeat for Dilution 3.  
   g. For Dilution 4: pipette 100 µL from Dilution 3 into Dilution 4, discard tip, vortex.  
   h. For Dilution 5: pipette 100 µL from Dilution 4 into Dilution 5, discard tip, vortex.  
   i. Do not pipette up/down; use a fresh tip for each transfer.

5. **Plate the dilutions**  
   - For each starting sample, spread 100 µL of Dilution 3 onto one LB + Cam agar plate, 100 µL of Dilution 4 onto a second plate, and 100 µL of Dilution 5 onto a third plate.  
   - In total: 18 samples × 3 plates = 54 agar plates.  
   - Label plates accordingly.

6. **Incubation**  
   - Incubate all plates at 37 °C overnight (18–20 h).

7. **Colony counting**  
   - After incubation, count colonies on each plate that has fewer than 300 colonies.  
   - Discard plates with >300 colonies or re‑dilute if necessary.

8. **CFU calculation**  
   - For each counted plate, multiply the colony count by the final dilution factor:  
     *Dilution 3*: 1 : 8000 → factor = 8 × 10³  
     *Dilution 4*: 1 : 80000 → factor = 8 × 10⁴  
     *Dilution 5*: 1 : 800000 → factor = 8 × 10⁵  
   - CFU mL⁻¹ = colony count × factor.  
   - This gives CFU per 1 mL of an OD600 = 0.1 culture.

9. **Data reporting**  
   - Provide a table with: sample ID, control type (positive/negative), OD600 measured, dilution factor, colony counts per plate, CFU mL⁻¹ for each dilution, and average CFU mL⁻¹ per sample.  
   - Attach raw images of plates and the spreadsheet.

10. **Quality notes**  
    - If any plate has >300 colonies, note the issue and omit that data point.  
    - Use fresh tips for each transfer to avoid cross‑contamination.  
    - Record the exact times of incubation and counting for reproducibility.

Please complete the experiment using the above procedure and return the colony counts and CFU calculations for all samples. Let us know if you need additional supplies or clarification.
