## Step-by-step method details
Step-by-step method details dCas9-KRAB-mediated enhancer silencing in differentiated 3T3-L1 adipocytes Timing: 3 days (performance of preparation 1 strictly necessary) This step uses a catalytically inactive Cas9 fused to the KRAB transcriptional repressor domain (dCas9-KRAB) to silence the RS1 enhancer in fully differentiated 3T3-L1 adipocytes. This approach interrogates enhancer function in its native genomic context. Lep expression following dCas9-KRAB targeting is compared to cells transfected with a non-targeting scramble sgRNA to determine whether RS1 contributes to Lep transcriptional regulation independently of DNA methylation. 1. Day 1: Cell Seeding a. After 10 days of differentiation in the 10 cm dish (see Preparation 1), transfer 3T3-L1 adipocytes to a 12-well plate: i. Wash cells with 1× DPBS, trypsinize, add antibiotic-free differentiation medium, gently pipette up and down ii. Count and seed cells at 4∗10 4 cells/well on a 12 well plate (one 10 cm dish is sufficient for 1.5–2 12 well plates). iii. Allow cells to recover for 24 h at 37°C, 5% CO 2 before transfection. 2. Day 2: Transfection a. Plasmid transfection (dCas9-KRAB): Prepare transfection mix per well as described in dCas9-KRAB transfection mix, materials and equipment . Vigorously mix all components by pipetting or vortexing, incubate for 15 min at 22 ± 1°C, and add dropwise to the well. b. Centrifuge the plate at 300 × g for 5 min. c. Incubate at 37°C, 5% CO 2 for 4.5 h, then change to fresh antibiotic-free differentiation medium. d. sgRNA transfection: Prepare sgRNA transfection mix per well as described in sgRNA transfection mix, materials and equipment . Vigorously mix by pipetting or vortexing, incubate for 15 min at 22 ± 1°C, and add dropwise to each well. e. Centrifuge at 300 × g for 5 min. f. After 4.5 h, change to fresh 3T3-L1 differentiation medium. g. Incubate for 36 h at 37°C, 5% CO 2 . 3. Day 3: Harvest a. Harvest cells for downstream RNA extraction by washing the cells once with cold 1× DPBS and adding Qiazol for lysis. Continue with RNA isolation and qRT-PCR to quantify Lep expression (see next step). CRITICAL: Always include a scramble sgRNA (non-targeting, no complementarity to the mouse genome) as a transfection control in parallel to the RS1-targeting sgRNA. This controls for non-specific effects of the transfection procedure and dCas9-KRAB overexpression on Lep expression. Transfect 3 different sgRNAs separately as sgRNAs can differ in their activity and specificity. When testing sgRNAs and specifically the scramble RNA, always include a negative transfection control (no sgRNA added) to check for off-target effects of the scramble RNA on gene expression read-out of interest. Note: Sequential transfection of plasmid and sgRNA (separated by 4.5 h) is necessary because 3T3-L1 adipocytes are difficult to transfect and benefit from the reduced toxicity of a two-step approach. Choose the appropriate cell line reflecting your tissue of interest. Pause point: Cells can be lysed and stored as RNA at −80°C after harvest before proceeding to qRT-PCR. RNA extraction, cDNA synthesis, and quantitative RT-PCR quantification of Lep expression Timing: 1 day This step quantifies the effect of dCas9-KRAB-mediated RS1 silencing on Lep mRNA expression in differentiated 3T3-L1 adipocytes and can be applied to check gene expression levels of interest. The housekeeping gene Hprt is used for normalization. 4. RNA Extraction a. Extract total RNA using the miRNeasy Mini Kit (used here) or RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) according to the manufacturer’s instructions using Qiazol. Include a on-column DNase digestion step as recommended and instructed by the manufacturer. b. Elute RNA in RNase-free water and quantify using NanoDrop or equivalent. Pause point: RNA can be stored at −80°C until cDNA synthesis. 5. cDNA Synthesis. a. Dilute RNA to the desired concentration in nuclease-free water (typically targeting a total of 500 ng or 1000 ng (50 ng/μL or 100 ng/μL). Prepare the cDNA synthesis master mix on ice as described in cDNA synthesis reaction mix, materials and equipment . b. Add 10 μL RNA (50 ng/μL or 100 ng/μL) per reaction tube, mix gently, and centrifuge briefly. Dilute cDNA in nuclease-free water to a working concentration of 5 ng/μL. Note: Always include a no-template control (NTC; water instead of RNA) and a no-reverse-transcriptase control (no-RT; omit MultiScribe™ reverse transcriptase) per RNA batch to detect contamination and genomic DNA carryover, respectively. Pause point: cDNA can be stored at −20°C. 6. qPCR. a. Prepare the qPCR reaction mix on ice (qPCR Reaction Mix, materials and equipment ) as master mix. b. Export Ct values for Lep (approx. 29) and Hprt (approx. 24) per sample. CRITICAL: Use the same Ct threshold for Lep and Hprt across all samples being directly compared. Changing thresholds between runs introduces systematic errors that cannot be corrected post hoc. Note: Run each sample in technical duplicates. Exclude technical replicates with a Ct standard deviation > 0.5 from analysis. DNA isolation from adipose tissue Timing: 1–3 h This step produces genomic DNA from frozen adipose tissue of sufficient purity and quantity for bisulfite conversion and subsequent methylation analysis. The QIAamp Fast DNA Tissue Kit (preferred for large tissue amounts) or the QIAamp Mini Kit can be used interchangeably. 7. Keep adipose tissue on dry ice until transferred. 8. Weigh 60–100 mg adipose tissue on dry ice. 9. Transfer tissue to a 2 mL screw-cap micro tube containing 3–4 cell lysis beads. 10. Prepare tissue lysis buffer, materials and equipment , and add to the tube. 11. Homogenize using a bead mill (2 cycles; pause 5 s between cycles; homogenization time 15 s per cycle at the instrument’s high-speed setting). Alternatives: For the disruption of cells, other mechanical alternatives to cell lysis beads and bead mill could be used such as tissue homogenizers. 12. Proceed with the QIAamp Fast DNA Tissue Kit or QIAamp DNA Mini Kit according to the manufacturer’s instructions. 13. Elute DNA in 50 μL DEPC-treated H2O. The expected yield ranges from 70–200 ng/μL. Pause point: Isolated DNA can be stored at −20°C for several months or at −80°C for long-term storage before proceeding. Alternatives: Other DNA extraction kits including DNA purification steps might work equally well (such as DNA from AllPrep DNA/RNA/Protein Mini Kit, Qiagen). DNA quantification Timing: 30 min Accurate DNA quantification is required to use the correct input amount for bisulfite conversion. 14. Prepare Qubit dsDNA BR working solution by diluting Qubit dsDNA BR Reagent 1:200 in Qubit dsDNA BR Buffer. 15. Calibrate the Qubit 4 fluorometer on each measurement day: a. Prepare 2 calibration tubes by mixing 190 μL working solution with 10 μL of Standard 1 (S1) and Standard 2 (S2), respectively. b. Insert into the Qubit 4 and run the calibration routine. 16. Prepare sample measurement tubes: add 199 μL working solution to Qubit 0.5 mL PCR tubes. 17. Add 1 μL of each DNA sample to its tube, mix by pipetting, and incubate at 22 ± 1°C for at least 2 min in the dark. 18. Measure on the Qubit 4 using the built-in dsDNA BR protocol; the instrument automatically reports the concentration in the original sample tube using the input volume that was set when starting the Qubit assay. Note: The Qubit dsDNA BR Assay is preferable to absorbance-based methods (e.g. NanoDrop) because it specifically detects double-stranded DNA and is insensitive to RNA, single-stranded DNA, and contamination (e.g. protein, phenol, salts). Bisulfite conversion of genomic DNA Timing: ∼2.5 h (hands-on: ∼30 min) Bisulfite conversion chemically deaminates unmethylated cytosines to uracil while methylated cytosines remain unchanged, enabling discrimination of methylation status at single-CpG resolution. 19. Use 1000 ng DNA where possible. Adjust volume and buffer, especially the elution buffer, accordingly (Bisulfite conversion reagent volumes, materials and equipment ). 20. Set up the bisulfite conversion reaction in a PCR tube according to the EpiTect Fast DNA Bisulfite Kit, adding DNA, bisulfite solution, and DNA Protect Buffer. 21. Run the following thermal cycling program: Steps Temperature Time Denaturation 95°C 5 min Incubation 60°C 30 min Denaturation 95°C 5 min Incubation 60°C 20 min Hold 20°C Until use 22. Perform clean-up and desulfonation steps as instructed by the manufacturer , including all recommended drying and washing steps. 23. Elute bisulfite-converted DNA in 15–50 μL Buffer EB, targeting ∼20 ng/μL relative to input DNA. Record elution volume. CRITICAL: Include all drying and washing steps during clean-up; incomplete desulfonation reduces PCR amplification efficiency. Note: For input DNA < 100 ng, add Carrier-RNA to Buffer BL as specified by the manufacturer to prevent adsorption of low-concentration DNA to the membrane. Pause point: Bisulfite-converted DNA is stable at −20°C for several weeks. Avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles as this can degrade the converted DNA. Bisulfite PCR Timing: ∼3 h (including gel electrophoresis) BisPCR amplifies the region of interest from bisulfite-converted DNA (bisDNA) using assay-specific primers. During amplification, bisulfite-converted uracils are converted to thymines. One primer carries a 5′ biotin tag required for pyrosequencing. 24. Thaw bisulfite-converted DNA, primers, and PyroMark PCR MasterMix on ice. 25. Prepare master mix on ice as described in materials and equipment . Prepare one additional H 2 O (no-template) control reaction. 26. Pipette 24 μL master mix (without DNA) into DNA-free PCR strip tubes. 27. Add 1 μL bisulfite-converted DNA to each tube. This amount can be adjusted depending on bisDNA yield. Pipette up and down carefully to ensure complete transfer. 28. Mix by brief gentle vortexing and centrifuge. 29. Run the following PyroMark cycling program: Steps Temperature Time Cycles Initial denaturation 95°C 10 min 1 Denaturation 94°C 30 s 40 (mouse)/45 (human) Annealing 49.5°C (ms)/52.9°C (hu) 25 s Extension 72°C 25 s Final extension 72°C 10 min 1 Hold 4°C max. 16 hours Note: The annealing temperature is assay specific. For other regions, optimize as described in Preparation 1. Pause point: PCR products can be stored at −20°C for longer storage. 30. Verify the PCR product by agarose gel electrophoresis: a. Prepare a 1% agarose gel in 1× TAE buffer with 1 μL SYBR Safe DNA Gel Stain per 10 mL gel. b. Load 4 μL of PCR product alongside GeneRuler 100 bp DNA Ladder. c. Run at 150 V for 20–25 min (times and voltage depends on used electrophoresis chamber size) in 1× TAE buffer. d. Image the gel on the ChemiDoc Touch or an equivalent imaging system and analyze band intensity using Image Lab software or equivalent (example shown in Figure 2 A). CRITICAL: Only proceed to pyrosequencing if a single, specific band of the expected size is visible and the no-template control is negative. Pyrosequencing Timing: ∼2 h (after PCR product verification) CpG pyrosequencing on the PyroMark Q48 Autoprep provides nucleotide-resolution quantification of DNA methylation at each CpG site within the sequenced region. 31. Program the PyroMark Q48 Autoprep software with the CpG sequencing dispensation order for the relevant assay. 32. Load and prepare the PyroMark Q48 Autoprep cartridge and disc according to the manufacturer’s instructions , using the PyroMark Q48 Advanced CpG Kit. Use the assay-specific sequencing primer (4 μM in annealing buffer) prepared in Preparation 3. 33. Load each well of the disc with 3 μL streptavidin-coated magnetic beads and 10 μL BisPCR product (15 μL for assays requiring higher input, e.g., sorted adipocyte samples). 34. Start the run; the instrument automatically performs bead capture, washing, denaturation, annealing, and sequencing. 35. After the run, inspect each well individually in the PyroMark Q48 Autoprep software and flag any wells showing sequencing failures (e.g., low signal, background peaks, failed dispensation). Note: Exclude wells with sequencing failures from downstream analysis. Common causes include insufficient bead loading, degraded PCR product, or primer artifacts, see troubleshooting problem 4 . 36. Export methylation data (percentage methylation per CpG) and normalize to the standard curve prepared from the methylated control DNA pools through linear regression (refer to Preparation 4). CRITICAL: Normalize all mouse methylation data to the assay-specific standard curve within each run to account for non-linearities and run-to-run variation. In vitro methylation of pCpGL-CMV vector Timing: ∼5 h (hands-on: ∼30 min) In vitro methylation of the insert-containing pCpGL-CMV vector with M.SssI methylates all CpG sites in the insert, enabling functional assessment of the effect of CpG methylation on enhancer-driven luciferase expression. 37. Dilute SAM stock (32 mM) 1:20 in nuclease-free water to prepare a 1.6 mM working solution (1 μL SAM + 19 μL nuclease-free water). Prepare fresh on the day of use. 38. Set up methylation and mock reactions, materials and equipment , targeting 750–1000 ng plasmid per 10–15 μL reaction. 39. Incubate at 37°C for 4 h. 40. Heat-inactivate M.SssI at 65°C for 20 min. 41. Purify the methylated and mock-methylated vectors by drop dialysis on a 0.1 μm nitrocellulose membrane floating on Milli-Q water for ≥5 min. 42. Recover DNA droplet. 43. Quantify, dilute to 50 ng/μL in nuclease-free water. Pause point: Methylated DNA can be stored at −20°C. CRITICAL: Prepare methylated and mock-methylated vectors from the same DNA preparation in parallel to minimize batch-to-batch variability. The mock-methylated control is essential for calculating relative luciferase activity. Transfection of HEK293T cells and luciferase measurement Timing: 3–4 days HEK293T cells are co-transfected with the firefly luciferase reporter (methylated or mock) and Renilla-SV40 as a transfection efficiency control. Luciferase signals are measured 24 h after transfection using the Dual-Glo system. Transfection time can be extended by additional 24 h to a total of 48h of transfection. 44. Day 1: Cell Seeding a. Seed HEK293T cells in 96-well plates (if using white 96-well plates here, no transfer of the lysate during luciferase measurement is necessary) at 1 × 10 4 cells per well in 100 μL antibiotic-free HEK293T growth medium per well. b. Incubate at 37°C, 5% CO 2 for 16 h. Cells should be 80–90% confluent at the time of transfection (Day 2). Note: Antibiotic-free medium is required during transfection to avoid cytotoxicity from the interaction of Lipofectamine 2000 with antibiotics. 45. Day 2: Transfection a. Prepare Lipofectamine 2000–Opti-MEM mix: dilute 0.2 μL Lipofectamine 2000 in 4.8 μL Opti-MEM per well (see materials and equipment ; scale up for required well number plus excess). b. Mix gently and incubate at 22°C ± 1°C for 5 min. c. Meanwhile prepare DNA–Opti-MEM mix per condition in PCR strip tubes (do not pre-mix all conditions in one tube): i. Add 2 μL plasmid (methylated or mock, 50 ng/μL) + 1 μL Renilla-SV40 (1 ng/μL) + 2 μL Opti-MEM per well (scale for number of reactions + excess). d. Combine 20 μL Lipofectamine–Opti-MEM (step 45a scaled) with 20 μL DNA–Opti-MEM (step 45c scaled) in the same PCR strip tube. Mix, briefly centrifuge. Incubate at 22 ± 1°C for 10–20 min. e. Using a multichannel pipette, dispense 10 μL of the transfection mix into each well of the 96-well plate (cells already contain 100 μL medium). Mix gently. f. Incubate at 37°C, 5% CO 2 for 24 h. CRITICAL: Each condition (methylated and mock for each insert) should be plated in technical triplicates. Multiple independent transfections on different days constitute biological replicates. Include appropriate transfection controls including background (transfection mix without any oligonucleotides) and empty pCpGL-CMV vector (accounting for background luciferase activity and additionally displaying the effects of the cloned enhancer). 46. Day 3: Luciferase Measurement a. Prepare Dual-Glo Luciferase Assay system reagents according to the manufacturer’s instructions . b. Add an equal to the medium amount (here 100 μL) of the Luciferase Assay reagent to the well. c. Lyse for 10 min at 22 ± 1°C. Shield plate from light. d. If a white plate was not used, transfer 180 μL of the lysate to a white plate appropriate for luminescence measurements. CRITICAL: Avoid bubbles when pipetting. Remove bubbles if visible e. Measure luminescence in a plate luminometer appropriate for 96-well formats. f. Add an equal to the medium amount (here 100 μL) or if 180 μL of the lysate were transferred add 90 μL of freshly prepared Stop & Glo Reagent. g. Incubate for 10 min at 22 ± 1°C. Shield plate from light. h. Read luminescence in a plate luminometer appropriate for 96-well formats. i. Export raw Firefly (FF) and Renilla (Ren) values. j. Subtract background from both FF and Ren values. k. Normalize each background subtracted FF signal to its corresponding Ren signal: normalized FF = FF/Ren. l. Take the average over the technical triplicates of mock and methylated value group. Adjust the averages of the normalized FF(mock or methylated) to the normalized FF(mock) within the same biological replicate. CRITICAL: The 1:1 ratio of Firefly to Renilla plasmid (100 ng : 1 ng) ensures robust Renilla signal for normalization without suppressing the experimental signal. Include appropriate transfection controls including background (transfection mix without any oligonucleotides) and empty pCpGL-CMV vector (accounting for background luciferase activity and additionally displaying the effects of the cloned enhancer). Alternatives: Instead of the Dual-Glo Luciferase Assay System, the Dual-Luciferase Reporter Assay System can be used.
