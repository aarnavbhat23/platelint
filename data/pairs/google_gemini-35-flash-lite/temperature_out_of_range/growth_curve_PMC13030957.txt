1. Process our provided bacterial samples through the s5PSeq library preparation and sequencing workflow, and run the standard growth assays where requested.

2. For the antibiotic sensitivity growth assays, dilute overnight cultures of our Bacillus subtilis and Lactiplantibacillus plantarum strains to an OD600 of 0.005–0.05 and incubate them aerobically in 96-well plates for 18–24 hours with chloramphenicol (0, 1.6, 8, or 40 μg/mL) or erythromycin (0, 0.1, 0.5, or 2.5 μg/mL), monitoring growth via a microplate reader. For Clostridioides difficile strains, dilute overnight cultures to an OD600 of 0.05 and incubate anaerobically in BHI medium with erythromycin (0, 1, or 256 μg/mL).

3. For culture treatments prior to RNA extraction, grow the appropriate bacterial strains to mid-exponential phase (OD600 = 0.6–0.8). Treat 1 mL aliquots with the specified test antibiotics for 10 minutes. For C. difficile clinical isolates, add 500 μL of RNAprotect Bacteria Reagent following antibiotic treatment and incubate for 5 minutes. Harvest cells by centrifugation at 13,000 rpm for 3 minutes and freeze the pellets on dry ice.

4. Extract total RNA using a phenol-chloroform method combined with bead beating, precipitate in ethanol, and elute in nuclease-free water. Quantify RNA using a Nanodrop and Qubit, and assess quality using an Agilent Bioanalyzer or gel electrophoresis.

5. Prepare s5PSeq libraries starting from 200 ng of total RNA without prior DNase treatment:
   - Block ribosomal RNA using 0.4 μM of universal blocking oligos and 4 μM of species-specific 16S/23S oligos in 50 mM NaCl, using a thermal profile of 75°C for 5 min, 65°C for 5 min, and stepwise 3°C decreases every 2 min down to 35°C.
   - Ligate single-stranded RNA adapters containing UMIs and barcodes to the RNA using T4 RNA ligase 1, 1 mM ATP, and 1X T4 ligation buffer at 75 °C for 1 h.
   - Purify ligated RNA using RNA CleanXP beads (1.8X volume).
   - Perform first-strand cDNA synthesis using SuperScript II reverse transcriptase and random hexamers.
   - Degrade template RNA with 28.6 mM NaOH at 65°C for 20 min, neutralize with Tris-HCl, and purify cDNA using Ampure XP beads (1.8X volume).
   - Amplify libraries using dual-barcoded Illumina-compatible primers and Phusion High-Fidelity PCR Master Mix (98°C for 30 s; 15 cycles of 98°C for 20 s, 65°C for 30 s, 72°C for 30 s; 72°C for 7 min).
   - Size-select PCR products using Ampure XP beads (0.6X–0.8X) to isolate 400–600 bp fragments.
   - For mixed-species controls, combine total RNA from B. subtilis, S. aureus, E. faecalis, and C. difficile at equal mass (100 ng each) and process identically.

6. Quantify size-selected libraries using the Qubit dsDNA HS assay and sequence them on the Illumina NextSeq 2000 platform at 650 pM.

7. Repurpose the Illumina libraries for Nanopore sequencing by attaching Nanopore adapters using the Oxford Nanopore Ligation Sequencing Kit v14 (SQK-LSK114). Sequence the libraries on both a Flongle flow cell (R10.4.1) using a MinION for 24 hours and a PromethION flow cell (R10.4.1) for 72 hours. 

8. Perform bioinformatics processing on the sequencing data:
   - Demultiplex Illumina reads using bcl2fastq, trim adapters with cutadapt, extract UMIs with UMI-tools, align to reference genomes using STAR, and analyze with the fivepseq package.
   - For Nanopore data, perform base calling using Dorado (fast model for Flongle) or Guppy (SUP model for PromethION), demultiplex using minibar.py, extract UMIs with UMI-tools, and align reads using minimap2 before running fivepseq and samtools analysis.
