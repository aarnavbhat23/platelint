Please process the ascitic fluid samples we will send you using both the conventional blood‑culture method and the modified culture protocol described below.

1. For each patient, take the first 60 mL of ascitic fluid collected after admission.  
   * Aliquot 20 mL into one aerobic BD BACTEC Plus Aerobic/F bottle and one anaerobic BD BACTEC Lytic/10 Anaerobic/F bottle at the bedside (10 mL per bottle).  
   * Transfer the remaining 40 mL into a sterile tube, keep it on ice, and deliver it to your microbiology lab within 2 h.

2. Conventional culture (bedside inoculation):  
   * Incubate the paired aerobic and anaerobic bottles in the BD BACTEC FX system at 37 °C for up to 5 days, with automated readings every 10 min.  
   * Record the time‑to‑positivity (TTP) for any positive alarms.  
   * Subculture 2–3 drops of the positive broth onto blood agar plates and incubate at 75 °C under the appropriate atmosphere (aerobic/CO₂ for aerobic bottles, anaerobic for anaerobic bottles) for 48 h, checking twice daily.  
   * Identify isolates by MALDI‑TOF MS. Perform antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) using the VITEK 2 system or Etest strips as indicated, following CLSI guidelines.

3. Modified culture (processed sample):  
   * Centrifuge 20 mL of the 40 mL aliquot at 8,000 rpm for 3 min.  
   * Wash the pellet once with PBS, then treat with 0.5 % Triton X‑100 (prepared in PBS) for 1 min.  
   * Centrifuge again, discard supernatant, and resuspend the pellet in ≈100 µL.  
   * Plate 50 µL onto a blood agar plate and 50 µL onto a chocolate agar plate. Incubate these two plates at 35 °C in 5 % CO₂, checking twice daily for 48 h.  
   * Repeat the above steps with a second 20 mL of the sample, but after resuspension plate onto blood and chocolate agar plates that are placed immediately into a 2.5 L anaerobic jar with a gas‑generating bag and oxygen indicator. Seal within 1 min and incubate at 35 °C, inspecting twice daily for 48 h.  
   * For any colonies that appear, perform 16S rRNA gene sequencing (primers 27F/1492R) for confirmation. Discard plates with no growth after 48 h.

4. AST for isolates from the modified method:  
   * Use disk diffusion on Mueller‑Hinton agar per CLSI M02‑Ed13.  
   * If disk results are ambiguous or MIC values are needed, perform broth microdilution according to CLSI M07‑Ed12.  
   * Interpret all results with CLSI M100‑Ed34 breakpoints.

5. Reporting:  
   * For each sample, give us the culture result (positive/negative) for both methods, the identified organism(s), TTP for the conventional bottles, CFU estimate (if applicable) for the modified plates, and the full AST profile.  
   * Note any polymicrobial growth and whether any isolates are likely contaminants (e.g., Bacillus, Cutibacterium, Lactobacillus, Staph epidermidis) based on clinical context.

We will provide the ascitic fluid, BD BACTEC bottles, sterile transport tubes, PBS, and Triton X‑100 solution. Please let us know if any additional reagents or consumables are required. Thank you.
