Please run a liquid growth assay for our Aspergillus spore samples in a 96‑well format using the following steps:

1. Prepare a spore suspension in PBS + 0.01 % Tween at a concentration of 4 × 10⁵ spores / mL (approximately 1800 s to complete).

2. In a non‑coated CytoOne 96‑well plate (Starlab), add 5 µL of the spore suspension to each well. Then add 195 µL of the appropriate liquid growth medium to bring the total volume to 200 µL per well (≈2000 spores per well). This step should take about 20 min.

3. Fill the spaces between the wells with either the same growth medium or sterile water to prevent edge effects. (~5 min)

4. Cover the plate with a breathable seal that allows gas exchange. (~2 min)

5. Place a blank well containing 200 µL of sterile medium (no spores) for background subtraction.

6. Load the plate into the plate reader (PowerWave X‑2 with KC software or BioTek reader). Pre‑heat the instrument to 37 °C, set the run time to 48 h, and program OD₆₀₀ readings every 10 minutes.

7. Start the run and collect the raw OD₆₀₀ data. Export the data as a plain‑text file (one column per well, time points in rows) for downstream analysis.

Let me know if you need any additional reagents or clarification. Thanks.
