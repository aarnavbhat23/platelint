Please run the following qPCR on our samples.  

**Materials we have ready**  
- cDNA from each sample (thawed on ice, vortexed and quick‑spun).  
- 8‑tube PCR strips and matching racks.  
- 384‑well PCR plates, adhesive cover, sticky film, and brown helper film.  
- ABI SDS software and a compatible real‑time PCR instrument (e.g., 7900 Prism).  

**Materials to supply**  
- 20 µl multi‑channel pipettes (Hilda) and 200 µl tip boxes.  
- Filtered 200 µl tips.  
- PCR tube lids.  
- ABI Sybr Green Mastermix (or equivalent).  
- Primers for each gene (final concentration 1.25 µM).  
- Autoclaved MilliQ H₂O.  
- 5 µl pipette for cDNA.  

**Procedure**  

1. **Strip setup**  
   - Place an 8‑tube PCR strip in a rack. One tube = one sample × one gene.  
   - Add 5 µl of the prepared cDNA to the bottom of each tube (use a 20 µl pipette, keep the strip on ice).  

2. **Add mastermix**  
   - Add 25 µl of Sybr Mastermix to each tube for duplicates (35 µl for triplicates). Use the 200 µl multichannel pipette. Keep on ice.  
   - Mix briefly with the multichannel and quick‑spin the strip.  

3. **Transfer to 384‑well plate**  
   - Dispense 10 µl of the cDNA/Mastermix mixture from each tube into the assigned well of the 384‑well plate (plate layout prepared in advance). Keep the plate on ice.  
   - Gently blot the top of the plate with a Kimwipe to avoid cross‑contamination.  

4. **Seal the plate**  
   - Place the adhesive plate cover over the plate, smooth with the brown helper film, paying close attention to the edges and working from the center outwards.  
   - Spin the plate 5 min at 3500 rpm, 4 °C.  
   - Seal with the sticky film, then vortex and spin again (5 min, 3500 rpm, 4 °C).  

5. **Set up ABI SDS program**  
   - Open SDS 2.3. File → New.  
   - In the “Real‑Time” tab, connect to the instrument and open/close the door.  
   - Insert the plate, aligning A1 to A1, and close the door.  
   - On the Layout tab, highlight unused wells and click “Omit wells”.  
   - Highlight used wells, click “Add detector” for each gene; set the reaction volume to 10 µl.  
   - Check cycle times and temperatures:  

     | Stage | Temp (°C) | Time |
     |-------|-----------|------|
     | A | 50 | 2:00 |
     | B | 95 | 10:00 |
     | C | 95 | 0:15 |
     | D | 60 | 1:00 |
     | E | 95 | 0:15 |
     | F | 60 | 0:15 |
     | G | 95 | 0:55 |
     | H |  |  |

   - Add a dissociation stage for SYBR primers: 95 °C 15 s, 60 °C 1 min, 95 °C 15 s, 60 °C 15 s.  
   - Set the cycle number to 40.  

6. **Run the assay**  

**Primer validation (if needed)**  
- Prepare a primer standard curve using an extra set of 8‑tube strips.  
- For each primer set, mix 12.5 ml (+) and 12.5 µl (−) into 1 mL MilliQ H₂O.  
- Prepare a 10 µl reaction (triplicates): 10.20 µl water, 20 µl SYBR Green, 4.80 µl primers (2.5 µM each). Total 35.00 µl Mastermix.  
- Run as above.

Please let me know if any reagents are missing or if you need the specific plate layout.
