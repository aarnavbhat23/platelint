Please perform the Alphazurine A and Crystal Violet double viability assay on our submitted cell culture samples using 12-well or 24-well clear flat-bottom TC-treated plates (Falcon #353043 or #353047). Please ensure all experimental conditions and controls are run with at least 4 replicates per plate.

1. Plate Setup & Controls
- Centrifuge cell plates at 1200 rpm for 2 minutes prior to processing to prevent cell loss.
- For 100% dead cell control wells, aspirate medium, treat with 300 µL of 50% non-denatured ethanol for 3 to 5 minutes at room temperature, aspirate ethanol, rinse once with fresh culture medium, and refill with standard culture medium volume.
- For 100% live cell control wells and test wells, refresh the medium with clean DMEM/culture medium.

2. Alphazurine A Assay (Dead Cell Index)
- Add Alphazurine A (from a 10 mg/mL stock in water) directly to all wells to reach a final concentration of 0.2 mg/mL.
- Incubate plate at 75 °C for 40 minutes.
- Centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes.
- Wash 3 times with 1X Ca2+/Mg2+-free PBS, centrifuging at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes after each wash, and carefully aspirate the final supernatant.
- Elute retained dye by adding 50% ethanol / 1% acetic acid solution (volume equal to original culture volume).
- Incubate on a plate shaker at room temperature for 30 minutes, then centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 2 minutes.
- Transfer 200 µL of the supernatant from each well into a clear 96-well plate and measure absorbance at 637 nm.

3. Crystal Violet Sequential Assay (Total Remaining Cell Index)
- Aspirate the remaining elution solution from the original cell culture plates and rinse once with PBS.
- Add 4 µg/mL Crystal Violet solution in PBS (volume equal to original culture volume) to all wells.
- Incubate at room temperature for 30 minutes.
- Centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes.
- Wash 3 times with PBS, centrifuging at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes after each wash, and remove final supernatant.
- Elute retained dye by adding 50% ethanol / 1% acetic acid solution (volume equal to original culture volume).
- Incubate on a shaker at room temperature for 45 minutes, then centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 2 minutes.
- Transfer 200 µL of supernatant from each well to a clear 96-well plate and measure absorbance at 590 nm.

4. Data Deliverables
Please return the raw A637 and A590 absorbance values for all wells, along with the calculated metrics for each condition using the following equations:
- Fraction dead = (A637_test - A637_live) / (A637_dead - A637_live)
- Fraction alive = 1 - Fraction dead
- Fraction present = A590_test / A590_live
- Adjusted Viability Index = Fraction alive × Fraction present
