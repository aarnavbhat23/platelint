Please generate cardiac organoids from human induced pluripotent stem cells using the previously published protocol by Hofbauer et al. Maintain them at 37 °C in 95% air and 5% CO2 in chemically defined medium (CDM) prepared from a 1:1 mix of IMDM and F-12 Nutrient Mix, supplemented with 5 mg/mL BSA, 1% chemically defined concentrated lipids, 0.004% 1-thiolglycerol, 15 µg/mL transferrin, 10 µg/mL insulin, and 100 ng/mL VEGF-165. Reassociate the organoids into Corning 96-well black/clear round-bottom ultra-low attachment spheroid microplates at day 0. Perform size measurements of the organoid diameter using brightfield imaging via the Leica Application Suite microscope software.

For the treatments, use staurosporine at a 1 µM stock concentration to treat control models for 24 hours at 37 °C to induce apoptosis.

For the cryopreservation method, take 10-day-old cardiac organoids and resuspend them in CryoStor CS10 cryopreservation medium. Place four organoids per cryovial (2 mL round-bottom) with 1 mL of medium. Cool and store them overnight at −80 °C using a CoolCell freezing vial container to achieve a slow cooling rate of −1 °C/min, then transfer them to liquid nitrogen (−196 °C) for either 7 or 14 days. Thaw the samples in a 37 °C water bath until a small ice clump remains, wash them in prewarmed maintenance medium supplemented with 10 µM ROCK inhibitor Y-27632, and transfer them to a Corning 96-well spheroid microplate. Change the medium to standard maintenance medium after 24 hours. Perform viability and functionality measurements at 1 day and 1 week post-thawing. Maintain unpreserved controls at 37 °C for the exact same total duration of cell culture (i.e., day 10 + 24 h controls for samples preserved for 7/14 days plus 24 h recovery, and day 10 + 1 week controls for samples preserved for 7/14 days plus 1 week recovery).

For the room temperature-based preservation method, transfer 10-day-old cardiac organoids individually into wells of a Corning 96-well spheroid microplate containing 200 µL of CellShip Cell Transport medium without insulin (one organoid per well). Seal the plates with Nunc Seals to prevent evaporation and store them in a vibration-protected transport box at room temperature (20–25 °C) for 7 or 14 days. Afterward, transfer the organoids to a Corning 96-well spheroid microplate containing prewarmed maintenance medium with 10 µM ROCK inhibitor Y-27632. Switch to standard maintenance medium after 24 hours. Perform viability and functionality measurements at 1 day, 1 week, and 3 weeks post-preservation. Maintain corresponding unpreserved controls at 37 °C for identical total culture durations (day 10 + 24 h, day 10 + 1 week, and day 10 + 3 weeks).

For immunofluorescence imaging on cryosections, fix the cardiac models in 10% neutral buffered formalin at room temperature, immerse them in a 30% sucrose solution until use, embed them in cryogel, and cut 10 µm cryosections using a cryostat. Mount the sections onto SuperFrost Plus adhesion microscope slides. Air-dry the slides for 30 minutes, wash 3 times for 5 minutes with PBS, boil in pH 6 citrate buffer for antigen retrieval, and permeabilize in TBS with 0.1% Tween-20 (TBS-T). Block the cells with 20% preimmune goat serum (PIG) in Tris-NaCl-blocking buffer (TNB) for 1 hour at room temperature. Incubate overnight at 4 °C with primary antibodies diluted in 5% PIG in TNB: rabbit anti-cleaved caspase-3 (1:200), rat anti-Ki67 (1:1000), and mouse anti-cardiac troponin T (CTNT). Wash 3 times for 5 minutes in TBS-T, then incubate with secondary antibodies diluted in TNB: goat anti-rat Alexa Fluor 568 (1:200), goat anti-rabbit Alexa Fluor 647 Plus (1:1000), and goat anti-mouse Alexa Fluor 568 (1:1000). Wash with TBS-T, incubate with DAPI (5 µg/mL in PBS) for 15 minutes, and mount using ProLong Diamond Antifade Mountant with DAPI. Store the mounted slides at −75 °C. Visualize the markers using a Nikon Eclipse Ti-E inverted widefield microscope with a 20× objective and a Prime BSI sCMOS camera. Use ImageJ (version 1.54) to analyze cleaved caspase-3 and Ki67 positive cells by applying rolling ball background correction via a nuclear mask on the DAPI signal, and normalize the counts to the total number of nuclei.

For ATP-level quantification, transfer a single organoid into each well of a white opaque 96-well F-bottom plate containing standard culture medium. Add an equal volume of CellTiter-Glo 3D reagent to each well. Mix vigorously on a plate shaker for 5 minutes to induce cell lysis, then incubate for an additional 25 minutes to stabilize the luminescent signal. Record luminescence using a CLARIOstar Plus Microplate Reader.

For the assessment of functional beating, record uncompressed real-time videos (.AVI files) of beating cardiac organoids at 37 °C using a Nikon Eclipse Ti-E inverted microscope with a 5× objective at 50 fps for 30 seconds. Quantify the contraction profiles using the open-source software MUSCLEMOTION V1.0 to determine parameters including contraction duration, relaxation time, peak-to-peak time, and 10-to-10 transient time in milliseconds.

Perform statistical analyses using GraphPad Prism (version 10.4.0) on data representing the average of two to three biological replicates per condition, incorporating standard deviation. Identify outliers using Grubb's test. Test data for normality and homoscedasticity, and apply two-way ANOVA, the Kruskal-Wallis test, or Welch and Brown-Forsythe ANOVA tests depending on distribution and variance equality. Correct p-values for multiple comparisons using Tukey's, Dunn's, or Dunnett's tests as appropriate, and perform correlation analyses using the Pearson correlation test at a 95% confidence interval, considering p < 0.05 statistically significant.
