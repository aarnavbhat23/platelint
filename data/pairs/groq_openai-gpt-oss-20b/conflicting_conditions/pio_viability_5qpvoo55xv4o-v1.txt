Please run the 3‑D cell viability assay on our spheroid samples as follows:

1. **Reagent preparation**  
   1.1. Thaw the CellTiter‑Glo 3D reagent in the refrigerator overnight.  
   1.2. Before use, let it sit at room temperature for 30 min and gently mix.  

2. **ATP calibration curve**  
   2.1. Prepare a series of ATP dilutions (e.g., 0, 10, 25, 50, 75, 100 µM) in the same buffer used for the assay.  
   2.2. Transfer 50 µL of each dilution into the wells of an opaque 96‑well white plate (Costar 96 Flat Bottom).  

3. **Spheroid transfer**  
   3.1. Move the spheroids from the incubation dish into the same opaque 96‑well plate.  
   3.2. Place the plate on the 28‑magnet holder and aspirate the existing medium.  
   3.3. Add 50 µL of DMEM/F‑12 without FBS (with 1 % L‑Glutamine and 1 % Pen/Strep) to each well.  

4. **Add CellTiter‑Glo reagent**  
   4.1. Add 50 µL of the prepared CellTiter‑Glo reagent to each well (now containing 50 µL medium).  
   4.2. Cover the plate with aluminum foil to block light.  

5. **Incubation and mixing**  
   5.1. Incubate the sealed plate at room temperature (≈22 °C) for 25 min, and hold the plate at 37 °C for the whole of this step.
   5.2. During this time, gently shake the plate on an orbital shaker for 5 min at 2 rpm.  

6. **Luminescence measurement**  
   6.1. Set up the Tecan Infinite M Plex:  
        - Shaking mode: Orbital shaking  
        - Shaking time: 3 s  
        - Attenuation: None  
        - Integration time: 1000 ms  
        - Settletime: 0 ms  
        - Temperature: 22 °C  
   6.2. Place the plate (no lid) on the reader platform, start the read, and export the data to an Excel file.  

Materials we provide: ATP (stock solution), spheroids, incomplete DMEM/F‑12, CellTiter‑Glo 3‑D kit, opaque 96‑well white plates, aluminum foil, shaker, Tecan reader.
