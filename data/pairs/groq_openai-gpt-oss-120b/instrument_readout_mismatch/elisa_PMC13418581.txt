Please carry out the following work on the serum panel we will send (16 known positive, 47 diarrhoeic, 106 asymptomatic, plus 24 confirmed negative samples):

1. **Expression and purification of MEAET**  
   - Synthesize the codon‑optimized MEAET gene (SEQ ID NO:1, see Table S1) and clone into a prokaryotic expression vector with an N‑terminal GST tag.  
   - Transform into *E. coli* BL21 (DE3), grow in LB + 100 µg/mL ampicillin to OD₆₀₀ = 0.4–0.6, induce with 500 µM IPTG at 16 °C, 120 rpm for 18 h.  
   - Harvest cells, lyse by sonication, purify GST‑ME AET on glutathione agarose, then digest with thrombin to remove GST.  
   - Verify purity by SDS‑PAGE, determine concentration with a BCA assay, aliquot and store at –80 °C.

2. **Coating of ELISA plates**  
   - Prepare carbonate‑bicarbonate coating buffer (pH 9.6). Dilute purified MEAET to the optimal concentration determined in the checkerboard (use the value from Table S3, e.g., 1 µg/mL).  
   - Add 100 µL per well to 96‑well plates, incubate 37 °C for 1 h, then overnight at 4 °C.  

3. **Blocking**  
   - Wash plates 3× with PBST (PBS + 0.05 % Tween‑20).  
   - Add 200 µL per well of 5 % (w/v) skim milk in PBST, incubate 37 °C for 2 h, then wash 3× with PBST.

4. **Serum incubation**  
   - Dilute each serum sample 1:400 in PBST.  
   - Add 100 µL per well, incubate 37 °C for 1 h, wash 3× with PBST.

5. **Secondary antibody**  
   - Dilute HRP‑conjugated goat anti‑pig IgG 1:10 000 in PBST.  
   - Add 100 µL per well, incubate 37 °C for 1 h, then wash 5× with PBST.

6. **Detection**  
   - Add 100 µL TMB substrate, incubate in the dark at 37 °C for 15 min.  
   - Stop reaction with 50 µL 2 M H₂SO₄.  
   - Read luminescence on a microplate reader.

7. **Cut‑off determination**  
   - Test the 24 confirmed negative sera in duplicate.  
   - Calculate the mean OD₄₅₀ of the negatives plus three standard deviations; convert to the S/P ratio.  The expected cut‑off is S/P ≥ 0.203 (adjust if your data differ).

8. **Analytical sensitivity**  
   - Select three of the known positive sera. Prepare two‑fold serial dilutions (1:100 to 1:12 800) in sample diluent.  
   - Run each dilution in triplicate using the ELISA above.  
   - Record the highest dilution that still yields an S/P ≥ 0.203; this defines the assay’s limit of detection.

9. **Analytical specificity**  
   - Test the following heterologous sera (all diluted 1:200, in triplicate): Salmonella spp., Pasteurella multocida, Clostridium perfringens, Listeria monocytogenes, Campylobacter jejuni, Streptococcus suis, non‑pathogenic *E. coli* K‑12, porcine epidemic diarrhoea virus (PEDV), and porcine deltacoronavirus (PDCoV).  
   - Include the MEAET‑positive serum as a positive control and a confirmed negative serum as a negative control.  
   - Any sample giving an S/P ≥ 0.203 will be considered cross‑reactive.

10. **Data analysis**  
    - Export all OD₄₅₀ values to a CSV file.  
    - Using Python 3.10 (matplotlib 3.11), generate ROC curves for the assay based on the 16 positives vs. 24 negatives.  
    - Determine the optimal cut‑off by the maximal Youden index and report sensitivity, specificity, AUC, and 95 % confidence intervals (Wilson score).  
    - Calculate repeatability (intra‑assay CV) and reproducibility (inter‑assay CV) from the duplicate/triplicate readings.

11. **Reporting**  
    - Provide a summary table with: raw OD₄₅₀ values, calculated S/P ratios, classification (positive/negative), sensitivity at each dilution, specificity against each heterologous serum, ROC parameters, and CVs.  
    - Send the purified MEAET protein aliquots (concentration and storage details) back to us for our records.
