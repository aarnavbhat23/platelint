1. Germinate tomato seeds by surface-sterilizing with 75% ethanol for 2 min, incubating in 5% sodium hypochlorite for 10 min with gentle shaking, and rinsing 6-7 times with sterile distilled water. Sow seeds on ISTA-compliant germination paper in 9 cm culture dishes, add distilled water without submerging the seeds, and incubate in the dark at room temperature for 2-3 days until radicles emerge.

2. Transplant germinated seeds into square plastic pots containing a 1:1 volume ratio of vermiculite and peat. Grow seedlings in a chamber at 26°C / 18°C (16 h light / 8 h dark) with 60% relative humidity and 180–220 μmol m−2 s−1 light intensity until they reach the two-leaf, one-heart stage. Maintain controlled watering to prevent stress.

3. Perform mechanical wounding on healthy seedlings at the two-leaf, one-heart stage using 14 cm straight hemostatic forceps (sterilized with 75% ethanol between plants). Apply a single clamp with consistent pressure to the lamina of the first true leaf to induce visible drooping, ensuring the wounded area faces downward. 

4. Harvest three distinct sample types per genotype at 12 h post-treatment, with at least 8 seedlings per genotype: (i) the wounded leaflet of the first true leaf, (ii) the undamaged second true leaf from the same wounded plant, and (iii) undamaged leaves from independent, untreated control plants. Snap-freeze all harvested tissues immediately in liquid nitrogen and store at −80°C.

5. Grind frozen leaf tissue into a fine powder in a pre-cooled mortar using liquid nitrogen, ensuring tissues do not thaw. Transfer powder up to the 500 μL line in 1.5 mL centrifuge tubes.

6. Extract total RNA using Trizol reagent and column-based purification. Add 1 mL Trizol to the powder, incubate for 5 min, load onto adsorption columns, and centrifuge at 4°C and 13500 × g for 20 s. Wash the column once with 700 μL Buffer RW1 and twice with 500 μL Buffer RW2. Dry the column by centrifuging for 2 min at 13500 × g, then elute RNA in 30 μL of RNase-free water. Quantify RNA concentration via NanoDrop spectrophotometer and validate integrity using agarose gel electrophoresis before proceeding or storing at −80°C.

7. Perform genomic DNA removal and first-strand cDNA synthesis on ice. Combine 1 μg total RNA, 2 μL 5X gDNA Eraser Buffer, 2 μL gDNA Eraser, and adjust with ddH2O to 10 μL; incubate at 42°C for 2 min, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step. Add 10 μL of RT master mix (containing 4 μL 5× PrimeScript Buffer 2, 1 μL PrimeScript RT Enzyme Mix I, 1 μL RT Primer Mix, and 4 μL ddH2O), incubate at 37°C for 15 min, and terminate at 85°C for 5 s. Store cDNA at −80°C.

8. Set up 20 μL reactions for quantitative real-time PCR using the Kapa Biosystems SYBR Fast qPCR Kit and a Roche LightCycler 480 system. Each reaction should contain 2 μL cDNA, 10 μL SYBR Fast qPCR Kit, 0.5 μL of 0.25 μM forward primer, 0.5 μL of 0.25 μM reverse primer, and ddH2O to 20 μL. Cycle with an initial denaturation at 95°C for 30 s, followed by 40 cycles of 95°C for 5 s and 59°C for 30 s. Include melt curve analysis from 60°C to 95°C at 0.3°C/s with continuous fluorescence acquisition to verify amplicon specificity.

9. Normalize target gene expression levels against tomato ACTIN2. Perform statistical analysis using two-tailed Student’s t-tests for pairwise comparisons, or one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post-hoc tests for datasets with multiple groups.
