ABSTRACT
This protocol measures the optical density (OD600) of bacterial cultures as a proxy for cell density and growth, based on light scattering by cells in suspension. Cultures are diluted 1:10 in a well plate to remain within the linear range of the assay, and absorbance is measured at 600 nm on a microplate reader. Media-only blanks are subtracted from each sample reading to correct for background absorbance from the culture medium.
