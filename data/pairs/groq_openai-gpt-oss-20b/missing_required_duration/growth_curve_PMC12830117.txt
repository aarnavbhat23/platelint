Please run the Allo CAR70‑NKT cell production and in‑vitro RCC killing assay for our cord blood CD34+ HSPC samples (≈1 × 10⁶ cells per batch). Follow the steps below; use only sterile, filtered reagents and keep everything on ice or at 4 °C whenever noted. Store all prepared media in a tissue‑culture fridge and discard after one month (C10/R10) or two weeks (other media). 

**1. Prepare media** 
1.1 C10 medium (1 L): mix in a sterile 1‑L bottle – 848 mL RPMI‑1640, 100 mL FBS (10 %), 10 mL Pen/Strep/Glutamine 100×, 10 mL MEM non‑essential amino acids 100×, 10 mL HEPES 1 M, 10 mL sodium pyruvate 100 mM, 10 mL β‑mercaptoethanol 5 mM, 2 mL Normocin 500×. Filter 0.22 µm into the same bottle. 
1.2 R10 medium (1 L): thaw 100 mL FBS, 10 mL P/S/G 100×, 2 mL Normocin 500× in a 37 °C water bath. Add 888 mL RPMI‑1640. Filter 0.22 µm. 
1.3 HSPC culture medium: X‑VIVO 15 (50 mL) + SCF 50 ng/mL (2.5 µg), Flt3‑L 50 ng/mL (2.5 µg), TPO 50 ng/mL (2.5 µg), IL‑3 20 ng/mL (1 µg). Filter 0.22 µm. 
1.4 HSPC expansion medium: StemSpan SFEM II (45 mL) + Lymphoid Progenitor Expansion Supplement 10× (5 mL). Filter 0.22 µm. 
1.5 NKT differentiation medium: StemSpan SFEM II (45 mL) + Lymphoid Progenitor Maturation Supplement 10× (5 mL). Filter 0.22 µm. 
1.6 NKT deep differentiation medium: StemSpan SFEM II (45 mL) + Lymphoid Progenitor Maturation Supplement 10× (5 mL) + IL‑15 10 ng/mL (0.5 µg). Add CD3/CD28/CD2 activator 12.5 µL/mL at start of culture. Filter 0.22 µm. 
1.7 NKT expansion medium: homemade C10 (50 mL) + IL‑7 10 ng/mL (0.5 µg) + IL‑15 10 ng/mL (0.5 µg). Filter 0.22 µm. 

**2. RetroNectin (RN) coating (6‑well non‑TC treated plate)** 
2.1 Thaw RN (1 µg/µL) at −20 °C. Dilute to 20 µg/mL in sterile 1× PBS (1 mL per well). Incubate 20–25 °C. 
2.2 Aspirate RN, add 1 mL of 2 % BSA in PBS, incubate 30 min at 20–25 °C. 
2.3 Wash each well once with 2 mL 1× PBS. Use the plate the same day; it can be stored at 4 °C sealed up to 7 days. 

**3. HSPC thaw & transduction** 
3.1 Rapidly thaw CD34+ HSPCs in a 37 °C water bath. 
3.2 Add 500 µL pre‑warm X‑VIVO 15 dropwise to cells. Transfer to 15 mL tube. 
3.3 Centrifuge 300 × g, 10 min, discard supernatant, resuspend in 10 mL HSPC culture medium. Count viable cells. 
3.4 Centrifuge again, resuspend at 1 × 10⁶ cells/mL in Stage 0 medium (HSPC culture medium). 
3.5 Add 1 mL cell suspension per RN‑coated well (remove PBS first). Incubate 37 °C, 5 % CO₂, 12–18 h. 
3.6 Thaw concentrated lentivirus (Lenti/iNKT‑CAR70‑IL‑15; 1 × 10⁸ IFU/mL). Gently mix (no vortex) and add to wells (MOI = 100). Rock gently to distribute. 
3.7 Incubate 37 °C, 5 % CO₂, 24 h. 
3.8 Harvest cells: pipette gently, rinse each well with cold HSPC medium, pool. Centrifuge 300 × g, 10 min, resuspend in 10 mL HSPC culture medium. Count cells. 

**4. HSPC expansion (CellSTAR 24‑well plate, non‑TC)** 
4.1 Add 500 µL StemSpan™ Lymphoid Differentiation Coating Material per well, incubate 2 h 20–25 °C or 16–20 h 4 °C. 
4.2 Rinse with 1 mL PBS. 
4.3 Seed 500 µL of engineered HSPCs per well, culture 3 days at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂. 
4.4 Day 4: add 500 µL expansion medium, continue 4 days. 
4.5 Day 7: replace half medium with 500 µL fresh expansion medium. 
4.6 Day 11: repeat half‑change, culture 3 days. 
4.7 Day 14: harvest cells, count, resuspend at 1 × 10⁵ cells/mL in NKT differentiation medium. 

**5. NKT differentiation (Falcon 6‑well, non‑TC)** 
5.1 Coat with 1 mL differentiation coating per well, 2 h 20–25 °C or 16–20 h 4 °C, rinse with PBS. 
5.2 Seed 2 mL cell suspension per well (1 × 10⁵ cells/mL). Culture 1 week at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂. 
5.3 Passage 2–3× per week to keep 0.5–1 × 10⁶ cells/mL: pipette up/down 5 ×, spin 300 × g, 10 min, resuspend in fresh differentiation medium at 1 × 10⁵ cells/mL. 

**6. NKT deep differentiation (Falcon 6‑well, non‑TC)** 
6.1 Coat as above. 
6.2 Take cells from stage 3, count, resuspend at 5 × 10⁵ cells/mL in deep differentiation medium (add activator to 12.5 µL/mL). 
6.3 Seed 2 mL/well, culture 1 week at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂, passage 2–3× per week (same procedure, keep 5 × 10⁵ cells/mL). 

**7. NKT expansion with aAPCs (non‑precoated Falcon 6‑well)** 
7.1 Harvest aAPCs (K562 CD80/86/4‑1BBL/CD70), count, resuspend 1–5 × 10⁶ cells/mL in C10 medium, keep on ice, irradiate 10,000 rad. 
7.2 Combine irradiated aAPCs with NKT cells from deep differentiation at a 1:1–1:2 ratio. 
7.3 Add IL‑7 10 ng/mL and IL‑15 10 ng/mL to reach final 0.5–1 × 10⁶ cells/mL. 
7.4 Seed 2 mL per well, culture 2 weeks at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂. 
7.5 Passage 2–3×/week, replenish IL‑7/IL‑15 each time. 
7.6 At week 2, count cells, freeze in CS10 freezing medium at 1 × 10⁷ cells/mL. 

**8. In‑vitro RCC killing assay** 
8.1 Use 786‑O‑FG and ACHN‑FG tumor lines (ensure ≥95 % GFP‑positive). 
8.2 Harvest tumor cells, resuspend in C10, keep on ice. 
8.3 Thaw and resuspend Allo CAR70‑NKT cells in fresh C10, keep on ice. 
8.4 In a Corning 96‑well clear‑bottom black plate, set up 4 replicates per condition: E:T ratios 0:1, 0.1:1, 0.5:1, 1:1, 2:1, 5:1, 10:1 (use only CAR‑positive cells for normalization). 
8.5 Incubate 24 h (or 48 h if needed) at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂. 
8.6 Add 100 µL D‑luciferin (150 µg/mL) per well, incubate 5 min at 20–25 °C, protected from light. 
8.7 Read luciferase with Infinite M1000 (Tecan). Compare relative light units to control wells (tumor only). 

**Notes & QC** 
- No antibiotics in HSPC or NKT media; maintain strict sterility. 
- Daily monitor culture morphology, viability (trypan blue or flow). 
- At the end of each stage, take 1 × 10⁵ cells for flow: stain for NKT TCR (6B11), CD3, TCRαβ, CAR70, CD4, CD8, viability dye. 
- Expected outcomes: >70 % CAR‑positive NKT after differentiation, >90 % viability; >95 % NKT after deep differentiation; >97 % after expansion.
