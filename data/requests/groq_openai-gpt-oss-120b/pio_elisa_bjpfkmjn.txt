Please run a SpL ELISA on our animal serum samples using the following protocol:

1. Coat a 96‑well microtitre plate overnight at 4 °C with 100 µL per well of unlabelled protein‑L (SpL) from *P. magnus* at 1 µg/mL in carbonate‑bicarbonate buffer, pH 9.6.  
2. Block the plate with 200 µL per well of 1 % (w/v) bovine serum albumin (BSA) in PBS; incubate 1 h at room temperature (RT). Wash the plate 4 × with PBS containing 0.05 % Tween‑20 (PBS‑Tween).  
3. Add 50 µL of each animal serum sample (adjusted to 1 mg/mL total Ig) to the appropriate wells, including at least three negative‑control wells (serum known to lack SpL‑binding antibodies). Incubate 1 h at RT. Wash 4 × with PBS‑Tween.  
4. Prepare peroxidase‑labeled SpL conjugate diluted 1:5 000 in PBS containing 5 % non‑fat dry milk. Add 50 µL to each well and incubate 1 h at RT. Wash 4 × with PBS‑Tween.  
5. Add 50 µL of 4 mg/mL o‑phenylenediamine (OPD) substrate solution to each well. Incubate 15 min at RT in the dark.  
6. Stop the reaction by adding 50 µL of 3 M H₂SO₄ to each well.  
7. Read the plate at 492 nm on a microplate reader. Record the optical densities.  
8. Calculate the cut‑off value as three times the mean OD of the negative‑control wells. Any sample with an OD above this cut‑off is considered positive for SpL‑binding antibodies.  

We will provide the unlabelled protein‑L, peroxidase‑SpL conjugate, BSA, OPD solution, and H₂SO₄. Please let us know if any additional reagents or consumables are needed. Thank you.
