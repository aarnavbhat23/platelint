Please run the following experiment on our 3K‑SNCA SH‑SY5Y cells (both differentiated and undifferentiated).  
Use 6‑well plates and perform each condition in triplicate.  
For each well, seed ~1.5 × 10⁶ cells.  
After differentiation (our standard 5‑day retinoic acid protocol), harvest cells and place directly into RLT buffer (1 mL per well + β‑mercaptoethanol).  

**RNA extraction (Qiagen RNeasy Mini Kit)**  
1. Lyse cells in the plate, homogenise, and load onto RNeasy spin columns.  
2. Wash with RW1 and RPE, dry spin.  
3. Elute in 30–50 µL RNase‑free water. Measure A260/280; keep RNA at –80 °C until use.  

**cDNA synthesis (QuantiTect Reverse Transcription Kit)**  
4. Prepare RT master mix on ice according to kit instructions.  
5. Combine 500 ng–1 µg RNA with RT mix (final volume 10–12 µL).  
6. Perform genomic DNA wipeout if needed, then incubate per kit cycle. Keep reaction on ice afterward.  

**qPCR (TaqMan Gene Expression Master Mix on QuantStudio 1)**  
7. For each assay (HEXA, GLA, GAPDH) prepare master mix: 1 × Master Mix, 1 × 20 × Assay, nuclease‑free water to 20 µL.  
8. Dispense into 96‑well plate (triplicates) and add 2 µL cDNA template to each well.  
9. Seal, spin briefly to remove bubbles.  
10. Run on QuantStudio 1: 95 °C 10 min, then 40 cycles of 95 °C 15 s, 60 °C 60 s (data acquisition), and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step.

**Controls**  
- No‑template control (NTC) and –RT controls for each assay.  
- Use GAPDH as reference gene.  

**Analysis**  
- Verify amplification plots; no signal in NTC/–RT.  
- Compute ΔCt = Ct(target) – Ct(GAPDH).  
- Choose undifferentiated cells as calibrator; ΔΔCt = ΔCt(sample) – mean ΔCt(calibrator).  
- Fold change = 2^(–ΔΔCt).  
- Report mean ± SEM for biological replicates.  

**Deliverables**  
1. Raw Ct values (Excel file) for all samples and controls.  
2. Calculated ΔCt, ΔΔCt, fold‑change values.  
3. Summary table with means, SEM, and any statistical tests performed.  

Please follow the standard lab SOP for RNase control, and let me know if you need any additional information or supplies. Thank you.
