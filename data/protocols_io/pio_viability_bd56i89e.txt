ABSTRACT
CellTiter-Glo® Luminescent Cell Viability Assay used for measuring cytotoxicity of compounds of interest.

MATERIALS
MATERIALS
 CellTiter-Glo(R) Luminescent Cell Viability, 10ml Promega Catalog #G7570

STEPS
1. Treat the cells (seeded in a 96 well white walled multiwell plate) as required.
3. Prepare reagent by adding Caspase Glo buffer to the CellTiter-Glo substrate.
4. Add equal volume of reagent to samples ( 100 µL reagent to 100 µL 
 media in 96 well plate). Include media only control.
2. Equilibriate reagents and plate at Room temperature for at least 00:30:00 mins.
5. Place plate on orbital shaker for 00:02:00 min and subsequently incubate at Room temperature 
 for 00:10:00 min.
6. Record luminescence.
