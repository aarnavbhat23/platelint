Please prepare and run the Allo CAR70‑NKT generation and the in‑vitro RCC killing assay on our samples according to the following steps. 

**1. Media preparation** 
1.1 C10 medium: 
- 848 mL RPMI 1640 (no phenol red) 
- 100 mL FBS (10 %) 
- 10 mL Penicillin‑Streptomycin‑Glutamine (100×, 1× final) 
- 10 mL MEM non‑essential amino acids (100×, 1×) 
- 10 mL HEPES buffer (1 M, 10 mM final) 
- 10 mL sodium pyruvate (100 mM, 1 mM final) 
- 10 mL β‑mercaptoethanol (5 mM, 50 µM final) 
- 2 mL Normocin (500×, 1×) 
Combine in a sterile 1 L bottle, mix thoroughly, filter through a 0.22 µm filter into the same bottle. Store at 4 °C, use within 1 month. 

1.2 R10 medium: 
- 888 mL RPMI 1640 
- 100 mL FBS (10 %) 
- 10 mL Penicillin‑Streptomycin‑Glutamine (100×, 1×) 
- 2 mL Normocin (500×, 1×) 
Filter through a 0.22 µm filter into a sterile 1 L bottle. Store at 4 °C, use within 1 month. 

1.3 HSPC culture medium: 
- X‑VIVO™ 15 (serum‑free) 
- 50 ng/mL stem cell factor (SCF) (2.5 µg) 
- 50 ng/mL Flt3‑ligand (2.5 µg) 
- 50 ng/mL thrombopoietin (TPO) (2.5 µg) 
- 20 ng/mL interleukin‑3 (IL‑3) (1 µg) 
Filter through a 0.22 µm filter. For seeding, resuspend 1 × 10⁴ thawed CD34⁺ HSPCs in 300 µL of medium. Store 4 °C, use within 2 weeks. 

1.4 HSPC expansion medium: 
- StemSpan™ SFEM II 
- 10× StemSpan™ Lymphoid Progenitor Expansion Supplement (add 5 mL per 50 mL medium) 
Filter through a 0.22 µm filter. For culture, resuspend 2 × 10⁴ transduced CD34⁺ HSPCs per 500 µL of medium. Store 4 °C, use within 2 weeks. 

1.5 NKT differentiation medium: 
- StemSpan™ SFEM II 
- 10× StemSpan™ Lymphoid Progenitor Maturation Supplement (add 5 mL per 50 mL medium) 
Filter. Resuspend 1 × 10⁵ Stage 2 NKT cells per 500 µL. Store 4 °C, use within 2 weeks. 

1.6 NKT deep differentiation medium: 
- StemSpan™ SFEM II 
- 10× StemSpan™ Lymphoid Progenitor Maturation Supplement (5 mL per 50 mL) 
- 10 ng/mL recombinant human IL‑15 (500 ng per 50 mL) 
- 12.5 µL/mL ImmunoCult™ Human CD3/CD28/CD2 T Cell Activator (add 12.5 µL per 1 mL) 
Filter. Resuspend 5 × 10⁵–1 × 10⁶ Stage 2 NKT cells per mL. Store 4 °C, use within 2 weeks. 

1.7 NKT expansion medium: 
- C10 medium 
- 10 ng/mL human IL‑7 (500 ng per 50 mL) 
- 10 ng/mL human IL‑15 (500 ng per 50 mL) 
Filter. Use at 0.5–1 × 10⁶ cells/mL. Store 4 °C, use within 2 weeks. 

**2. HSPC engineering (Day 0–2)** 
2.1 Thaw 1 µg/µL Retronectin (RN) at −20 °C. Dilute to 20 µg/mL in sterile 1× PBS. 
2.2 Dispense 1 mL RN solution into each well of a 6‑well, non‑tissue‑culture–treated plate. Incubate 20–25 °C, 2 h. 
2.3 Aspirate RN, add 1 mL 2 % BSA in PBS, incubate 30 min. 
2.4 Remove blocking solution, wash wells 2 mL PBS. Use RN‑coated plate the same day. 

2.5 Rapidly thaw human CD34⁺ HSPCs in a 37 °C water bath. Add 500 µL pre‑warmed X‑VIVO 15 dropwise, transfer to a 15 mL conical tube. 
2.6 Centrifuge 300 × g, 10 min, discard supernatant, resuspend pellet in 10 mL HSPC culture medium. Count viable cells. 
2.7 Centrifuge again, resuspend at 1 × 10⁶ cells/mL in Stage 0 medium (same as HSPC culture medium but without cytokines). 
2.8 Remove PBS from RN wells, seed 1 mL cell suspension per well. Incubate 37 °C, 5 % CO₂ for 12–18 h. 

2.9 Thaw concentrated lentiviral supernatant (Lenti/iNKT‑CAR70‑IL‑15 1) to reach 1 × 10⁸ IFU/mL (MOI ≈ 100). Gently mix by pipetting, add to each well, tilt gently to distribute. 
2.10 Incubate 37 °C, 5 % CO₂. 

2.11 Gently pipette to detach cells, transfer to a 15 mL tube. Rinse wells with cold HSPC culture medium, pool all washes into the same tube. 
2.12 Centrifuge 300 × g, 10 min, discard medium, resuspend pellet in 10 mL HSPC culture medium. Count viable cells. 

2.13 For transduction efficiency, culture 1 × 10⁵ HSPCs for 3 days in 1 mL Stage 0 medium, then fix, permeabilize, stain intracellularly for Vβ11, analyze by flow. 

2.14 Resuspend cells in HSPC expansion medium at 2 × 10⁴ cells per 500 µL for the next stage. 

**3. HSPC expansion (Day 3–14)** 
3.1 Thaw StemSpan™ Lymphoid Differentiation Coating Material at 20–25 °C. Add 500 µL to each well of a CELLSTAR® 24‑well, non‑Tissue‑culture–treated plate. Incubate 2 h at 20–25 °C or 16–20 h at 4 °C. 
3.2 Remove coating solution, rinse with 1 mL PBS. 
3.3 Transfer engineered HSPCs 500 µL/well. Culture 3 days 37 °C, 5 % CO₂. 
3.4 Day 4: add 500 µL expansion medium to each well, continue 4 days. 
3.5 Monitor for overgrowth (medium yellowing or >70 % confluence). If needed, collect cells, centrifuge 300 × g, 10 min, resuspend in fresh expansion medium, split into two wells. 
3.6 Day 7: remove half the medium, replace with 500 µL fresh expansion medium. Continue 4 days. 
3.7 Day 11: repeat half‑medium change, continue 3 days. 
3.8 Day 14: harvest cells, count, centrifuge 300 × g, 10 min. Resuspend in NKT differentiation medium at 1 × 10⁵ cells/mL for the next stage. 

**4. NKT differentiation (Day 15–22)** 
4.1 Thaw StemSpan™ Lymphoid Differentiation Coating Material at 20–25 °C. Add 1 mL to each well of a Falcon® 6‑well, non‑Tissue‑culture‑treated plate. Incubate 2 h at 20–25 °C or 16–20 h at 4 °C. 
4.2 Remove coating, rinse with 1 mL PBS. 
4.3 Transfer Allo CAR70‑NKT cells 2 mL per well in NKT differentiation medium. 
4.4 Culture 1 week 37 °C, 5 % CO₂.
