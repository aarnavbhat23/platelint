1. We have 60 bacterial isolates from glycerol stocks that need to be tested against one compound (e.g., 6‑Methoxy‑2‑benzoxazolinone). Prepare the run with three technical replicates per strain, 8 treatment conditions (seven concentrations + solvent control), plus no‑bacteria controls (6 per plate) and a media‑only control plate. Use the existing stacker/plate‑reader set‑up (BioStack 4 + Synergy H1) with the 96‑channel Liquidator 96 TM.

2. Media:  
   a. Half‑strength TSB – 15 g TSB powder per litre of deionised water, autoclave 20 min 121 °C, keep room temperature.  
   b. TSA – 30 g TSB + 15 g agar per litre, autoclave 20 min 121 °C. Make 1.5 L for 60 × 25 mL plates (two 750 mL bottles).  
   c. Make a 250 mL half‑strength TSB for preculture plates (3.75 g per 250 mL).

3. Plate preparation:  
   a. Pour 25 mL TSA into 60 × 9 cm petri dishes. Let dry 15 min, seal with parafilm.  
   b. Streak each glycerol stock onto its plate with a sterile loop, dry, seal.  
   c. Incubate 4–10 days at 28 °C (keep age consistent).  
   d. Prepare two deep‑well 96‑well plates (2 mL, round‑bottom, Carl Roth EN07.1). Sterilise by 15 min UV.  
   e. Label plates A & B, add 1 mL half‑strength TSB to each well (8 channel pipette).  
   f. Using an inoculation needle, transfer a few cells from each agar plate into the wells. Seal with breathable foil.  
   g. Incubate at 28 °C, 180 rpm for 4 days, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step.

4. Treatment solutions (8 concentrations + solvent):  
   a. Dissolve the compound in DMSO to 500 mM, filter‑sterilise (0.20 µm).  
   b. For each of 8 concentrations (0, 125, 250, 500, 750, 1 250, 2 500, 5 000 µM), prepare 45 mL half‑strength TSB in a 50 mL tube.  
   c. Add appropriate volume of 500 mM stock to reach target µM, keeping solvent at 1 % (e.g., 450 µL stock for 5 000 µM).  
   d. For the solvent control, add 45 mL half‑strength TSB + 450 µL DMSO.  
   e. Prepare fresh just before use.

5. Stacker plate set‑up (19 plates total):  
   a. Media control: 96‑well plate, 200 µL half‑strength TSB per well (96‑channel pipette).  
   b. For each of 8 concentrations × 2 preculture plates (A & B), fill two stacker plates (96‑well, 200 µL). Use the 96‑channel pipette: first fill a reservoir with the corresponding concentration solution, then dispense 196 µL into each well.  
   c. After dispensing media, discard tips.  
   d. Switch pipetting volume to 4 µL. From preculture plate A, pipette 4 µL into each well of the corresponding A stacker plates, starting with lowest concentration. Repeat for plate B. Use fresh tips each time.  
   e. Add empty stacker plates to top and bottom as evaporation/handling controls.  
   f. Prepare a label list (labellist.xlsx) with plate order, strain, concentration, and replicate information.  

6. Load the stack (A1‑well always in top‑left of each plate) into the stacker input rack, lock, and start Gen5.  
   a. Use protocol “GrowthCurveOD600.prt” (provided). Confirm: Read 600 nm, Shake linear 2 min.  
   b. Tick “use stacker and plates have lids”, untick “leave lids on during read”.  
   c. Start run; run 68 h (or adjust if growth slower).  
   d. Monitor for blockages; check NBCs and media control periodically.  
   e. After 68 h, stop, export all data as Excel (Sample_data_HT_Chem_Pheno.xlsx).  

7. Waste: discard all plates with bacterial material according to biosafety guidelines.

8. Analysis: We will use the supplied R script (HT_Chem_Pheno.rmd) with Strain_Data.xlsx and labellist.xlsx to calculate AUC, normalise, and compute tolerance index (TI). TI >0.75 tolerant, 0.5–0.75 intermediate, <0.5 susceptible. Please provide the exported data and the label list. Let us know if any issues arise (e.g., evaporation, contamination). Thank you.
