ABSTRACT
This protocol provides a viability measurement for 3D culture.

MATERIALS
ATP (Mw = 551.14 g/mol) (A7699-1G, Sigma-Aldrich)
 spheroids
 Incomplete DMEM/F-12 culture medium without FBS (1%L-Glutamine, 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin) (Gibco 21331-020)
 CellTiter-Glo‱ 3D Cell Viability Assay (Promega G968A)
 Aluminium
 Shaker
 Opaque 96-well plate for reading (Costar 96 Flat Bottom White Polystyrene)
 Luminescence reader (Tecan Infinite M Plex)

STEPS
## Preparation of the CellTiter-Glo 3D Solution
1. ● Thaw the CellTiter-Glo reagent in thefridge at 4°C the day before the experiment.
## Preparation of the CellTiter-Glo 3D Solution
2. ● Before use, let the kit reach roomtemperature for 30 minutes.
## Preparation of the CellTiter-Glo 3D Solution
3. ● Gentlymix the solution.
## Preparation of ATP Solutions at Different Concentrations for the Calibration Curve
4. Dilution of the ATP Solution
## Preparation of ATP Solutions at Different Concentrations for the Calibration Curve
5. ● Transfer the ATP solutions into theopaque 96-well plate for luminescence
## Preparation of Spheroids in the Opaque Plate
6. ● Transfer the spheroids from theincubation plate to the opaque 96-well plate for reading.
## Preparation of Spheroids in the Opaque Plate
7. ● Place the plate on the 28-magnetplate (Plate Holding) and aspirate the medium.
## Preparation of Spheroids in the Opaque Plate
8. ● Add 50 µL of DMEM without FBS perwell.
## Addition of CellTiter-Glo and Incubation of the Opaque Plate
9. ● Add 50 µL of CellTiter-Glo per well(into wells containing 50 µL of DMEM without FBS).
## Addition of CellTiter-Glo and Incubation of the Opaque Plate
10. ● Cover the plate with aluminum foil toprotect the samples from light, as CellTiter-Glo is light-sensitive.
## Addition of CellTiter-Glo and Incubation of the Opaque Plate
11. ● Incubate the plate at room temperaturefor 25 minutes.
## Addition of CellTiter-Glo and Incubation of the Opaque Plate
12. ● During this incubation period, shakethe plate on a vortex for 5 minutes at 2 rpm.
## Luminescence Reading
13. ● Setup the device:
 ○ Shakingmode: Orbital shaking
 ○ Shakingtime: 3 s
 ○ Attenuation:None
 ○ Integrationtime: 1000 ms
 ○ Settletime: 0 ms
 ○ Temperature:22°C
## Luminescence Reading
14. ● Place the plate without the lid onthe reader platform and position the plate.
## Luminescence Reading
15. ● Start the reading and save the Excelfile.
