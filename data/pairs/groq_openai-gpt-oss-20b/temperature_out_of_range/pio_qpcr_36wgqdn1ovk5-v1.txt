Please run a 384‑well qPCR assay on our samples using the following reagents and workflow.  The sample IDs, gene list, and plate layout will be sent with the order.

**Reagents (to be prepared fresh or as indicated)**  
- 1:10 diluted cDNA (2 µL per well; dilute 1 µL cDNA in 9 µL MilliQ H₂O)  
- SYBR Green PCR Master Mix (12 µL reaction per well):  
  - 6 µL SYBR Green  
  - 2 µL primer mix (2.5 µM each primer; 1.25 µM final per primer in the 10 µL reaction)  
  - 2 µL MilliQ H₂O  
  - 2 µL 1:10 diluted cDNA (see above)  
- Adhesive plate film (sticky film) and a clear adhesive plate cover  
- 384‑well PCR plate (black, optical)  
- 200 µL filtered pipette tips (two boxes for one plate)  
- Optional: 8‑tube strips for primer stock preparation (if needed)

**Equipment**  
- Electronic multichannel pipette (Hilda)  
- Centrifuge (capable of 3500 rpm, 4 °C)  
- ABI 7900 Prism or equivalent real‑time PCR system with SDS 2.3 software  
- Kimwipe for blotting

**Procedure**

1. **cDNA preparation**  
   1.1 Thaw cDNA on ice, vortex briefly, and spin down.  
   1.2 Dilute 1 µL cDNA in 9 µL MilliQ H₂O to obtain 1:10 stock.  

2. **Master mix preparation**  
   2.1 In a 1.5 mL Eppendorf tube per gene, combine:  
        – 2 µL water  
        – 6 µL SYBR Green  
        – 2 µL primer mix (2.5 µM each primer)  
        – 2 µL 1:10 diluted cDNA  
   2.2 Mix gently, aliquot the 12 µL reaction into the reservoir of a 10 µL multichannel pipette (keep on ice).  

3. **Dispensing**  
   3.1 Use the 10 µL multichannel pipette to dispense 10 µL of the cDNA/Master mix into each well of the 384‑well plate according to the pre‑prepared layout.  
   3.2 Keep the plate on ice during dispensing.  

4. **Plate handling**  
   4.1 Gently blot the top of the plate with a Kimwipe to prevent cross‑contamination.  
   4.2 Place the clear adhesive plate cover over the plate, smoothing with a brown “helper” film from the centre outward, taking care at the edges.  
   4.3 Spin the plate for 5 min at 3500 rpm, 4 °C.  
   4.4 Seal the plate with the sticky film. Vortex briefly, then spin again 5 min at 3500 rpm, 4 °C.  

5. **Program set‑up (SDS 2.3)**  
   5.1 File → New, select Real Time.  
   5.2 Connect to the instrument, open the door, and insert the plate, aligning A1 to A1.  
   5.3 Close the door.  
   5.4 In the layout tab, highlight unused wells and click “Omit Wells.”  
   5.5 Highlight used wells and click “Add Detector” for each gene.  
   5.6 Set the reaction volume to 10 µL.  
   5.7 Program the thermal profile:  
        – 95 °C for 2 min (initial denaturation)  
        – 40 cycles: 95 °C for 10 s, 60 °C for 1 min (extension)  
        – Dissociation (melt curve): 95 °C to 60 °C, -80 °C steps, 5 s hold per step.  

6. **Run**  
   6.1 Start the run and monitor as normal.  

7. **Primer validation (optional, if requested)**  
   7.1 Prepare a standard curve for each gene:  
        – Add 10 µL of primer (+) and 10 µL of primer (–) to 180 µL MilliQ H₂O.  
        – In 1.5 mL tubes, mix the PCR master mix per the table (2 µL water, 6 µL SYBR, 2 µL primers, 2 µL cDNA).  
        – Run these in 8‑tube strips in parallel with the main assay to generate standard curves.  

**Deliverables**  
- Completed qPCR run data (raw Ct values, melt curves).  
- Primer efficiency calculations if standard curves were run.  
- Any issues observed during the run (e.g., plate sealing, pipetting errors).
