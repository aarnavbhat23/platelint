Please run a qPCR assay on our cDNA samples as follows:

1. Plate each of our cDNA samples into a 96‑well qPCR plate, using 1 µL of sample per well. Plate each sample 2–4 times to provide technical replicates.
2. For each well, prepare the reaction mix on ice:
   * 5 µL Fast SYBR Green Master Mix (Applied Biosystems, #4385616)
   * 3 mL nuclease‑free water
   * 0.5 µL forward primer (5′‑GTTCGCCCCCTTTAATAGTGC‑3′)
   * 0.5 µL reverse primer (5′‑TGAACTCCAACGTCAAGCGG‑3′)
   * 1 µL of the cDNA sample
3. Include a negative control well for each plate: 5 µL Master Mix, 3 µL water, 0.5 µL forward primer, 0.5 µL reverse primer, and no template.
4. Run the qPCR on a Real‑Time PCR system, collecting Ct values for each well.
5. After data acquisition, normalize the Ct values for Atg7 to GAPDH (housekeeping gene) by ΔCt = Ct(Atg7) – Ct(GAPDH).
6. Provide us with the raw Ct values and the ΔCt values for each replicate. If you calculate ΔΔCt relative to a control sample, please include that as well.
