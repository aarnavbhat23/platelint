Please run an SpL ELISA on our provided serum samples using the following procedure:

1. Coat a 96-well microtiter plate overnight at 4°C with unlabeled *P. magnus* protein-L (SpL) at 1 µg per well in carbonate-bicarbonate buffer (pH 9.6).
2. Block the plate with a bovine serum albumin (BSA) solution, then wash 4 times with PBS-Tween.
3. Add 50 µL of sample serum (normalized to 1 mg/mL) to each well, along with negative controls. Incubate for 1 hour at room temperature, then wash 4 times with PBS-Tween.
4. Add 50 µL per well of peroxidase-labeled SpL conjugate diluted 1:5000 in PBS containing non-fat milk. Incubate for 1 hour at room temperature, then wash 4 times with PBS-Tween.
5. Add 50 µL of 4 mg/mL o-phenylenediamine (OPD) substrate solution per well and incubate for 15 minutes at room temperature in the dark.
6. Stop the reaction with 50 µL of 3M H2SO4 per well.
7. Visually inspect color development, read absorbance at 492 nm on a microplate reader, and calculate the cut-off threshold as 3 times the mean OD of the negative controls. Please send us the raw OD data and the calculated cut-off values.
