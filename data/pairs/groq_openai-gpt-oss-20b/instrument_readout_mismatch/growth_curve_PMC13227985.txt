1. Print the agar pipette (AP) devices from the provided STL file (File S1) using a Form 3+ stereolithography printer.  Use Form labs clear V4 resin.  Print ten devices at a time with full support, 12 touchpoints, a 0.1 mm layer height, and 468 layers.  After printing cure the pieces in the Form Cure machine at 405 nm, 60 °C for 15 min.  Autoclave each AP once at 121 °C, 16 psi, 30 min.  Store the APs sterile until use; they can be reused after each autoclave.

2. Prepare the substrate–agar media for the Agar‑Plug Extraction (APE) assay.  For each substrate, mix the substrate with 3 % (wt/vol) agar in a test tube.  The substrate concentrations should be: 0.2 % (wt/vol) glucose (control); 0.2 % (wt/vol) barley β‑glucan; 0.2 % (wt/vol) potato starch; 2 % (wt/vol) autoclaved S. cerevisiae cells; 0.2 % (wt/vol) pectin; 0.2 % (wt/vol) potato galactan.  Autoclave the mixture (121 °C, 30 min, liquid cycle).  For any substrate with pH ≤ 5.0 sterilize the substrate separately (e.g., by filtering or autoclaving a separate solution) and mix after sterilization.  Measure pH with full‑range paper and record.

3. While the agar is still molten, transfer 135 µL of the sterile substrate–agar mix into each well of a sterile 96‑well flat‑bottom microplate (Corning 3370) using a multichannel pipette.  Seal the plate by covering it with a lid, sealing the seam with parafilm, and leave at 25 °C for 2 h to allow solidification.  After solidification, seal again with parafilm and store the plate at 4 °C overnight (~12 h) before use.  Plates can be stored up to 3 days at 4 °C; longer storage is possible with lid closed, parafilm‑sealed seam, and plate inverted.

4. For each experiment, attach a sterile AP to a Fisher Scientific rubber bulb (HS20629AF).  Using the AP, extract a 4 mm cylindrical agar plug from the center of each well, leaving ~50 µL of substrate agar in the well.  Discard the plug.

5. Add 148 µL of MOPs minimal media (TeKNova M2101) without any carbon source to each well.  Inoculate each well with a 1:100 dilution of the overnight bacterial culture you provide.  For environmental microbial assays, use 140 µL of M9 basal media (1× M9 salts + CaCl₂ + trace elements + FeCl₃ + MgSO₄) and inoculate with 10 µL of a colony suspension in 500 µL basal media.

6. Seal the plate with a lid and parafilm.  Incubate the plates at 30 °C with high aeration (≈200 rpm).  For OD measurements, use a BioTek EPOCH 2 plate reader set to 30 °C, 205 rpm.  Measure OD₆₀₀ at the start (time 0) and every 24 h for the required duration (typically 5–7 days).  Record data for each well and each biological replicate (three replicates per condition).  Use GraphPad Prism 9 (default settings) to calculate growth rates and standard deviations.

7. For the N‑acetylglucosaminidase (NAGase) assay, prepare a separate 96‑well plate with 3 % (wt/vol) agar containing the substrate of interest (e.g., 0.2 % β‑glucan).  Add 1 µL of β‑N‑acetylglucosaminidase S (NEB P0744L) to 1 µL of 0.18 % 4‑nitrophenyl N‑acetyl‑β‑D‑glucosaminide (Millipore Sigma N9376) per well.  Supplement each well with 1× Glycobuffer (5 mM CaCl₂, 50 mM sodium acetate).  Incubate at 37 °C, no shaking, and read luminescence every 15 min for 2 h with a BioTek EPOCH 2.  Include three negative controls (no enzyme, no substrate, neither).  Perform each assay in triplicate and plot absorbance curves in Prism.

8. For the cornstarch digestion and glucose detection assay, prepare a 96‑well plate with 0.2 % glucose, 0.5 % insoluble cornstarch, or agar only.  Add 148 µL MOPs minimal medium, then incubate at 40 °C for 24 h.  Add 2 µL (6 U) of thermostable α‑amylase (MegaZyme E‑BSTAA) to the appropriate wells.  After incubation, collect 100 µL from each well and quantify glucose using the Sigma‑Aldrich GAGO20‑1KT kit per the manufacturer’s instructions.  Run each assay in triplicate.

9. For biomass contaminant device (BCD) experiments with insoluble particulate substrates (e.g., 1 % filter paper or 1 % β‑chitin), weigh 50 mg of substrate into an 18 mm glass test tube.  Autoclave (121 °C, 16 psi, 30 min).  Add 5 mL of MOPs minimal media (no carbon).  Inoculate with a 1:100 dilution of the overnight culture.  Grow at 30 °C, 200 rpm.  Measure OD₆₀₀ every 24 h with a Spectronic 20D+ spectrophotometer.  Perform in triplicate and analyze with Prism.

10. Return all data sheets, raw OD readings, and any additional notes.  If any steps were ambiguous or if further clarification is needed, contact us promptly.
