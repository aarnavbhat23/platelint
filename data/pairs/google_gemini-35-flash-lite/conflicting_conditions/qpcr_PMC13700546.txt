Please process our 32 equine umbilical cord samples using our paired non-inferiority study design to compare the 3-hour reference digestion method (RM) against the 24-hour novel digestion method (NM). Follow the workflow outlined below.

1. Sample Collection and Preparation
- Clean the 20 cm allantoic umbilical cord segments under running 4 °C tap water for 5 to 10 minutes until macroscopically clean.
- Disinfect by submerging in 70% ethanol for 3 min with constant agitation, air-dry, submerge in 1% iodine solution for 3 min with constant motion, and thoroughly rinse in sterile PBS without Ca2+ and Mg2+ (PBS (−/−)) until clear.
- Submerge each sample in 500 mL of sterile transport solution (PBS (−/−) with 5% Pen/Strep, 1% Amphotericin B, and 0.1% Gentamycin), double bag, agitate heavily, and store overnight at 4 °C for 12 hours.

2. Tissue Preparation and Enzyme Digestion
- Rinse samples thoroughly in fresh transport solution, isolate the perivascular Wharton's jelly from vessels, intervascular Wharton’s jelly, and endothelium, and rinse again.
- Finely mince the tissue into 1–3 mm³ cubes.
- For each foal, divide the minced tissue equally to create paired samples for both the RM and NM treatments using the same batch of collagenase I.
- Submerge 10.5 ± 1.0 g of minced tissue into 50 mL centrifuge tubes containing enzyme solution (low-glucose DMEM with 500 U/mL collagenase I) at 2 mL/g.
- Incubate in a water bath at 37 °C for 3 h (RM) or 24 h (NM), vortexing regularly, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step.

3. Cell Isolation and Primary Culture
- Filter digested samples through a 2 mm sterile metal mesh, rinse with PBS (−/−), centrifuge at 600× g for 10 min at room temperature, filter through a 100 μm cell strainer, rinse, and centrifuge again.
- Resuspend the final cell pellet in basic cell culture medium (low-glucose DMEM, 20% FBS, 1% Pen/Strep, 1% Amphotericin B, and 1% Amphotericin B adjusted total).
- Seed cells into one T75 culture flask per sample.
- Culture at 37 °C with 5% CO2. Wash three times with PBS with Ca2+ and Mg2+ (PBS (+/+) 24 hours post-seeding, and continue culture with medium replacements and microscopic checks twice per week until 80–90% confluence.
- Score isolation success and contamination at passage 0 (P0). Discard both paired samples if either is contaminated at P0.

4. Passaging and Cryopreservation
- Detach cells using 0.25%/0.02% trypsin/EDTA for 10–15 min, split 1:3, and expand through passages.
- Cryopreserve passage 2 (P2) cells at 2 million cells/mL in cryopreservation medium (low-glucose DMEM, 40% FBS, 10% DMSO, 1% Pen/Strep, 1% Amphotericin B, 0.1% Gentamycin) using Mr. Frosty™ at −80 °C for 24 h before transferring to liquid nitrogen.
- Record inter-passage time (IPT) in days from isolation to cryopreservation across passages for 20 paired samples.

5. Characterization and Assays (Using N = 15 paired samples for flow cytometry/RT-qPCR, and N = 3 paired samples at P4 for proliferation and differentiation)
- Population-Doubling Time (PDT): Seed 30,000 cells/well in 12-well plates. Harvest and count in duplicate daily up to day 8 using an Automated Cell Counter to determine PDT.
- Flow Cytometry: Detach cells using Accutase®, stain 1 × 105 cells in 96-well V-bottom plates using the specified panel for CD29, CD34, CD44, CD45, CD90.1, CD90.2, and CD105 alongside eF506 viability dye, and acquire on a BD FACSCanto II.
- RT-qPCR: Extract RNA, synthesize cDNA, and perform SYBR Green RT-qPCR on a ViiA7 Thermocycler using housekeeping genes (ACTB, GAPDH, RN18S) and stemness markers (POU5F1/OCT4, DNMT3B, DENND4A, MYC, THY-1).
- Trilineage Differentiation: 
  - Chondrogenic: 3D pellet culture of 5 × 105 cells in 15 mL vials with CD medium for 21 days, followed by formalin fixation, paraffin embedding, Alcian blue/H&E staining, and Bern score evaluation.
  - Osteogenic: Culture 20,000 cells/cm² in laminin-coated 24-well plates with OD medium for 21 days, followed by formalin fixation, von Kossa/nuclear fast red staining, and semi-quantitative scoring.
  - Adipogenic: Culture 20,000 cells/cm² in laminin-coated 24-well plates with StemPro™ Adipogenesis Kit medium supplemented with 5% rabbit serum, 1% Pen/Strep, 1% Amphotericin B, and 0.1% Gentamycin for 7 days, followed by formalin fixation, Nile red/DAPI staining, and scoring.
