Please run a double viability and total cell mass assay using Alphazurine A and Crystal Violet on our treated mammalian cell cultures. Use 12-well or 24-well clear flat-bottom TC-treated plates, maintaining a minimum of four technical replicates per condition. 

For each assay plate, include dedicated control wells consisting of an all-live cells control (untreated) and an all-dead cells control. Induce the dead cell control by removing the medium, adding 300 µL of 50% ethanol for 3 to 5 minutes at room temperature, and then replacing it with fresh culture medium. For the test wells, replace the spent medium with fresh culture medium before starting the staining procedure.

Perform the Alphazurine A assay according to the following steps:
1. Centrifuge the culture plate at 1200 rpm for 2 minutes.
2. Add Alphazurine A (using MP Biomedicals ref #ICNA0215473025 or ThermoFisher ref #AC189370100) directly to the fresh medium in all wells to reach a final concentration of 0.2 mg/ml (from a 10 mg/ml water stock).
3. Incubate the plate for 40 minutes at 37 °C.
4. Centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes.
5. Rinse the wells three times with 1X calcium- and magnesium-free PBS, centrifuging at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes after each rinse, and remove the final supernatant.
6. Elute the dye by adding a volume of 50% ethanol / 1% acetic acid solution equivalent to the cell culture medium volume.
7. Incubate for 30 minutes at room temperature on a shaker, then centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 2 minutes.
8. Transfer 200 µL of the supernatant into a 96-well plate and measure the absorbance at 637 nm.

Immediately follow with the Crystal Violet assay on the same plates:
1. Remove the remaining Alphazurine eluate from the culture plate and rinse once with PBS.
2. Add one volume (equivalent to the culture medium volume) of Crystal Violet solution (Sigma-Aldrich ref #C0775) at 4 µg/ml in PBS.
3. Incubate for 30 minutes at room temperature.
4. Centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes.
5. Rinse the wells three times with PBS, centrifuging at 1200 rpm for 3 minutes after each rinse, and remove the final supernatant.
6. Elute by adding a volume of 50% ethanol / 1% acetic acid solution equivalent to the culture medium volume.
7. Incubate for 45 minutes at room temperature on a shaker, then centrifuge at 1200 rpm for 2 minutes.
8. Transfer 200 µL of the supernatant into a 96-well plate and measure the absorbance at 590 nm.

Please provide the raw absorbance readings at 637 nm and 590 nm for all replicates, along with the calculated fraction of live cells relative to the untreated controls.
