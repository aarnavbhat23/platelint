Please process the ascitic fluid samples from our cirrhosis patients as follows:

1. **Sample receipt and handling**  
   * Receive 60 mL of ascitic fluid per patient, already collected after admission.  
   * Work immediately; keep all fluid at 4 °C and transport to the lab within 2 h.

2. **Conventional culture**  
   * Aliquot 10 mL of fluid into each of the paired BD BACTEC Plus Aerobic/F and BD BACTEC Lytic/10 Anaerobic/F blood culture bottles.  
   * Incubate bottles in a BD BACTEC FX system at 37 °C for 5 days.  
   * Record time‑to‑positivity (TTP) when the system signals positivity.  
   * When a bottle alerts positive, subculture 2–3 drops of the broth onto a blood agar plate and place immediately in the appropriate atmosphere (aerobic, CO₂, or anaerobic).  
   * Incubate plates at 35 °C, examining twice daily for 48 h.  
   * Identify colonies by MALDI‑TOF MS.  
   * Perform antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) by VITEK 2 or Etest; if needed, confirm with broth microdilution.  
   * Interpret results per CLSI M100‑Ed34 breakpoints.

3. **Modified culture (to be run in parallel)**  
   * Take 20 mL of fluid, centrifuge 8,000 rpm for 3 min.  
   * Wash pellet once with sterile PBS, then resuspend in ~100 µL PBS.  
   * Treat with 0.5 % Triton X‑100 (prepared in PBS) for 1 min, then recentrifuge 8,000 rpm for 3 min.  
   * Resuspend final pellet in ~100 µL PBS and inoculate onto equal amounts on one chocolate agar plate and one blood agar plate.  
   * Incubate these plates in 5 % CO₂ at 35 °C, inspecting twice daily for 48 h.  

4. **Anaerobic processing (same 20 mL aliquot)**  
   * Repeat the above centrifuge‑wash‑Triton step.  
   * Plate the resuspended pellet onto both blood and chocolate agar.  
   * Immediately place plates into a 2.5 L anaerobic jar equipped with a 2.5 L anaerobic gas‑producing bag and an oxygen indicator.  
   * Seal the jar within 1 min of plating; confirm color change of the indicator.  
   * Incubate at 35 °C, inspecting twice daily for 48 h.  

5. **Identification of isolates**  
   * For any colonies grown on modified or anaerobic plates, perform 16S rRNA PCR using primers 27F (AGAGTTTGATCCTGGCTCAG) and 1492R (GGTTACCTTGTTACGACTT).  
   * Identify species by sequencing or MALDI‑TOF as appropriate.

6. **Antimicrobial susceptibility**  
   * Use disk diffusion on Mueller‑Hinton agar per CLSI M02‑Ed13.  
   * If zones are indeterminate, or an MIC is required, perform broth microdilution following CLSI M07‑Ed12.  
   * Interpret all results with CLSI M100‑Ed34 breakpoints.

7. **Controls & contamination assessment**  
   * Run negative controls (PBS) with each batch.  
   * Record any growth of common laboratory contaminants (Bacillus spp., Cutibacterium spp., Lactobacillus spp., Staphylococcus epidermidis); note these as contaminants if the patient has no clinical infection.

8. **Documentation**  
   * Record all incubation times, TTP, plate inspection dates, colony counts, identification results, and AST outcomes in the provided spreadsheet.  
   * Provide a brief narrative summary for each patient, indicating whether conventional or modified culture yielded a pathogen and any discrepancies.

Reagents and consumables needed:  
* BD BACTEC Plus Aerobic/F & BD BACTEC Lytic/10 Anaerobic/F bottles  
* BD BACTEC FX incubator  
* Blood agar, chocolate agar, Mueller‑Hinton agar plates  
* 2.5 L anaerobic jars, 2.5 L anaerobic gas bags, oxygen indicator strips  
* PBS, Triton X‑100 (0.5 % solution)  
* MALDI‑TOF MS access, 16S rRNA primers 27F/1492R, PCR reagents  
* VITEK 2 or Etest kits, broth microdilution panels  
* CLSI reference materials (M02, M07, M100)

Please complete the culture and AST for each sample according to the above steps and send the results back to us by the end of the week.
