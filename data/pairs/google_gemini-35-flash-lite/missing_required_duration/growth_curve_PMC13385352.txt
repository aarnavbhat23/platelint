1. Prepare MBD-fused and CXXC-fused Tn5 transposase constructs by amplifying the respective domains from organoid cDNA, OC cDNA clones, or synthesized sequences (TwistBiosciences), and cloning them into TXB1-pA/G-Tn5 using the specified restriction sites or Gibson assembly with designed primers.
2. Transform the sequence-verified constructs into Rosetta cells, express the fusion proteins in LB with Ampicillin and Chloramphenicol at 18 °C overnight after induction with 0.25 mM IPTG, and purify them using Chitin resin followed by dialysis, concentration, and dilution to 300–400 ng/µl in 50% glycerol (excluding CXXC-MLL1, which must be kept fresh without long-term glycerol storage).
3. Culture iPSCs on Matrigel in mTeSR Plus with penicillin/streptomycin, passage them using TrypLE or EDTA with ROCK inhibitor Y-27632, and maintain K562 cells in RPMI with 10% FCS and antibiotics.
4. Generate unguided brain organoids by aggregating 2000–3000 dissociated iPS cells in 96-well ultra-low attachment plates, culturing embryoid bodies in mTeSR Plus, adding neural induction medium when they reach 400–500 µm, and transitioning to retinoic acid-containing differentiation medium from day 40 onward.
5. Perform DNMT1 inhibitor treatment on WIBJ2 iPSCs by adding 5 µM GSK3484862 to the culture medium for two weeks, splitting regularly to deplete DNA methylation.
6. Prepare single-cell suspensions from human brain organoids using the Neural Tissue Dissociation Kit (P) with papain and DNase, and dissociate zebrafish embryos using chilled buffer and mechanical pipetting after pronase dechorionation.
7. Execute standard CUT&Tag for histone modifications and 5mC starting from 0.1–1 million cells or isolated nuclei: bind samples to BioMag Concanavalin A beads, permeabilize with 0.01% digitonin in wash buffer, incubate overnight at 4 °C with primary antibodies and 2 mM EDTA, add secondary antibody for 1 h at 4 °C, bind pA/G-Tn5 (600 ng) at 20 °C for 1 h, activate tagmentation with 10 mM MgCl₂ at 37 °C for 1 h, and stop with EDTA, SDS, and Proteinase K.
8. Perform $C_me$ CUT&Tag on purified genomic DNA (1–50 ng) or nuclei (0.1–1 million cells) by incubating samples with 600 ng of MBD-fused or CXXC-fused Tn5 transposase in CUT&Tag med buffer at 4 °C, washing, tagmenting with 10 mM MgCl₂ at 37 °C for 1 h, and purifying with the ChIP DNA Clean & Concentrator kit while adding 10 pg of Tn5-digested lambda DNA spike-in during elution.
9. For single-nucleus $C_me$ CUT&Tag, lyse 2 million dissociated cells with 0.01% digitonin, pool equal nuclei numbers from iPSC, K562, and organoid lines, incubate with MBD-Tn5, perform tagmentation with 10 mM MgCl₂, terminate with Chromium Single Cell ATAC buffers, load 10k nuclei onto the Chromium Next GEM Chip, and process according to the manufacturer's protocol.
10. For $C_me$ CUT&Tag followed by bisulfite or enzymatic conversion, perform initial tagmentation using MBD-Tn5 loaded with single fully methylated adapters (Tn5ME-A or Tn5ME rev combined with Tn5mC1.1-A1block), spike in unmethylated T7 DNA, replace the adapter via gap-filling with Tn5mC-ReplO1, dNTPs, T4 Polymerase, and Ampligase, and convert unmethylated cytosines using the EZ DNA Methylation Lightning Kit or NEBNext Enzymatic Methyl-seq v2 Conversion Module.
11. Generate sequencing libraries for standard, conversion, and single-cell assays using NEBNext High-Fidelity 2x PCR Master Mix or KAPA HiFi Uracil Mastermix with Illumina i5/i7 indices (15–17 PCR cycles), purify with Ampure beads, and quality-control using Qubit DNA HS Assay and Agilent TapeStation prior to paired-end sequencing (2 × 75 bp).
12. Perform immunofluorescence stainings on inhibitor-treated iPSCs grown on poly-L-lysine coverslips by fixing with 4% PFA, performing antigen retrieval with HistoVT One at 50 °C, permeabilizing with 0.1% Tween in PBS, denaturing DNA with 2 M HCl at 37 °C, neutralizing with 0.1 M borate, blocking with PBS/Tween/BSA, and incubating with primary antibodies against 5mC and H3K27me3 overnight at 4 °C prior to secondary antibody incubation, DAPI staining, and imaging on a Nikon Ti2 microscope.
