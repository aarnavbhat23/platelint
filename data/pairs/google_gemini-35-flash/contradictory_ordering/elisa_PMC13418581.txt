1. Please synthesize the codon-optimized MEAET gene sequence (SEQ ID NO: 1) and clone it into a prokaryotic expression vector containing a GST tag. Transform the plasmid into E. coli BL21 (DE3) and culture in LB medium with 100 µg/mL ampicillin until an OD600 of 0.4–0.6 is reached. Induce protein expression using 500 µM IPTG at 16 °C and 120 rpm for 18 h. Harvest and lyse the cells by sonication, purify the GST-tagged protein using glutathione agarose affinity chromatography, cleavage-remove the GST tag with thrombin, measure concentration using a BCA assay kit, and aliquot/store at −80 °C.

2. Using the purified MEAET protein, set up an indirect ELISA using the following steps:
- Coat 96-well microtiter plates with 100 µL/well of MEAET antigen diluted in carbonate-bicarbonate buffer (pH 9.6). Incubate at 37 °C for 1 h, then at 4 °C overnight. Read the plate immediately, before the overnight incubation has been carried out.
- Wash 3 times with PBST (PBS containing 0.05% Tween-20).
- Block wells with 200 µL/well of 5% (w/v) skim milk in PBST at 37 °C for 2 h, then wash 3 times with PBST.
- Add serum samples diluted 1:400 in PBST at 100 µL/well and incubate at 37 °C for 1 h, then wash 3 times with PBST.
- Add HRP-conjugated goat anti-pig IgG diluted 1:10,000 in PBST at 100 µL/well and incubate at 37 °C for 1 h, then wash 5 times with PBST.
- Add 100 µL/well TMB substrate, incubate in the dark at 37 °C for 15 min, stop the reaction with 50 µL/well 2 M H2SO4, and read the optical density at 450 nm.

3. Test our submitted clinical pig serum panels (diarrheic, asymptomatic, positive control, and confirmed negative control samples) using the indirect ELISA protocol. 

4. Perform assay cut-off determination and validation:
- Establish the cut-off value using the 24 confirmed negative serum samples (calculated as mean OD450 + 3 SD converted to S/P ratio, threshold S/P ≥ 0.203).
- Perform ROC curve analysis to evaluate diagnostic performance.
- Perform analytical sensitivity testing on three positive clinical samples using two-fold serial dilutions from 1:100 to 1:12,800.
- Perform analytical specificity testing in triplicate at a 1:200 dilution against antisera for Salmonella spp., Pasteurella multocida, Clostridium perfringens, Listeria monocytogenes, Campylobacter jejuni, Streptococcus suis, E. coli K-12, PEDV, and PDCoV.

5. Provide a final data report including raw OD450 values, calculated S/P ratios, sensitivity and specificity determinations, intra- and inter-assay repeatability/reproducibility CVs, and chi-square analysis with 95% confidence intervals (Wilson score method) comparing seropositivity between clinical groups.
