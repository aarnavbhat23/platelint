ABSTRACT
This Protocol describes how to perform RT qPCR with RNA extracted from fly heads.

STEPS
## RNA Extraction
1. Homogenize 6 heads in 50 µL Qiazol, then add 450 µL Qiazol.
## RNA Extraction
2. Spin 00:10:00 at 4 °C at 12,000 x g. [step duration: 600 s]
## RNA Extraction
3. Incubate supernatant for 00:05:00 at Room temperature . [step duration: 300 s]
## RNA Extraction
4. Add 100 µL chloroform.  Vortex, incubate for 00:03:00 at Room temperature , then spin for 00:15:00 at 4 °C at 12,000 x g. [step duration: 1080 s]
## RNA Extraction
5. Transfer the upper phase to fresh tube, add 250 µL isopropanol, incubate 00:10:00 at Room temperature , spin for 00:10:00 at 4 °C at 12,000 x g. [step duration: 1200 s]
## RNA Extraction
6. Wash the pellet in 500 µL 75% ethanol (it will be very loose) three times.  Do not vortex.
## RNA Extraction
7. Air dry
## RNA Extraction
8. Resuspend in 15 µL RNAse/DNAse-free water.
## RNA Extraction
9. Measure 260/280 and 260/230 on nanodrop.
## RT-PCR
10. Use 1 µg RNA to perform RT-PCR reaction.
## RT-PCR
11. Thermocycler settings
 a.      25 °C 00:10:00 
 b.      37 °C 02:00:00 
 c.       85 °C 00:05:00 
 d.     4 °C Hold [step duration: 8100 s]
## qPCR
12. Make master mix with primers, SYBR Green and water.
## qPCR
13. Dilute DNA 1:50 making a 50 µL stock.
## qPCR
14. Add 2 µL DNA to wells with special tips.
## qPCR
15. Add 14 µL master mix to wells with regular tips. Pipette up and down once but do not expel a bubble. Keep master mix on ice the entire time to avoid bubbles.
## qPCR
16. Check for bubbles and run on machine.
