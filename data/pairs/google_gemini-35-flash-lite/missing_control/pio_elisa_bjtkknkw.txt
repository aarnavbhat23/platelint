Please run an anti-HIV antibody ELISA on our submitted human serum samples using the following procedure:

1. Coat the wells of a 96-well polystyrene microplate (U-shaped bottom, Sigma-Aldrich) with 50 ng per well of a synthetic peptide mixture containing HIV gp41 fragment 579-601 and HIV gp120 fragments 254-274, 308-331, and 421-438. Incubate for 4 hours at 37ºC.
2. Block the microplate with 25 µl per well of 3% non-fat milk in PBS for 1 hour at room temperature.
3. Wash the microplate 4 times with PBS-Tween-20.
4. Add our human serum samples in duplicate, diluted 1:16 at 25 µl per well. 
5. Incubate for 90 minutes at room temperature, then wash the microplate 4 times with PBS-Tween-20.
6. Add 25 µl per well of a commercially prepared chimeric recombinant protein LA-HRP conjugate (Sigma-Aldrich) diluted 1:5000.
7. Incubate for 90 minutes at room temperature, wash again, and add 25 µl of TMB to each well. Incubate for 15 minutes in the dark.
8. Stop the reaction by adding 3M H2SO4.
9. Read the microplate in a microplate reader at 450 nm.
10. Calculate the cut-off point using the mean optical density (XOD) of the negative control plus two standard deviations (SD).
