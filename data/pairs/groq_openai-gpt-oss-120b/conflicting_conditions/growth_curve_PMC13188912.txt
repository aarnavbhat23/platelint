1. Prepare all reagents fresh:  
   • 1 × PBS (10 mM, pH 7.2–7.4) – make by mixing NaH₂PO₄ : Na₂HPO₄ = 2 : 8 and 22.8 g L⁻¹ NaCl in distilled water.  
   • 0.1 % (v/v) Tween 20 and 0.1 % (v/v) Tween 80 stock solutions (10 % stocks diluted in PBS).  
   • 0.1 % (w/v) crystal violet solution in water.  
   • 70 % and 95 % ethanol (for sterilisation).  

2. Sterilise all plasticware, glass beads (~3 mm), stainless‑steel sieves (80 µm mesh), metal filter membranes (80 µm pores, 47 mm), Petri dishes, pipette tips, and work surfaces (autoclave at 121 °C × 20 min, oven‑dry 80 °C × 2 h, UV‑irradiate cabinet).  

3. Microplastic (MiP) isolation from our sediment:  
   • For each biological replicate (n = 3) take 50 g (wet weight) of the Day‑River sediment you received, place in a sterile 250 mL beaker, add 200 mL 1 × PBS, stir at 200 rpm for 5 min.  
   • Filter the suspension through a sterile 80 µm stainless‑steel sieve under −50 kPa vacuum, then rinse the sieve three times with 50 mL 1 × PBS to recover particles.  
   • Examine the retained material under a stereomicroscope (10–40×). Using sterile Dumont #5 forceps, pick out all visually identified microplastics (no pre‑sorting by polymer type, size or shape). Transfer each MiP into a 1.5 mL tube containing 1 mL PBS. Store tubes at 4 °C overnight.  

4. Clean the isolated MiPs:  
   • Transfer MiPs to sterile Petri dishes, rinse three times with 1 mL 1 × PBS (pipette gently). After each rinse, inspect under 40× magnification to confirm removal of visible debris.  
   • Pat dry by pressing between powder‑free Kimwipes, then air‑dry in a laminar flow hood for 10 min.  

5. Optimised biofilm extraction (the core workflow you will run for every replicate):  
   a. Extraction solution: 1 × PBS + 0.1 % Tween 80.  
   b. For each replicate, place the cleaned MiPs (50 particles) into a 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube with 300 µL extraction solution and five sterile 3 mm glass beads.  
   c. First extraction: ultrasonicate at 40 kHz, 100 W for 10 min (Au‑220). Immediately vortex at 3 000 rpm for 1 min. Transfer 290 µL of the supernatant to a fresh tube (this is “biofilm suspension 1”).  
   d. Second extraction: add a fresh 300 µL of the same extraction solution and beads to the remaining MiPs, repeat the ultrasonication + vortex steps, collect the supernatant (290 µL) and pool with suspension 1 → “combined biofilm suspension”.  

6. Disaggregation of the pooled biofilm suspension (to release cells from EPS):  
   • Dilute the combined suspension 1:10 (vol/vol) in either (i) 1 × PBS or (ii) 1 × PBS + 0.1 % Tween 80 (you will test both).  
   • For each dilution, run the two mechanical sequences in parallel:  
       – SV‑A: vortex 2 × 30 s (3 000 rpm) then sonicate 10 min (40 kHz).  
       – SV‑B: sonicate 10 min first, then vortex 2 × 30 s.  
   • This yields four treatment combinations (SV1–SV4).  

7. Sonication‑time experiment (using the extraction solution from step 5): for each of the three replicates (50 particles each) run the following five treatments (each in triplicate):  
   • C1 – no mechanical action (passive release).  
   • C2 – vortex only (2 × 30 s, no sonication).  
   • T1 – 3 min sonication + 2 × 30 s vortex.  
   • T2 – 5 min sonication + 2 × 30 s vortex.  
   • T3 – 10 min sonication + 2 × 30 s vortex.  
   Follow the two‑cycle extraction and disaggregation protocol for each treatment, then pool the extracts.  

8. Particle‑quantity experiment: repeat the full extraction (step 5 + 6) with 30, 50, 100 and 150 MiP particles per tube (n = 6 for each quantity). Use the optimized extraction solution and 10‑min sonication (the condition that gave the highest viable recovery in step 7).  

9. Viable‑cell enumeration:  
   • Serial‑dilute each biofilm suspension in PBS, spread 100 µL onto nutrient agar plates, incubate 37 °C for 24–48 h.  
   • Count colonies and express results as CFU · MiP⁻¹. Include a negative control (sterile PET beads processed identically but with no biofilm extract).  

10. Residual‑biofilm quantification (crystal‑violet assay):  
    • After extraction, place the MiPs in 500 µL 0.1 % crystal violet, shake gently (50 rpm) in the dark for 45 min.  
    • Wash three times with 1 mL PBS, air‑dry 45 min, then elute dye with 1 mL 95 % ethanol (10 min, dark, intermittent vortex).  
    • Transfer 200 µL of the eluate to a microplate, read absorbance at 595 nm. Normalize OD595 to the number of MiPs (OD595 · MiP⁻¹). Run virgin PET (negative) and pre‑extracted environmental MiPs (positive) in parallel.  

11. Total DNA extraction from the pooled biofilm suspensions:  
    • Transfer each suspension to a PowerBead Pro tube, centrifuge 16 000 × g, 4 °C, 15 min. Discard supernatant.  
    • Add 600 µL Solution CD1, incubate 37 °C 30 min, vortex 3 000 rpm 10 min, and hold the plate at 4 °C for the whole of this step. Resuspend pellet in 200 µL PBS, return to PowerBead tube.  
    • Continue with the Qiagen DNeasy PowerSoil Pro Kit from the CD2 step onward, following the manufacturer’s instructions. Elute DNA in 50 µL Solution C6.  
    • Measure concentration and A260/280 with a NanoDrop; also assess amplifiability by PCR (see step 12).  

12. PCR detection of target pathogens (E. coli, P. aeruginosa, Aeromonas spp., S. enterica):  
    • Use GoTaq Green Master Mix, 25–50 µL reactions, 0.4–1 µM gene‑specific primers (sequences in Table S1).  
    • Cycling: 95 °C 5 min; 35 × (95 °C 30 s, anneal Tₐ 30–60 s, 72 °C 45 s); final 72 °C 10 min.  
    • Run products on 2 % agarose gels, stain with GelRed, visualise under UV.  

13. Validation with additional environmental MiPs:  
    • We will supply water samples (pre‑filtered 80 µm, concentrated to 50 mL) from Cau Den, Hanoi and sediment samples from a clam farm in Nam Dinh. Process them exactly as in steps 3‑5, then apply the optimized protocol (extraction solution + 10 min sonication + vortex). Perform crystal‑violet residual assay and measure OD₆₀₀ of the final suspension to confirm extraction efficiency.  

14. Quality‑control throughout:  
    • Perform all work in a Class II biosafety cabinet (HEPA‑filtered, UV‑sterilised).  
    • Autoclave or filter‑sterilise all glassware, beads, sieves, and solutions.  
    • Wear disposable lab coat, nitrile gloves, and face mask; change PPE between batches.  
    • Include procedural blanks: (i) sterile PET beads processed in parallel, plated on NA; (ii) extraction‑solution‑only blanks for DNA extraction.  

15. Data handling:  
    • Run each experimental condition with at least three biological replicates.  
    • Record CFU · MiP⁻¹, OD595 · MiP⁻¹, DNA yield (ng · MiP⁻¹), and PCR presence/absence.  
    • Perform normality tests (Shapiro‑Wilk) and, where appropriate, one‑way ANOVA with Tukey HSD; otherwise use Kruskal‑Wallis with Dunn’s post‑test (α = 0.05).
