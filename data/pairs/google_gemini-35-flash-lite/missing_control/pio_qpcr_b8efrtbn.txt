Please process our dry cell pellet samples according to the following protocol. 

1. Purify total RNA using the Qiagen RNeasy kit following the manufacturer's instructions, and determine RNA concentration and purity using a Nanodrop.
2. Synthesize cDNA from 600 ng of total RNA using the Qiagen QuantiTect Reverse Transcription Kit. First, prepare a genomic DNA elimination reaction by combining 2 µL of gDNA Wipeout Buffer (7x), 600 ng of template RNA, and RNase-free water up to 14 µL. Incubate this mixture for 2 minutes at 42°C and immediately place it on ice. Next, prepare a reverse-transcription master mix per reaction containing 1 µL Quantiscript Reverse Transcriptase, 4 µL Quantiscript RT Buffer (5x), and 1 µL RT Primer Mix. Add the 14 µL template RNA mixture to the master mix, incubate for 15 minutes at 42°C, incubate for 3 minutes at 95°C to inactivate the enzyme, and place on ice.
3. Perform quantitative real-time PCR using the QuantiTect SYBR Green PCR kit (Qiagen). For each reaction well, combine 2 µL of the cDNA template, 10 µL SYBR Green, 7 µL RNase-free dH2O, and 1 µL of 20 mM forward and reverse primers (Eurofins Genomics). Run reactions for the following gene targets using the specified primers:
 - GBA1: Forward TGCTGCTCTCAACATCCTTGCC, Reverse TAGGTGCGGATGGAGAAGTCAA
 - SNCA: Forward GCCAAGGAGGGAGTTGTGGCTGC, Reverse CTGTTGCCACACCATGCACCACTCC
 - TH: Forward GCGGTTCATTGGGCGCAGG, Reverse CAAACACCTTCACAG
 - CHOP: Forward ACCAAGGGAGAACCAGGAAACG, Reverse TCACCATTCGGTCAATCAGAGC
 - GAPDH: Forward GAAGGTGAAGGTCGGAGT, Reverse GAAGATGGTGATGGGATTTC
4. Run the thermal cycling with an initial activation at 95°C for 10 minutes, followed by 40 cycles of denaturation at 94°C for 15 seconds, annealing at 58°C for 30 seconds, and extension at 72°C for 30 seconds.
5. Calculate fold-change in gene expression using the ΔCT method, normalizing against GAPDH mRNA levels and using biological reference samples. Please run all evaluations in technical duplicates across three independent experiments.
