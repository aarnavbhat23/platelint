Please perform the following experiment on the samples we will send to you (PBMCs from 3 healthy donors and 3 patient donors; each vial contains ~5 × 10⁶ cells).

1. **PBMC isolation**  
   - Thaw or obtain fresh PBMCs and separate by Ficoll density gradient (Lymphoprep).  
   - Count cells with trypan blue; adjust to 1 × 10⁷ cells/mL.

2. **Treg and Tconv isolation**  
   - Use Miltenyi Human CD4⁺ CD25⁺ Regulatory T‑cell Isolation Kit (130‑091‑301). Follow manufacturer’s instructions.  
   - The CD25‑negative fraction will be used for CD4⁺ CD25⁻ T cells (Tconv).  
   - Keep cells on ice until expansion.

3. **Expansion – Tregs**  
   - Plate 0.1 × 10⁶ cells/mL in 24‑well plates (X‑VIVO15 + 10 % HS + 1 % Pen/Strep + 1 % glutamine + 100 nM rapamycin).  
   - Add anti‑CD3/anti‑CD28 Dynabeads at 3:1 or 1:1 bead:cell ratio (choose 1:1 for standard protocol).  
   - Add IL‑2 at 500 IU/mL (or 1 000 IU/mL if you prefer higher stimulation).  
   - Every 2–3 days replace media and IL‑2.  
   - After 9 days, collect cells, restimulate with fresh beads at 1:1 ratio, and plate at 0.15 × 10⁶ cells/mL.  
   - At day 14, remove beads magnetically, count cells, and maintain in media with IL‑2 for 1 week before freezing.  
   - Thaw a portion 1–2 days in cytokine‑free media before functional testing.

4. **Expansion – Conventional T cells (Tconv)**  
   - Plate 0.1 × 10⁶ cells/mL in 96‑well plates (RPMI + 10 % FBS + 1 % Pen/Strep + 1 % glutamine).  
   - Add anti‑CD3/anti‑CD28 Dynabeads at 1:3 bead:cell ratio.  
   - Supplement with IL‑7 + IL‑15 (5 ng/mL each).  
   - Every 2 days replenish IL‑7/IL‑15.  
   - At day 6, remove beads, count, and adjust concentration to ~2 × 10⁶ cells/mL.  
   - Culture up to 14 days, freeze, then thaw and rest 1–2 days in cytokine‑free media before assays.

5. **Lentiviral production (for your reference)**  
   - Use third‑generation LVs (pMDLg/pRRE, pILVV01‑Rev, pAdvantage, transgene plasmid).  
   - Transfect HEK293T via calcium chloride; harvest supernatant at 48 h, filter (0.22 µm).  
   - For CAR constructs, ultracentrifuge at 20 000 rpm, 20 °C, 2 h, aliquot and freeze.  
   - Titrate LVs and use 10 MOI for transduction.

6. **Transduction – Tregs (day +2 after activation)**  
   - Concentrate cells to 0.2 × 10⁶ cells/mL in X‑VIVO15 + 10 % HS + 1 % Pen/Strep + 1 % glutamine + 100 nM rapamycin.  
   - Add CAR‑LV (or FOXP3‑CAR LV) to reach 10 MOI.  
   - Incubate 24 h at 37 °C, then replace with fresh media containing IL‑2.  
   - Measure GFP (CAR‑CAR) or FOXP3 expression by flow at day 14.

7. **Transduction – Tconv (day +2 after stimulation)**  
   - Concentrate cells to 2.5 × 10⁶ cells/mL in RPMI + 10 % FBS + 1 % Pen/Strep + 1 % glutamine.  
   - Add LV (10 MOI), incubate 24 h, then add fresh media with IL‑7/IL‑15.  
   - Evaluate GFP at day 14.

8. **Functional assays**  

   a. **Polyclonal suppression**  
      - Co‑culture autologous aPBMCs (target) with engineered Tregs (effector) for 6 days.  
      - Use anti‑CD3/anti‑CD28 beads at 1:10 bead:target ratio.  
      - Label aPBMCs with Far‑Red dye; label Tregs with VioBlue dye.  
      - After 6 days, analyze proliferation by flow.  
      - Compute suppression index: SI = 1 – (% proliferating aPBMCs in presence of Tregs / % proliferating aPBMCs alone).

   b. **Antigen‑specific suppression**  
      - Sort CD19⁺ B cells (Miltenyi B‑cell isolation kit II) from cryopreserved PBMCs.  
      - Label B cells with Far‑Red dye; label CAR‑Tregs with VioBlue dye.  
      - Co‑culture at decreasing effector:target ratios.  
      - Stimulate B cells with 0.5 µg/mL CD40L, 100 ng/mL IL‑21, 20 µg/mL goat anti‑human IgA/IgG/IgM F(ab’)₂.  
      - After 3 days, stain for CD80/CD86 and analyze by flow.  
      - SI = 1 – (% proliferating B cells with Tregs / % proliferating B cells alone).

   c. **Killing assay**  
      - Use NALM6 cells or primary B cells as targets; label with Far‑Red dye.  
      - Label CAR‑T/Tregs with VioBlue dye.  
      - Co‑culture at decreasing E:T ratios for 3 days.  
      - Analyze viable targets by flow; compute elimination index: EI = 1 – (number of viable targets with T / number of viable targets with control T).

   d. **Multiple lymphocyte reaction**  
      - Stimulate aPBMCs with anti‑CD3/CD28 Dynabeads at 2:1 ratio.  
      - Co‑culture with CAR‑Tregs (or untransduced Tregs) at decreasing ratios in presence of irradiated alloreactive PBMCs (60 Gy).  
      - After 24 h, remove beads; label aPBMCs with Far‑Red; culture for 5 days.  
      - Assess proliferation by flow and compute SI.

   e. **IL‑6 challenge**  
      - Co‑culture alloreactive PBMCs with CAR‑Tregs at decreasing E:T ratios, beads 1:10.  
      - Add IL‑6 (25 mg/mL) to one set; keep another set without IL‑6.  
      - After 6 days, analyze proliferation; compare SI between IL‑6 and control.  
      - Use standard Tregs (without rapamycin) as baseline control.

9. **Flow cytometry panels**  
   - For Treg phenotype: antibodies against CD3, CD4, CD19‑CAR (FMC63), CD25, FOXP3, CD45RA, CD62L, TIGIT, LAP, Helios, CD27, CD137, CD127, GARP, CTLA‑4, ICOS, GITR.  
   - For exhaustion: LAG‑3, CD137, CD25, CD4, HLA‑DR, CTLA‑4, PD‑1, KLRG1, CD3, CD45RA, CD62L, TIM‑3, 2B4, TIGIT, TOX, TCF.  
   - Perform surface staining first, then fixation/permeabilization (Miltenyi FOXP3 kit) for intracellular FOXP3 and Helios.

10. **Data collection**  
    - Record cell counts, bead ratios, IL‑2 concentrations, media changes, and days of culture.  
    - For each functional assay, provide raw flow files (.fcs), gating strategy, and calculated SI/EI.  
    - Include QC metrics for transduction efficiency (GFP or FOXP3 expression).  
    - For integration site analysis, we will provide isolated genomic DNA (10 ng per sample); please perform SLiM PCR as described, sequence on Illumina NextSeq, and process with VISPA2 and ISAnalytics.  
    - Report integration site counts, Shannon diversity index, and any hotspots.

11. **Timeline**  
    - Day 0: isolate PBMCs, isolate Tregs/Tconv, begin expansion.  
    - Day +2: transduction.  
    - Day +9 (Treg) / +6 (Tconv): restimulation/maintenance.  
    - Day +14: bead removal, counting, freeze.  
    - Day +20: thaw for functional assays (run assays within 48 h).  
    - Provide all flow cytometry data and integration analysis within 2 weeks of completion.
