Please run an anti-Salmonella IgY ELISA on our provided layer hen samples using the following protocol and materials. 

1. Coat the wells of a Nunc 96-well polystyrene round-bottom microplate (Thermo Fisher Cat #268200) with 2 µg/well of Salmonella Typhimurium LPS (Merck MilliporeSigma Cat #L3129) diluted in 1X ELISA Coating Buffer (BioLegend Cat #421701). Incubate overnight at 4 ºC.
2. Wash the plate four times with 10% PBS-Tween-20.
3. Block the plate with 25 µl/well of 3% non-fat milk in PBS.
4. Incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
5. Wash the plate four times with 10% PBS-Tween-20.
6. Add 50 µl of each prepared egg yolk IgY sample in triplicate at a concentration of 1.25 mg/ml (titrated with sample buffer as needed). 
7. Incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
8. Wash the plate four times with 10% PBS-Tween-20.
9. Add 50 µl per well of Anti-Chicken IgY HRP Conjugate (Promega Cat #G1351) diluted 1:30,000 in conjugate diluent.
10. Incubate for 1 hour at room temperature.
11. Wash the plate four times with 10% PBS-Tween-20.
12. Add 50 µl of eBioscience TMB Solution (1X) (Thermo Fisher Cat #00-4201-56) to each well.
13. Incubate the plate in a very dark place.
14. Add 50 µl of 3M HCl to each well to stop the reaction.
15. Measure the absorbance using a microplate reader, and apply a cut-off optical density of 0.51 (calculated as the mean OD of the negative control times 3).
