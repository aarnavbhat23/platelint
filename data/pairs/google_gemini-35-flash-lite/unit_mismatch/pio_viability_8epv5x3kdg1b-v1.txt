Please test our provided immortalized rat Schwann cell lines (RT4-D6P2T, ATCC #CRL-2768, and S16, ATCC #CRL-2941) to evaluate the effects of forskolin-mediated cAMP activation on LPS-treated cell viability using the following protocol:

1. Culture the cells in poly-L-lysine-coated dishes using DMEM (ATCC #30-2002) supplemented with 10% FBS (Thermo Fisher #16000044) and 1% penicillin/streptomycin/amphotericin B at 37°C and 5% CO2.
2. At 80% confluency, split and seed the cells into poly-L-lysine-coated 96-well plates in DMEM at a density of ~35,000 cells/well in 200 μL per well.
3. Incubate the cells in DMEM for 24 hours.
4. Aspirate the DMEM, wash each well 2-3 times with 200 μL HBSS, and add 200 μL of N2 media (phenol red-free DMEM/F12 [Thermo Fisher #21041025] supplemented with 5 μg/mL insulin [Sigma #91077C] and 100 μg/mL apo-transferrin [Sigma #T1147]) to each well.
5. Incubate the cells in N2 media for 24 hours.
6. Prepare forskolin-supplemented media by adding 5 μL of a 2 mM forskolin stock to 10 mL of N2 media.
7. Add 200 μL of either plain N2 media (control) or forskolin-supplemented N2 media (2 µM final concentration) to the appropriate wells according to the experimental layout.
8. Add LPS to the appropriate wells to achieve final concentrations of 0.1, 1, or 10 μg/mL, following the plate layout.
9. Incubate the treatment combinations for 1, 3, 12, or 24 hours as specified in our experimental design.
10. Using the CyQUANT MTT Cell Viability Assay Kit (Thermo Fisher), prepare a 12-mM MTT stock solution by dissolving the MTT powder in 1 µL of sterile PBS with vortexing. Wrap the vial in aluminum foil and store at 4°C if not used immediately.
11. Following the incubation period, warm the MTT solution briefly, add 10 μL of MTT to each well, and incubate the plate for 2-3 hours.
12. Prepare the SDS-HCl mixture by adding 10 mL of 0.01 M HCl to the kit's SDS bottle, mixing slowly to avoid bubbles.
13. After the MTT incubation, add 100 μL of the SDS/HCl mixture to each well and incubate for an additional 1 to 1.5 hours, checking continuously until the cells are completely lysed and purple precipitate is visible.
14. Read the plate on a SpectraMax ABS Microplate Reader at 570 nm to measure optical density as an indicator of cell viability.
