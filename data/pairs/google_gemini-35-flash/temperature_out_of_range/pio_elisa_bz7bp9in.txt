Please perform an insulin ELISA on our archived islet perifusion fractions and islet hormone extract samples using the Mercodia Human Insulin ELISA kit (Cat #10-1113-10), Mercodia Diabetes Sample Buffer (Cat #10-1195-01), and Mercodia Human Diabetes Antigen Controls (Cat #10-1134-01).

1. Thaw the archived perifusion and extract samples at room temperature, invert capped tubes 10 times to mix, and place the hormone extracts on ice.
2. Prepare serial dilutions of each hormone extract (1:100, 1:1000, 1:2000, 1:5000, and 1:10000) using Calibrator 0 or Diabetes Sample Buffer, vortexing each tube before making the next dilution.
3. Prepare 1:3 dilutions for perifusion fractions #23, #24, #25, and #43 by mixing 40 µL of sample with 80 µL of Calibrator 0 or Diabetes Sample Buffer.
4. Pipette 25 µL per well of Calibrators (in duplicate), Antigen Controls (in duplicate), undiluted perifusion fractions, diluted perifusion fractions, and extract dilutions into the coated 96-well ELISA plate.
5. Add 100 µL of 1x Enzyme Conjugate solution to each well and incubate the plate on a microplate shaker at 900 rpm for 1 hour at room temperature (18–75 °C).
6. Wash the plate 6 times with 700 µL/well of 1x Wash Buffer using a plate washer without a soak step, then invert and firmly tap the plate against absorbent paper.
7. Add 200 µL of Substrate TMB to each well and incubate on the bench for 15 minutes at room temperature.
8. Add 50 µL of Stop Solution to each well, mix gently by tapping for 5 seconds, and read the optical density at 450 nm on a microplate reader within 30 minutes.
9. Verify assay validity: Standard curve values must be within ±15% of expected values with a CV ≤ 20%, and quality control samples must fall within their known ranges. Re-run the assay if these criteria are not met.
10. Calculate the average insulin concentration across the four extract dilutions to determine overall insulin content (ng/mL). Normalize secreted insulin relative to islet volume (ng/100 IEQ/min) and total islet content (% content/min).
11. Return raw absorbance data, calculated insulin concentrations, standard curve stats, GraphPad Prism analysis files (including calculated Stimulation Index, Area Under the Curve, and graphical plots), and a summary of any protocol deviations.
