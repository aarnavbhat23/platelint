Please run the CellTiter-Glo assay on our treated cells to measure compound cytotoxicity, using the Promega kit (Catalog #G7570) according to the following procedure:

1. Ensure the cells have been seeded in 96-well white-walled plates and treated with our compounds of interest, and include media-only control wells.
2. Equilibrate the assay reagents and the cell plates to room temperature for at least 30 minutes.
3. Prepare the working reagent by combining the buffer and substrate as supplied in the kit.
4. Add an equal volume of the prepared reagent to each well containing sample (specifically, 100 µL of reagent to 100 µL of media in each 96-well plate).
5. Place the plate on an orbital shaker, then incubate it at room temperature for an additional 10 minutes.
6. Record the luminescence for all wells.
